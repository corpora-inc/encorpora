- Troubleshooting procedures are logic trees drawn across a two-page spread: the left page holds the
  question boxes and their YES/NO arrows, the right page the numbered test procedure for each
  question ("TEST PROCEDURE", "STEP 3 TEST"), with its own WARNING, CAUTION and NOTE blocks.
- Beside each question box: TEST OPTIONS, REASON FOR QUESTION, KNOWN INFO and POSSIBLE PROBLEMS
  boxes (facets test_option, reason, known, possible).
- A corrective action box ("Replace WTEC II VIM (para 8-6).") is an action; its target is the
  paragraph or task it names, as printed ("para 8-6").
- INITIAL SETUP opens each procedure: equipment conditions, tools and special tools (each with an
  item number into an appendix: put that number in ident), materials and parts, references,
  personnel required.
- Tables of displayed diagnostic codes have a "perform troubleshooting task" column: that gives
  the tree ref and, when printed, the step or question number.
- Change bars in the margin and "Change N" in the footer mark changed content; they are not text
  of any box.
