# Spaces

Everyone in AMP is in one **space** at a time. The space decides the frame, the navigation, the words and what a screen composes. It never decides a permission: the server authorizes every request exactly as it would without spaces, and nothing a space shows may offer an action the server would refuse.

| Space       | Who                                                                                                                   | Where                                                                        | Home                                             |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| `family`    | a family's account holder, its second parent, a co-teacher of a family's class; their children on the parent's device | `/school/<family org>/classes/<class>/…` today; `/family/<family>/…` from P2 | the family's Today                               |
| `classroom` | a teacher, aide, co-teacher, or a viewer or admin inspecting a class                                                  | `/school/<org>/classes/<class>/…`                                            | the class's Today                                |
| `school`    | a school, network or district admin or viewer                                                                         | `/school/<org>/…`                                                            | Overview, or the one class of a one-class school |
| `me`        | any adult, about themselves, and practice as a profile                                                                | `/me/…` and the shared learning screens for their own learner                | Levels (`/me`)                                   |
| `student`   | a child in a student session                                                                                          | `/practice`, `/join`, `/c`, `/standings`                                     | practice                                         |
| `account`   | any adult: their account, setup, the starter and payment steps                                                        | `/account/…`, `/start/…`, `/school/start` …                                  | Account (`/account/security`)                    |
| `public`    | anyone: public pages, and links that carry their own authority                                                        | `/schools`, `/sign-in`, `/p`, `/school/invite`, `/family/approve` …          | —                                                |

Tutoring is not a space: an existing tutoring class is a family-kind org and resolves to `family` with the `co-teacher` or holder relationship. Practicing at a level is `me`.

## Resolution

`resolveSpace(match, session, resources)` (`src/web/features/spaces/resolve.ts`) is the one description of where the person is. It is pure: the same inputs always describe the same frame. `useSpace` (`useSpace.ts`) feeds it from the settled route and the queries the route already loaded; `AppShell`, `Tabs`, the drawer and the place switcher render from what it returns, and nothing else reads "the current place".

- **match**: the settled route's address, its search, the learner it names (`$learnerId`) and its static `space` (`staticData.space` on every typed route, `spaceOfPath` for the legacy catch-all; a test keeps the two in agreement). Classroom and school routes resolve to `family` when the org they name is a family's.
- **session**: `none`, `student` or `adult`. A student session always wins (`auth.ts`): a parent's cookie on the same device never brings the parent's chrome. A verified staff sign-in that replaces a student session arrives as an adult session; the resolver never decides that.
- **resources**: the org, its classes, the class's year and its practice coverage, as the server returned them for this account. `undefined` is not yet answered; `null` is refused, missing or failed.

The result is a discriminated union, one shape per space:

| Space       | Carries                                                                                                                                                         |
| ----------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `public`    | `entry`: `page`, `invite`, `approval`, `buyer` or `student-entry`                                                                                               |
| `account`   | `scope`: `settings`, `setup`, `starter` or `steps`                                                                                                              |
| `student`   | the student's own learner                                                                                                                                       |
| `me`        | `mode` (`own`, `profile`, `none`) and the adult's own learner or profile                                                                                        |
| `family`    | `status`, the family id (its org), the `relation` (`holder`, `second-parent`, `co-teacher`), its class, its own children, the chosen child, target capabilities |
| `classroom` | `status`, the org, the class (`past`, `board`, `start`), how this person reaches it (`teacher`, `aide`, `viewer`, `admin`, `staff`), target capabilities        |
| `school`    | `status`, the org, target capabilities                                                                                                                          |

Every shape also carries the bar (`tabs`, `practice`, `plain` or none), the tabs and the open one, the bar's actions, the drawer's destinations, the bar's place name and the space's words.

### The truth table

| No session                                                                                                                                                                                                                | `public`, no frame                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |

| Class route in a family this account is the second parent of                                                                                                                                                              | `family`, `second-parent`: that family's children; their practice is the family's Today handover, never hers                                                                                                                                             |
| Class route in another family-kind org (co-teacher, tutoring)                                                                                                                                                             | `family`, `co-teacher`: the family's tabs, no children as theirs                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| School class, teacher / aide / viewer / admin                                                                                                                                                                             | `classroom` with `role`; the tabs are the class's, the actions only what the server offers (Start class needs a current year and running practice)                                                                                                       |
| School, network or district                                                                                                                                                                                               | `school`: exactly the tabs its `can` list allows; a one-class school is its class                                                                                                                                                                        |
| Shared learning route (`/practice`, `/overview`, `/pathways`, `/inventory`, lessons, `/learners/$id/workbooks`, `/continue/$id`)                                                                                          | The learner the address names (`$learnerId`, `?learner=`, `?profile=`); else the device's choice. The adult's own or a profile is `me`; a child is that child's family. A learner who isn't this account's is nobody's: `me` with no learner and no tabs |
| Account, starter, setup, payment steps                                                                                                                                                                                    | `account` with its scope                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| A resource not yet answered                                                                                                                                                                                               | `status: "loading"`: no tabs yet, the bar holds its place                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| A resource refused or missing, a role ended, a class that isn't the named family's                                                                                                                                        | `status: "unavailable"`: no tabs, no destinations. Never another family, child, class or session                                                                                                                                                         |

"Once per settled route" means a render snapshot, never a cached grant. Every query is keyed by the account and the resource; a `/me` for another account or an org answer for another id is ignored; the descriptor is recomputed whenever the session, `/me`, the org, its classes or the year change, so a sign-out, an ended role or a refetch redraws the frame on the next render.

### Capabilities

Capabilities are the server's, named by target (`{ target: { kind: "org", id }, can }`). The org's `can` list covers the org; a class's write rights come from that class's own reads. The descriptor never derives a permission from a space or an org kind.

## Shell

- **Bar**: the logo, the place's name ("Grade 4 Math · Alder Grove", "Pell family", "My practice", or the child whose record a learning screen shows), the space's tabs, and its actions. Below 1024 px the tabs take a row of their own.
- **Tabs** (`tabs.ts`), measured to fit at every width; what does not fit goes under More, the open tab always shown.

| Space            | Phone                               | More                                                                                                       |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Family           | Today, Map, Workbooks, Time         | Report cards, (Grades for a group of more than six), People, Settings                                      |
| Classroom        | Today, Map, Grades, Students        | Time, Workbooks, Settings                                                                                  |
| School           | Overview, Classes, Staff, License   | Calendar, Privacy, Activity, Settings, Integrations (each by capability); district and network add Schools |
| Me               | Practice, Levels, Workbooks         | Pathways, Inventory, Profiles                                                                              |
| A child's record | Overview, Pathways, Inventory       | —                                                                                                          |
| Account          | Sign-in, Learners, Billing, Privacy | —                                                                                                          |

- **Drawer**: the space's own destinations (and in a family, which of its children), then the place switcher, then the account. It never shows another space's learners or destinations.
- **Place switcher**: one bounded request, `GET /api/v1/school/places` (`src/schools/places.ts`): the orgs this account holds a staff role at, the schools below a district it oversees, and the classes it teaches or aides this year, each list stopping at 40 with `more` set. Grouped as **Family** (their own, second-parent families, co-teaching), **Teaching** (each school with its role, its classes, viewer and aide included) and **My practice** (their own, Practice at a level). Six teaching places show before "More places"; past the server's limit, "All your schools" opens `/school`. Choosing a place lands on its home, chooses a learner who belongs there (none for a class or school), and remembers it for `/` only.

## Vocabulary

`spaceWords(space)` (`words.ts`) is a typed table of every noun that differs by space: the learner and learners, whose names a sign-in list shows, the group, the people screen, a map key's own-grade line, how sign-in is introduced, the switcher's heading and the tab row's name. Shared widgets take these as props; a family never reads "student" and a class never reads "your children".

`scripts/check-space-boundaries.ts` (part of `npm run lint:navigation`) fails when a shared module (`src/web/ui/**`, ClassMap, ProgressRecord, the grade parts, Feed, WeekFacts, SignInCode, JoinClass, CodeTiles, the pathways Graph, WorkbookList, AllowancePause, PracticeWorkspace) branches on an org kind or a space, takes a `family` flag, reads the account's places, or imports the resolver, the shell's navigation, onboarding's place logic or the second-parent API. Server-produced copy (Today's tasks, report cards and PDFs, activity) is part of each space screen's composition contract, reviewed with the screen.

## Account

The account's own settings have neutral addresses, and every older one opens the same screen:

| Address                                                                              | Older address (same screen)                                            |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `/account/security`                                                                  | `/family/security`                                                     |
| `/account/security/delete`                                                           | `/family/security/delete`                                              |
| `/account/billing`, `/account/billing/purchases`, `/account/billing/records`         | `/family/access`, `/family/access/purchases`, `/family/access/records` |
| `/account/privacy`, `/account/privacy/{export,consent,delete,retention,measurement}` | `/family/privacy/…`                                                    |

Learners (`/family/learners`, `/family/learners/new`) stay where they are until P2's family settings. A sign-in that returns to security (`?return=security`) continues at `/account/security` or, when it asked for it, `/account/security/delete`.

## Continuations

Every entry keeps working and lands in the right space. A secret travels only in a fragment, is taken out of the address before routing, and never goes into `next`, analytics or a forwarded hash. Returns today: `returnTarget` (`lib/return-to.ts`) accepts only a same-origin app path, normalized, at most 2,048 characters; an `open` stack is validated separately (`nav/entities.ts`, five layers); `safeSchoolPath` holds a school sign-in's `next` to 300 characters and the root's `next` is capped at 300. P2 makes these one route-aware contract with one length limit, long enough for a valid `open` stack, before any family redirect ships.

### Family-org legacy addresses (P2 implements; nothing redirects in P1)

P1 resolves these in place: a family-kind org's routes are the `family` space at their current URLs. P2 moves them to `/family/$family/…`; each old address then resolves to its row below, checking that the class belongs to the named family and carrying the query, hash and `open` stack. A mismatch shows the family's unavailable state; it never picks the first accessible class.

### Learning screens

`/overview`, `/progress`, `/practice`, `/pathways`, `/inventory`, `/lessons/…`, `/books/…` and `/learners/$learner/workbooks/…` name their learner: `?learner=` (carried between learning screens), `$learnerId`, or `?profile=` for practice as a profile (carried with its `from`). A learner in the address is chosen only when this account reads them; a refresh, Back or a second tab keeps that person. Moving from a family to My practice changes the address, never just the device's choice.
