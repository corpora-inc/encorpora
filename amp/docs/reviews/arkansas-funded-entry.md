# Arkansas funded purchase interface

The Arkansas purchase route presents the configured program and one learner, with the annual total and quote action visible at the bottom of the screen. Parent confirmation, verified consent and learner setup precede this view. Quote preparation does not establish payment or learning access.

These browser-test images use a synthetic parent, learner and offer. They demonstrate responsive layout, not live Arkansas offer availability. The test journey covers account creation, email verification, consent completion in another tab, learner creation or free-practice adoption, and quote retries. Campaign privacy controls and financial authorization are tested separately.
