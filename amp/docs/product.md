# AMP product specification

AMP's target is a complete adaptive mathematics program from early counting through precalculus and SAT Math preparation. Parents purchase one annual student license covering instruction, practice, progress reporting, and downloadable learning materials. One parent account can manage several independently licensed learners.

The application is a conventional online service with shared identity and progress across browsers and mobile devices. PDFs provide offline study. Interactive offline packs, translations, narration, video, and AP mathematics are subsequent capabilities. Mathematical structures and instructional prose are separate so additional media and languages can be introduced without rewriting the mathematics.

## Curriculum completion criteria

Every advertised requirement needs an instructional sequence and explicit assessment facets. Those facets need suitable problem families, mathematical verification, accessible rendering, explanations, and held-out transfer tasks. A single correct answer cannot certify all standards attached to a problem. Crosswalks distinguish official identifiers from editorial alignment and prerequisite choices.

The complete numbered Common Core inventory provides a national foundation. State-specific standards and standardized-test requirements require their own verified crosswalks. SAT Math preparation does not imply preparation for SAT reading/writing or a score guarantee. Coverage reports must identify component-level gaps, unsupported response forms, unreviewed instruction, and missing publications.

## Learning behavior

Placement samples multiple strands without equating a child's age with ability. Practice follows the learner's evidence, prerequisites, review needs, and challenge readiness. Correct independent work, assisted practice, repeated mistakes, and transfer performance carry different evidence. Response speed informs fluency only when the task explicitly assesses fluency. Families can inspect progress and revisit instruction.

A problem's semantic structure determines the rendered figure and the grading reference. Immutable template versions, seeds, and displayed instances preserve reproducibility. New content cannot reinterpret historical attempts. Problem generation and grading do not require live AI inference.

## Production and release

The production queue assigns bounded authoring tasks and separate review work. Shared contract changes are coordinated before dependent workers adopt them. New renderers must include semantic validation, accessibility, mathematical tests, and device/print evidence. Agents may generate candidate material; source presence does not establish quality or publication.

Release useful improvements continuously. Publish complete-curriculum claims only after the coverage gates support them. Complete working vertical slices include instruction, practice, feedback, remediation, parent reporting, and printable material. The development preview is an integration environment for those slices.

## Distribution and business

Use channel-independent student grants for web subscriptions, app-store subscriptions, and fixed-term ESA purchases. Track approval, delivery, payment, and refunds separately. Prepare a source-linked registry for all states and DC, including related public purchasing programs. Provider approval, SKU approval, marketplace listing, and payment integration are independent milestones.

Price selection follows comparable-product research and direct evidence of parent willingness to pay. Measure activation, ongoing use, conversion, support demand, refunds, and renewal. A marketplace listing is not evidence of product-market fit.

Public availability also requires configured identity, verified parent enrollment, data export/deletion and retention operations, production monitoring, tested recovery, accessible interfaces, authenticated payment integrations, and platform acceptance. Native shells, test receipts, and local PDFs alone do not satisfy these requirements.
