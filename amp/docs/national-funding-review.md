# National education purchasing inventory

Corpora’s funding inventory separates the person who chooses a product from the organization that authorizes and pays for it. It is a sales and compliance research tool, not a list of places where ¡AHA! is approved. Source review date: October 17, 2026.

The scope is all 50 states and DC: statewide education savings accounts, special-needs scholarships, supplementary purchasing programs, school scholarships, relevant family tax benefits, and representative institutional purchasing routes. A state with only an institutional record has no verified standalone parent purchase route **in this review**; that is not a determination that no such program exists. Local district, charter, municipal, library and nonprofit purchasing opportunities require separate account-level research. Named tuition programs may be grouped where their commercial route is the same; they do not count as separate curriculum marketplaces.

`src/funding/national-review.ts` records the source supporting each channel, the purchase model, operating status, application or guidance URL, practical onboarding work, and unresolved questions. Onboarding text combines published requirements with next actions; it is not a substitute for the actual application. Older handbooks retain their original year in the record rather than being treated as newly issued policy. `operating` describes the program, not an open family application window or a Corpora approval.

## Purchase models

| Model | Who pays Corpora | What must be established |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Parent curriculum | Program/platform or an eligible family seeking reimbursement | Vendor and SKU eligibility, allowed purchase route, student-specific invoice and annual term |
| Tuition first | Marketplace or eligible family after required school charges | Remaining available funds, approved residual-expense category and term |
| School tuition | A participating school purchasing its own materials | A school contract and budget; the scholarship is not a standalone app entitlement |
| Approved specialist | An approved school or service provider, if permitted | Service credentials, student-plan fit and whether software can be included |
| Family tax benefit | The family | Accurate paid receipts; the family’s eligibility and expense qualification |
| Institutional | District, charter, agency or other authorized purchaser | Procurement, curriculum review, accessibility, data terms and a purchase order/contract |

Keep vendor approval, SKU approval, marketplace publication and tested payment settlement as independent gates. A state’s use of ClassWallet or Odyssey does not grant access to every other state on that platform. Nor does a portal account or preliminary expense review guarantee reimbursement.

## Commercial consequences

- **Iowa:** tuition and fees precede marketplace access each semester. Prioritize families with remaining funds rather than counting every award as available curriculum spending. [Iowa DOE](https://educate.iowa.gov/pk-12/educational-choice/education-savings-accounts)
- **Indiana:** the 2026–27 authority is IDOE. Its page explicitly permits subscriptions sold by approved ClassWallet marketplace vendors and describes Access Indiana plus ClassWallet applications. [IDOE ESA](https://www.in.gov/doe/eoq/esa)
- **Missouri:** educational-products applicants provide business registration or articles of incorporation. The provider rules require a US entity and prohibit sharing or rebating scholarship money to families. [Treasurer vendor requirements](https://treasurer.mo.gov/moscholars/Providers)
- **Utah Fits All:** review covers both the business and each offering. EIN, direct delivery and ownership-change reapproval matter. [Administrator requirements](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/37494425843611-Who-can-be-a-Utah-Fits-All-Scholarship-provider)
- **Utah Carson Smith Opportunity:** curriculum-provider and subscription-product classifications have different spending controls. Obtain the correct classification for an annual curriculum plus app bundle. [Fund-use guide](https://scholarship.cfe-fund.org/2026/07/20/scholarship-fund-use-guide/)
- **South Carolina:** ESTF participation is distinct from Option 1–3 homeschooling. Do not market it as a blanket homeschool wallet. [SCDE FAQ](https://ed.sc.gov/newsroom/estf-faq/)
- **Florida New Worlds:** existing balances can support mathematics purchases, but new awards ended July 1, 2025. [FLDOE](https://www.fldoe.org/schools/school-choice/k-12-scholarship-programs/reading/)
- **Montana:** the authority page’s litigation notice references a stay through a school-year end without establishing the current 2026–27 position. Treat new funded purchases as unverified until current authority is obtained. [OPI](https://opi.mt.gov/Families-Students/Parent-Resources/Education-Savings-Account)

Receipt-based tax benefits can reduce purchasing friction without a payment-platform integration. Ohio expressly names subscriptions, software and apps; Idaho requires the qualifying curriculum to cover four core subjects collectively and does not prequalify expenses. A math-only product must therefore avoid claiming that it alone meets Idaho’s curriculum requirement. Tax deductions, credits and advance payments have distinct eligibility and cash-flow effects. [Ohio documentation](https://tax.ohio.gov/individual/file-now/ohio-tax-credits-and-their-required-documentation), [Idaho requirements](https://tax.idaho.gov/taxes/income-tax/individual-income/popular-credits-and-deductions/parental-choice-tax-credit-and-advance-payment/)

## National and future channels

The federal scholarship tax credit under Internal Revenue Code 25F begins January 1, 2027. The IRS lists 30 states with advance elections as of September 14, 2026: AL, AK, AR, CO, FL, GA, ID, IN, IA, KS, KY, LA, MS, MO, MT, NE, NV, NH, ND, NC, OH, OK, SC, SD, TN, TX, UT, VA, WV and WY. An advance election is not an approved SGO list, family scholarship award, or vendor approval. Investigate qualified SGOs and their expense/onboarding rules when available; do not offer a federal scholarship checkout on the strength of state election alone. [IRS participating-state list](https://www.irs.gov/government-entities/federal-state-local-governments/federal-scholarship-tax-credit-fstc)

Section 529 accounts are family savings with tax advantages, not public grants. IRS guidance includes expanded K–12 educational-expense categories and a $20,000 annual federal limit from 2026. Account owners must establish expense qualification, enrollment/attendance connection and state tax treatment. A useful product capability is an accurate receipt, not a claim of universal 529 approval. [IRS Topic 313](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc313)

Title I and IDEA equitable services are institutional sales channels nationwide. The responsible public agency controls the program and funds; private-school participation does not create an individual parent entitlement to buy this app. Evaluate a school or district’s approved intervention plan, eligible costs and procurement process before proposing a purchase. [ESEA equitable services](https://www.ed.gov/laws-and-policy/laws-preschool-grade-12-education/every-student-succeeds-act-essa/esea-equitable-services-private-schools), [IDEA private-school guidance](https://sites.ed.gov/idea/idea-files/questions-and-answers-on-serving-children-with-disabilities-placed-by-their-parents-in-private-schools/)

## Operating controls

An application-ready dossier needs the legal entity and authorized signer, secure EIN/W-9 and bank records, actual released curriculum scope, sample materials, accessibility and privacy evidence, per-student SKU and term, price, refund policy, invoice examples and fulfillment instructions. The public repository contains requirement descriptions only. Keep actual identifiers, bank details, executed agreements and student documents in restricted systems.

Use the released coverage and evidence in every catalog listing. A reusable annual SKU should state its start/end policy, included downloads, learner limit and renewal rules. Approval for one SKU/version must not silently cover an expanded bundle, token credits, extra learners or unrelated services. Preserve originating program/order references through activation, settlement and refunds.

Before each application or sale, re-open the authority page and current handbook, verify the school year and any litigation notice, and record written product/term decisions. Check current eligibility again after administrator, platform, statutory or ownership changes. The audit checks references and completeness of required fields; it cannot certify the law, source accuracy or completeness of all purchasing opportunities.
