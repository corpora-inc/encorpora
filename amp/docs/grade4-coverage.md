# Grade 4 coverage

New Hampshire uses the Common Core State Standards verbatim. Arizona 2016 is that text with edits, inventoried on its own. This page is the practice judgment for grade 4. The source ledger still records every linked row as partial and leaves `coverageComplete` false.

Full means the row's mathematical demand is generated. Partial means a named part is still missing; the gap is the inventory note. Out of scope means a process standard, which item practice does not assess. None means the row has no item. Prime factorization, two-digit divisors, and thousandths were moved out of grade 4 on purpose and are not linked.

Problem types are the template ids. A type is listed only when a generated item asks for that row, not merely the same topic. When a row also links an other-grade template, the table lists the grade-4 types. The inventory note names the other-grade link.

### Remaining gaps

- **4.OA.B.4** (partial): 1 is not asked, and the item does not state that the number is a multiple of each of its factors. Prime factorization is grade 6 and is not linked.
- **4.OA.C.5** (partial): A later term and a feature are generated. A free explanation is not.
- **4.NBT.A.1** (partial): A comparison can use a factor of 100 or 1,000, so it is not limited to the neighboring place. Dividing the whole number by ten is not its own item.
- **4.NBT.B.4** (partial): The result of a four- to six-digit sum or difference is checked. The written algorithm is not.
- **4.NF.A.1** (partial): Equivalents are generated from numbers, strips, and one marked point on a number line. A written explanation of the part size is not.
- **4.NF.C.5** (partial): Tenths are rewritten as hundredths and can be added to hundredths. A sum that keeps both denominators visible is not certified.
- **4.MD.A.1** (partial): Both conversion directions are generated for length, mass, capacity, and hours and minutes. Seconds are not in the conversion table.
- **4.MD.A.2** (partial): Time, money, and some length stories are generated. All four operations are not assessed on distance, liquid volume, and mass.
- **4.MD.A.3** (partial): Grade-4 items choose a rectangle formula, find a missing area side, or measure a figure of two rectangles. The whole-number border items are the grade 3 perimeter skill and are not served as grade 4 practice.
- **4.MD.C.6** (partial): A drawn angle is measured. Sketching an angle of a given measure is not.
- **4.G.A.2** (partial): Triangles and quadrilaterals are classified from sides and angles. An angle of a stated degree, other than a right angle, is not the sorting rule.
- **4.G.A.3** (partial): Lines of symmetry are counted and checked. Drawing the line is not.

### Remaining gaps

- **4.NR.1.2** (partial): multidigit-digit-value names one digit's value. The usual number has four to six digits; other draws use six or seven digits, one million, or up to ten digits. digit-value-ratio and place-shift-value are the grade 4 large-place-value skill. digit-value-ratio compares the same digit in two places by 10, 100, or 1,000, so a comparison is not limited to the neighboring place. place-shift-value gives the new place and value after a one-place shift left or right, and the product when a whole number is multiplied by 10 or 100. Dividing the whole number by ten is not its own item.
- **4.NR.2.1** (partial): Numeric sums and differences of four- to six-digit numbers. Fluency and a required story on every item are not assessed.
- **4.NR.2.5** (partial): full-kits, seats-needed and kit-leftovers use totals through 55 or, less often, through 9,999. compatible-number-estimate gives one estimate, or asks which two whole numbers a quotient lies between. That between form uses a two-digit divisor. estimate-reasonableness gives one best estimate.
- **4.PAR.3.2** (partial): rule-table-fill-slots completes a two-column table from a stated rule. pattern-term-unknown-rule finds a later term when the rule is not stated first. Charts other than that table are not assessed.
- **4.PAR.3.3** (partial): factor-pairs-select asks for every factor of a number from 2 to 100, with a rarer number from 101 to 144. It does not ask about 1. factor-partner completes one factor of a pair, not every pair. multiples-select chooses the multiples of a one-digit number from a short list; those multiples stay below 100. divisibility-rule-check uses the rules for 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 9, and 10. It does not list the multiples.
- **4.PAR.3.4** (partial): prime-composite-sort asks which number is prime, composite, or neither. Sampled choices included 1 and numbers past 100; 0 was not in the sample. mcas-prime-factor-select chooses primes or some factors. Explaining the link to factor pairs is not generated. Other-grade: prime-factorization-exponents and prime-factorization-value are skill integer-powers, grade 6, and are not served as grade 4 practice. They are not linked.
- **4.NR.4.1** (partial): Items scale both parts or select an equal fraction from strips or numbers. equivalent-fractions-number-line chooses three names for one marked point. The line uses 2, 3, 4, or 5 equal parts, the names can use 5, 10, 12, or 100, and some points are past 1. A drawing the learner makes, and a box for the multiplier n/n, are not generated.
- **4.NR.4.2** (partial): compare-fractions-reason-blanks asks for the greater fraction and a reason when the numerator or the denominator matches, and it does not record the symbol. compare-fractions-symbol-blanks records <, =, or > for two pairs of the same whole, including an equal pair about half the time. Those pairs are not limited to a shared numerator or a shared denominator. Two different-size wholes are not a surface.
- **4.NR.4.3** (partial): Items ask for the greater fraction, an order, or the symbol <, =, or >. compare-fractions-symbol-blanks records the symbol for two pairs, and about half the seeds include an equal pair. order-fractions-slots can use one half as the benchmark. Benchmarks 0 and 1, and two different-size wholes, are not their own surfaces.
- **4.NR.4.4** (partial): fraction-decompositions-select chooses every matching sum, and one of them is made only of unit fractions. Amounts can be greater than one. Denominators above one are 2, 3, 4, or 6. Denominators at most one are 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, or 12. The learner selects the sums. Writing the equation is not generated.
- **4.NR.4.5** (partial): like-fraction-missing-part is one missing addend in a single equation. fraction-decompositions-select chooses several sums with the same denominator, including a unit-fraction-only sum, from a shaded strip. The learner does not write the equation.
- **4.NR.5.1** (partial): tenths-as-hundredths rewrites tenths as hundredths and, in the registered description, can add those hundredths to a hundredths amount as a numerator or a decimal. The grade-4 seed check found values below 1. A sum kept as two fractions with both denominators visible is not certified.
- **4.NR.5.2** (partial): decimal-grid-read names a shaded tenths or hundredths grid. decimal-mixed-number-blanks writes a decimal that has a whole part as a mixed number with denominator 10 or 100. decimal-line-point reads a labeled point. decimal-fraction-locate chooses the letter at a named tenth, written as a fraction with denominator 10, or at a named hundredth, including values above 1. Making a drawing is not generated.
- **4.NR.5.3** (partial): compare-decimals-hundredths asks for the greater or lesser decimal, including a shorter decimal that is greater. compare-decimals-symbol-blanks records <, =, or > for two pairs to hundredths, and one pair is a trailing zero such as 0.8 and 0.80. A written justification is not generated. Ordering decimals to thousandths is outside this expectation and is not linked.
- **4.MDR.6.1** (partial): elapsed-time-intervals finds an end time to the minute, including a crossing of an hour and noon. metric-length-rename and customary-unit-rename go from a larger unit to a smaller unit. unit-rename-larger goes the other way as a whole number, including grams to kilograms, meters to kilometers, and milliliters to liters. rename-time-units converts time in both directions. conversion-table-slots fills feet and inches, kilograms and grams, hours and minutes, and liters and milliliters. measurement-line-fraction-story reads a fraction of a mile with denominators 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, or 12. All four operations on every attribute are not assessed. Other-grade link: elapsed-time-hm is skill elapsed-time, grade 3, and metric-length-reverse is skill measurement-conversions, grade 5. They are not served as grade 4 practice.
- **4.MDR.6.2** (partial): Asking a question and collecting data are not generated. scaled-bar-grade4 answers a two-step question from a grade-4 scaled bar graph. frequency-table-grade4 builds a frequency table and answers a two-step comparison. fraction-plot-difference answers an addition or subtraction question from a line plot. A picture graph and a circle graph are not on these items.
- **4.GSR.7.1** (partial): angle-classify names a drawn angle and can be straight or reflex, which this expectation does not name. angle-kind-select asks for acute, right, or obtuse angles. Drawing an angle stays a printable and is not claimed.
- **4.GSR.7.2** (partial): turn-degrees finds a measure as a fraction of a full turn. protractor-reading reads a drawn angle. A missing-factor equation is not its own item. Sketching an angle stays a printable and is not claimed.
- **4.GSR.8.1** (partial): Items name a line, a ray, or a segment, tell parallel from perpendicular, classify an angle, or check a line of symmetry. angle-classify can be straight or reflex. Drawing those objects stays a printable and is not claimed.
- **4.GSR.8.2** (partial): shape-sides-select asks for a pair of parallel sides, a pair of perpendicular sides, an obtuse angle, or no right angle. symmetry-lines-select checks dashed lines of symmetry. classify-triangle-angles names a triangle acute, right, or obtuse. triangle-sides-angles-blanks names one triangle by its sides and by its angles; isosceles means exactly two equal sides. quadrilateral-attributes gives the most exact name of a trapezoid, parallelogram, rectangle, rhombus, or square from its parallel sides, right angles, and equal sides. A trapezoid here has exactly one pair of parallel sides. An angle of a stated degree, other than a right angle, is not the sorting rule. Drawing a polygon stays a printable and is not claimed.
- **4.GSR.8.3** (partial): composite-rectangle-measure finds the area or the perimeter of a figure made of two rectangles. Every outer side is labeled in centimeters or meters. The item asks for one of the two measures, not both, and does not recover a missing side. Three or more rectangles are not generated. A real-world story is not required. Drawing the figure stays a printable and is not claimed.
- **4.MP** (out of scope): Process standard. Item practice does not assess it.
