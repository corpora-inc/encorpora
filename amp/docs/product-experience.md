# AMP product and experience specification

AMP is a parent-managed mathematics program spanning early counting through precalculus and the full SAT Math domain. Its product is a connected learning environment: adaptive practice, visual instruction, substantial printable books, parent insight, and conventional annual student access. The provisional name is configurable. This document defines the intended product and its acceptance standard; it is not a claim that every described capability is released.

## The promise to the learner

Open AMP and find something worth thinking about. A session should feel like a mathematical conversation: a familiar idea, a surprising connection, a challenge within reach, and an earlier idea returning when remembering it is useful. The learner should not spend a session trapped in a dropdown-selected exercise family. There is no public ranking, punitive streak, countdown by default, or equation between fast clicking and understanding.

One learner may be confident with patterns, uncertain with fraction units, and still developing multiplication fluency. Preserve those separate paths. A correct algebra response must not invent evidence for every prerequisite below it; a missed calculation must not demote the learner's entire profile. Explain progress in terms of observed work and useful next steps.

A representative session might move from a fraction picture to a measurement puzzle, then an equation, then a number-line interpretation. An earlier error returns after intervening work, with a helpful representation or prerequisite rather than a near-identical demand to try harder. Secure ideas return at widening intervals. New representations and transfer tasks test whether the learner understands the idea beyond a familiar template.

## An immersive working surface

The learning application occupies the device viewport. Its chrome is compact and consistent. Navigation has generous padded hit areas and a visible active state without changing geometry. A marketing introduction never consumes the upper half of an active practice screen.

The problem owns the central stage. The answer controls occupy a predictable dock. Checking an answer keeps the question, diagram, and primary action in stable positions. Check becomes Continue in the same place. A short celebration appears in the problem's working space, with reduced-motion behavior, and gives way to calm feedback. There is no automatic page jump and no result appended below the visible screen.

Incorrect work opens a bounded teaching panel with the learner's answer, the correct relationship, and a step-by-step explanation. The learner can return to inspect the original problem. Correct work offers an explanation without forcing a lengthy interruption. Hints and lessons open directly in focus-managed panels. Escape and close restore the initiating control; advancing moves focus deliberately to the next problem or answer field. Network retry preserves the exact answer and submission identity.

On ordinary phone layouts, the prompt, required diagram, answer, and primary action fit without page scrolling. A fraction-capable math keypad provides digits, decimal point, minus, slash, clear, and backspace. Hardware and device keyboards remain supported. Safe areas, browser chrome, software keyboards, landscape orientation, and larger text are part of the design. Where physical space cannot hold legible content, a focused reading or answer surface is preferable to clipping content or shrinking labels into illegibility. Internal scrolling is an accessibility fallback, not the primary composition.

The lesson library opens a selected lesson as the primary view. It does not insert a reader beneath the catalog. Returning restores the learner's place. Search and concept grouping make a large library approachable. Downloads belong to a compact reader toolbar, not a stack of oversized buttons between the title and the first idea.

## A learning engine with several live frontiers

The curriculum graph separates official requirements, instructional skills, assessment facets, problem families, and prerequisite relationships. State alignment, SAT alignment, and broader mathematical enrichment are distinct crosswalks. Component-level requirements matter: reading a number is not evidence that the learner can explain its place-value structure.

Placement is woven into early practice with clearly bounded exploratory probes. Probes can sample a skill without declaring its ancestors mastered. Each strand maintains its own evidence and challenge range. Selection combines prerequisite support, recent errors, due retrieval, unpracticed facets, representation variety, and meaningful challenge. Within-session reservations prevent a generated batch from repeating one skill accidentally.

Assessment distinguishes independent solutions, hint-assisted work, lesson-assisted work, revisions, and transfer. Time may inform fluency only in tasks explicitly designed for that purpose. Distractors and error categories should identify plausible reasoning, not merely label an answer wrong. Diagnostic claims require calibrated evidence; heuristic selection must not be described as psychometric measurement.

Progress is a map of ideas encountered, ideas becoming secure, and ideas to revisit. Parent reporting explains evidence with examples and directs the parent to an appropriate lesson or activity. The learner-facing view emphasizes possibilities and growth rather than a wall of deficits. Optional focused practice is a deliberate temporary mode with an obvious return to the mixed journey.

## Mathematical content that earns attention

Every problem family has an explicit learning purpose, bounded parameter space, deterministic generation, exact grading, semantic diagram data, accessible representation, explanatory reasoning, and a review record tied to the source revision. Changing a seed changes values without breaking the mathematical meaning. Different numbers alone do not constitute pedagogical variety.

The complete program includes comparison and classification, expressions and equations, graphs and tables, geometric construction, multi-step modeling, data interpretation, probability, explanation, counterexamples, and proof appropriate to the grade. The response system must eventually support these forms rather than squeezing every requirement into a numeric blank. Calculator and non-calculator skills and SAT-style representations need their own assessment blueprints.

Diagrams are mathematical objects, not decoration. Fraction wholes remain consistent; tick marks have defined units; geometric relationships are represented accurately; axes, scale, legends, and labels are legible. Color reinforces meaning alongside labels, patterns, and shape. No solution depends on color alone. A separate solver checks mathematical results from displayed givens; a separate rendering check compares geometry with those givens.

## Books a family would choose to keep

A mini-textbook develops a coherent idea with enough depth to change understanding. It is not a template description plus a list of answers. Length follows the instructional arc, not a page-count target. Each book has a clear entry point, progression, visual language, opportunities to predict and explain, worked examples, guided practice, independent practice, mixed review, transfer, and useful solutions.

For fractions, an exemplary arc begins by identifying the whole and choosing a unit. It moves between sharing, measurement, number lines, areas, and symbols. Equivalence is shown as a change of units preserving amount. Addition combines quantities measured in compatible units. Multiplication becomes scaling. Division is developed through both sharing and measuring before the reciprocal rule is justified. Learners encounter plausible mistakes, explain why they fail, and choose examples that distinguish competing claims.

Page design serves that thinking. Use an expressive but restrained palette, clear hierarchy, generous writing space, readable mathematical notation, and diagrams placed beside the explanation they carry. Distinct page archetypes support discovery, visual proof, worked example, investigation, error analysis, practice, and reflection. Avoid repeating the same box stack on every page. Covers, running heads, page numbers, navigation, answer references, and print margins form a coherent book system.

Workbook tasks include drawing, annotation, explanation, building an example, finding a counterexample, and solving unfamiliar problems. Solutions show reasoning and acknowledge multiple valid methods. Where the available clues do not determine a unique answer, teach the learner to identify the missing information. Answer keys must not leak onto student practice pages.

The same semantic content supports a responsive reading view and carefully composed Letter/A4 PDFs. Screen layouts can reveal or manipulate a diagram; print versions must retain the explanatory argument without relying on interaction. Personal printing should be practical, with color and grayscale readability and adequate writing space.

## A family product with a straightforward purchase

Parents manage learners, annual access, progress, data export, deletion, and consent from a comprehensible family area. A student license covers the app and its associated learning materials. Child accounts do not encounter payment prompts, token balances, or provider setup. A parent can understand what is included, the term, renewal behavior, cancellation, refunds, and who to contact before purchase.

Ordinary annual subscriptions, native store purchases, and ESA-funded fixed terms converge on the same student access model. Provider approval, product approval, marketplace listing, family order approval, fulfillment, and settlement remain separate states. Show the parent the actual next action and status; a successful browser redirect alone never grants access. Duplicate or delayed payment messages do not create duplicate licenses or charges. Refunds and cancellations have explicit, tested effects.

Public-funding coverage includes all states and DC, with authoritative evidence for each relevant route and clear distinctions between direct curriculum purchasing, tuition-first accounts, reimbursements, tax benefits, and institutional procurement. An unknown or closed program is not presented as available. Tax benefits are not labeled ESA checkout. Marketplace approvals and real transaction acceptance must be evidenced before being advertised.

## Review and release standard

Review the actual learner journey and rendered artifacts. An automated count of templates, standards, or PDFs cannot establish instructional quality. A compiler cannot establish that a diagram communicates. A screenshot of the first page cannot establish that a book is well composed.

For a working slice, reviewers must follow a new family through enrollment, a varied session, an error, a hint, a lesson, a return to practice, progress interpretation, and a printable activity. Repeat under slow/failing requests and on small phones, landscape, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, and enlarged text. Inspect every page of representative publications and all unique diagram/page archetypes. Look for clipping, hidden controls, unstable layout, ambiguous mathematics, excessive text, weak progression, repetitive tasks, and misleading claims.

Instructional authors, mathematical reviewers, and visual reviewers have separate responsibilities. Findings require revision and fresh evidence. Publication evidence binds the reviewed content to its source revision and generated artifacts. Production readiness also requires real identity and payment sandbox acceptance, backups and restore, monitoring, privacy operations, accessibility, platform acceptance, and provider approvals for the channels advertised.

The completion target is a cohesive, usable, attractive and trustworthy program across the advertised scope. Demonstration content, green infrastructure checks, and an approved marketplace listing each prove only their own part of that target.
