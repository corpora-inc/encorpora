# Education-fund purchase requests

AMP can prepare a PDF quote or a program-payment invoice for a parent, or for an adult student paying for herself, and track its submission separately from verified approval, payment and learning access. An authenticated account holder selects an owned learner (a child, or their own adult learner) and an explicitly configured program/product offer. The server fixes the price, tax, term (a year or one month) and approved route in an immutable request snapshot. A guest named invoice reserves funded-pending access immediately; connecting it to a verified account and permitted learner activates that access. Program approval and payment remain separate verified facts. Preparing an authenticated quote or reporting submission does not grant access.

No program offers are enabled by default. The national funding registry records research; it does not establish Corpora's vendor approval or a product's eligibility. Deployment requires documentary evidence for the merchant, purchasing route, payment method and document requirements, plus the seller's actual product, price and term. Marketplace and direct-pay routes are supported. Reimbursement and app-store purchasing are outside this request workflow.

## Operator configuration

`AMP_FUNDING_OFFERS_FILE` identifies a private, bounded JSON array validated by `loadFundingOffers`. Each entry follows `fundingOfferSchema` in `src/funding-purchase/contracts.ts`. It includes a revision, program/year, SKU, merchant details, price and tax in integer cents, verified HTTPS destination, parent instructions, validity window and private evidence references. Use a new revision when an offer changes. To align a private file with the price already enforced by the loader, run `tsx scripts/align-funding-prices.ts /private/current.json /private/aligned.json`. It writes a new file without deploying or changing effective offer snapshots. The price a program sells at is declared one line per program in `src/funding-purchase/program-prices.ts`: Arkansas, Arizona and New Hampshire sell at the public list prices, $599 a year (`programAnnualPriceMinor`) and $59 a month (`programMonthlyPriceMinor`). A listed program sells at that price whatever `subtotalMinor` the file says (the loader logs the difference), and an unlisted program keeps the file's price. An issued invoice's snapshot is never rewritten; as with any offer change, a new price withdraws an invoice not yet converted, which the family replaces with a new one.

### Monthly offers

A program with a monthly price also sells one month at a time. `withMonthlyOffers` (`src/funding-purchase/monthly-offers.ts`) derives, from each of its annual named invoices under individual purchase review, a sibling with `termMonths: 1`, `termPolicy: "one-month-from-invoice"` after the invoice-date transform, the monthly price, and `annual` changed to `monthly` in its id, SKU and title ("one year" to "one month" in its description). Everything else, including merchant, route, instructions, refund terms and validity, is the program's own offer. The derivation is deterministic, so an issued invoice still matches after a restart. It is skipped for an offer approved product by product (its term evidence names a year), one with tax, text that still names a year, and a program whose offers file already sells a month (`termMonths: 1` may also be configured directly). The annual offer stays first: the pitch quotes it, and the invoice form offers **Yearly** or **Monthly** as a segmented choice. Optional `statusLabel` (at most 100 characters) is the one public status line for the program, copied exactly from the parent site's funding presentation (`presentation.statusLabel` in the site configuration), for example its vendor status and payment route. The signed-out pitch shows it under the funded Buy line; without it, the pitch makes no status claim. Each offer states its program's `refundPolicy`: `full-to-account` (the full amount back to the child's program account), `purchase-to-account` (the purchase as received; a payment platform's fee may not return) or `none` (the program allows no refunds or cancellations). A refunding policy also needs `refundSummary` (at most 200 characters), the terms in one line. The invoice form shows that line beside its purchase action; for `none` it says the program doesn't allow refunds or cancellations, so the money-back guarantee can't apply. Researched terms with their official sources are in `src/funding-purchase/refund-terms.ts` (`ar-efa`, `nh-efa`: full-to-account; `az-esa`: purchase-to-account); copy them into the offers file. An offer without `refundPolicy` loads with an operator warning and makes no program-specific refund promise. The parent pages show the 30-day money-back guarantee on every route with one footnote: refunds go back the way you paid, and a program that allows none is disclosed before payment. Synthetic fixtures in tests are not product prices or available offers.

There are two purchase-review modes:

- Without `purchaseReview`, the approved-offer configuration requires references for the exact SKU and term (`annualTermReference`, whatever the term) as well as vendor, route and payment evidence.
- `purchaseReview: "individual-approval-required"` lets an approved DirectPay vendor prepare a named invoice for the program to review. The exact SKU and annual-term approval references may be absent because the invoice starts that review. The seller must accurately describe its product, price and service period. This mode requires `route: "direct-pay"`, `documentPolicy: "named-student-invoice"` and `activationPolicy: "after-settlement"`. It cannot enable marketplace checkout or mark an unpaid purchase as settled. The separate funded-pending bridge below is seller-provided access, not program approval or credit authorization.

Preparing an invoice is not program approval. The family submits the invoice through its program's ordinary purchase process; no separate blanket product ruling is needed merely to generate that document. Verified paid fulfillment requires independently verified approval and full settlement for the matching invoice, correct account and learner, permitted learning, and the quoted price and term. A rejected or differently approved purchase must be reconciled; it cannot be provisioned as if the original invoice had been accepted.

An offer selects one reviewed document policy:

- `opaque-student-reference-accepted` produces a quote using the learner reference. No student billing name is accepted.
- `named-student-invoice` produces an invoice with a name explicitly entered by the parent as it appears in the program account. It includes a unique invoice number, issue and submission dates, merchant details, product/SKU, quantity, subtotal, tax, total and the one-learner term. The form asks for one email, "Email for the AMP account (parent, or the student if 18+)": the account that later claims the invoice. It is not a receipt or proof of program approval, payment or access. Submit it through the configured program route only once; do not also pay by card.

Enable an offer only when the specific program accepts its document format. Additional tax forms or a program-specific format require a separate supported workflow. The supplied PDF fonts support Western Latin text, including accents. Unsupported names are rejected before persistence with an instruction to contact the merchant for an invoice; names are never transliterated or discarded. Configuration rejects unsupported merchant/product text before showing offers.

A request has an initial payment deadline at most 30 days after creation, bounded by the offer's validity. Disabling an offer or changing its price, product, merchant or other purchase conditions blocks document download, submission reporting and conversion of an unconverted request. The deterministic invoice-date policy transform is compatible with issued snapshots: their original dates and terms are honored without rewriting the record. An original invoice linked to a verified order remains available to its parent even after offer withdrawal or expiry; it does not purport to show current payment status. Its history displays an unavailable-offer notice. It does not revoke existing licenses. An exact replay of a completed conversion remains a no-op even if its offer has since been disabled.

## Service dates

Each new named DirectPay invoice states a fixed service period. It begins at invoice creation and ends one calendar month or twelve calendar months later, using `fundedInvoiceTerm` in `src/funding-purchase/term.ts`. The UTC time of day is preserved; a day absent from the ending month is clamped to that month's last day. For example, January 31, 2027 plus one month ends February 28, 2027; February 29, 2024 plus twelve months ends February 28, 2025. Access is valid before the end timestamp, not at or after it.

The PDF, purchase history and paid license use those same bounds. Signing in, connecting a learner, reporting submission, receiving payment or retrying an import never moves them. An early renewal can overlap existing paid access; both paid grants are retained, and access is available whenever either is in force. The invoice is not silently scheduled after another license.

Date fields make the document usable for program review. The [New Hampshire EFA Provider Handbook, page 12](https://nh.scholarshipfund.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Provider-Handbook-August-2025.pdf#page=12) lists the invoice date and dates of service for DirectPay provider invoices. The [Arizona ESA Parent Handbook 2026–2027, page 43](https://www.azed.gov/sites/default/files/2026-11/ESA%202026-2027%20Parent%20Handbook.pdf#page=43) requires service dates with itemized charges. Starting AMP's term on the invoice date is the seller's business policy; these document requirements do not prescribe that start date.

`withInvoiceDateTerms` gives eligible live offers a deterministic revision, individual purchase review and the policy `one-month-from-invoice` or `twelve-months-from-invoice`. A changed service period is reviewed as a new purchase rather than borrowing approval for a different term. Stored invoices retain their snapshot. An invoice carrying `one-month-from-verified-provisioning` or `twelve-months-from-verified-provisioning` keeps its activation-based term, including an already scheduled renewal. `fundingOfferMatchesSnapshot` accepts only an exact current offer or that snapshot's exact date-policy transform. Other offer changes cannot use this compatibility path.

## Access while payment is pending

A guest invoice atomically creates its billing intent, one email-address reservation in `amp_access_gifts` with source `funded-pending`, and one PDF email in `amp_funding_invoice_outbox`. It creates no account or learner. Temporary access ends 14 days after invoice creation, or at an earlier invoice deadline or fixed service-period end. An exact creation retry neither extends that deadline nor queues another email. Reporting submission extends an activated bridge to the earlier of 60 days after invoice creation and the fixed service-period end. Thus a fixed monthly invoice never grants unpaid access beyond its invoiced month; a fixed yearly invoice can use the full 60-day processing period. An activation-based legacy invoice retains its 14-day initial and 60-day submitted windows. Submission preserves the invoice for payment processing through day 60, including renewal invoices with no new bridge. Repeated submission never restarts the clock.

The account holder confirms the same email, selects or creates the learner, and connects the invoice. Confirming the address binds the invoice to the parent it was made out to; it is not consent. Claiming binds the reservation and activates the existing grant machinery in the same transaction. Ordinary gift screens cannot consume invoice reservations. There is no consent step: a learner 13 or older is outside COPPA ([16 CFR 312.2](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-312/section-312.2)), and a child under 13 learns on the same restricted basis as free practice (`namePending`, [16 CFR 312.5(c)(7)](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-16/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-312/section-312.5)), including names entered by the parent. Every funded request, claim, provisioning and activation email asks the same question, `purchaseConsentSatisfied` in `src/server/account-access.ts`: does this learner learn on some basis today. Payment does not write consent or change the child's restricted data handling. Withdrawal for a named child under 13 blocks activation and learning; a grant never supplies consent. Payment for an unavailable learner waits without spending the provisioning retry budget. A fixed invoice's service dates continue to run while it waits; a legacy activation-based term has not started until activation.

Prepared invoices accept verified settlement through their original deadline. Submitted invoices accept it through 60 days after creation. A settlement verified within that window can be recorded after the deadline if learning permission is restored later. A fixed monthly service period can end before this payment-processing window closes. Late payment records the invoiced period without issuing another month. The stored payment evidence, account, learner, amount, program and exact offer snapshot must match; cancelled invoices and payments outside the window cannot use this path. Unpaid submitted records expire after the processing window. Retained submitted invoices can be downloaded after their original expiry without changing the document or treating it as proof of payment.

The temporary grant has status `funded-pending` in purchase history and does not mark a purchase approved, fulfilled or paid. Its expiry does not move on claim or retry; reporting submission applies the fixed processing deadline described above. Each account and normalized verified email can receive one unpaid bridge per program, across all learners and offer terms. The eligibility record contains no learner data and survives individual learning-data deletion. Adding a sibling, replacing a learner, cancelling an invoice or waiting for expiry does not reset it. Another invoice can be connected and paid without granting more unpaid access. Account deletion erases the eligibility record under the account privacy policy. Existing paid access is preserved. Operators can revoke the reservation through the existing access-grant command; cancellation and deletion revoke its grant, and the ordinary entitlement clock enforces expiry without a worker.

Verified payment within a fixed service period replaces the learner's temporary bridges with paid access through the printed end. Payment after the period ends records the settled purchase with no activation or new grant; it revokes only that invoice's bridge and preserves newer invoice bridges and paid access. A legacy activation-based invoice starts at verified activation or after existing paid access, with temporary bridges excluded from that scheduling calculation. A failed reconciliation rolls back bridge changes.

The invoice page explains the steps before collecting details, offers a link back to AMP, and sends the parent into learner setup and practice. The requested email includes the PDF, the program's payment instructions, a Billing link and the pending-access deadline. The same funded-delivery worker sends one submission reminder three days before that deadline, never on the creation day. A submitted unpaid invoice receives one reminder three days before its access deadline: the earlier of day 60 and its fixed service-period end. Legacy invoices use day 60. Both include the original invoice PDF and a Billing link; the submission reminder repeats the program's exact instructions. Submission suppresses pending submission reminders, and received payment, cancellation, revocation, deletion or expiry suppresses applicable reminders.

Invoice and reminder delivery use the funded worker's reviewed mail transport, with a lease, stable attachment bytes and distinct provider keys, eight attempts at most, and a retry window shorter than 24 hours. Delivery rechecks eligibility immediately before dispatch. An accepted provider response does not establish inbox delivery. Tests inject a fake transport and never send real email.

## Parent document and status

Billing contains education-fund invoice history, authenticated PDF downloads, program payment links and submission reporting. A signed-in parent creates an invoice on the learner's pay screen (`/pay/<learner>`, its education-funds tab): one action creates the invoice (queuing its email) and claims it for that learner, so access starts at once; the invoice's own page (`/pay/<learner>/invoice/<invoice>`) gives the PDF and the offer's submission instructions. The public invoice form (`/invoice/<program>`) creates an invoice before any account exists. Connecting that invoice to a learner uses the server's account ownership and learning-permission checks. Viewing existing records does not create access. Stable account identity is separate from the renewable access token.

### An adult buying for herself

An adult account holder can buy for her own learner without parental consent. The server distinguishes that learner from a practice profile and checks ownership. A claimed child's invoice uses the child's existing learning permission, including restricted parent-entry learning. Payment does not grant consent or permit additional data use. Every authenticated funding request uses `purchaseConsentSatisfied` in `src/server/account-access.ts`.

The account email must match the invoice and be confirmed before claim: the invoice is found by that address, so confirmation keeps anyone else from taking it. The page uses the selected learner or a single unambiguous learner choice; the invoice's billing name is separate from a learning nickname. A claimed invoice always stays attached to its recorded learner, regardless of a later URL selection.

The Letter PDF includes merchant/contact details, invoice or quote and student references, program/year, SKU, description, subtotal, tax, total, UTC payment deadline and the service period. Fixed invoices print their exact dates. Legacy invoices describe their one-month or twelve-month activation-based term (`fundedTermText`). The quote policy produces an unpaid quote, not an invoice, receipt, approval or promise of reimbursement. The invoice policy produces the separate payment-request document described above. Downloading it does not open a popup or send it to an agency. Parents must complete the program's process themselves. Cancelling a quote in AMP does not cancel an order submitted to the program.

Payment, approval, fulfillment, refund and access are separate status fields. A parent's “submitted” report is unverified. Private evidence references are excluded from parent purchase-status responses.

## Verified provisioning

`npm run funding:import -- /trusted/path/events.json` requires an explicit `AMP_DATABASE_URL` and the current offer configuration. This is an operator-only import, not a public webhook or marketplace integration. The bounded JSON input contains `requestId` and an explicit commerce-event bundle: order creation, approval, settlement as required by policy, fulfillment and, for an unexpired license, activation. An expired fixed invoice uses creation, approval, settlement and fulfillment only; it records payment without inventing an activation event. Verify the program transaction and its documents before importing anything.

The bounded operator import takes an exclusive parent-account advisory lock before request, order and learner locks, checks learning permission and learner ownership, and binds account, learner, SKU, channel, amount, currency and activation policy to the quote snapshot. It applies the commerce events and links the request in one transaction. Invalid or incomplete bundles roll back. An identical replay is idempotent; changed replay payloads are rejected. For legacy activation-based invoices, a second active or scheduled grant overlapping the proposed term is rejected with `overlapping_learning_access`, including pilot access. A legacy term paid early is not an overlap: it starts exactly when the learner's current access ends (`fundedTermStart` follows back-to-back terms, up to a year ahead), and provisioning accepts a later start only when an active grant of that learner ends at that moment (`funded_term_start_unsupported` otherwise). Beyond that the operator must reconcile the existing entitlement before provisioning; this importer does not silently replace it. Exact replay of an already converted request remains a no-op. This overlap check covers recorded entitlements; it does not detect an unpaid checkout session or an unissued future renewal. Operators must reconcile pending purchases across payment channels before accepting another funded order.

The default activation policy requires settlement. Activation after approval requires an explicit approved-credit agreement reference in the private offer. An operator cannot change that policy in the order. Fixed invoice licenses must exactly match `fundedInvoiceTerm(request)`; altered dates are rejected with `funded_invoice_term_required`. Activation must fall within those dates. A paid invoice processed after the end is fulfilled as a financial record without a grant. Legacy licenses start at activation, or at the supported end of current access for an early renewal, and end one calendar month or year later (`fundedTermEnd`). An invalid legacy term is rejected with `funded_activation_term_required`. Events preserve approval, fulfillment, settlement and activation as distinct facts. Refunds and revocations use the commerce ledger; disabling a purchasing offer does not silently alter a paid license.

## Purchase emails

Provisioning an unexpired license queues one account email in `amp_funded_activation_outbox` in the same transaction as the grant. An expired fixed invoice queues no activation message because it creates no active access. Immediate access uses the activation email, "Your AMP Math access is ready". A settled purchase whose grant starts after issuance uses a payment confirmation: it identifies the invoice and gives the learner's next month (or year) start and end dates from the grant, in UTC. Approval without settlement is not a received-payment confirmation.

Both messages go to the account's verified email through the funded delivery worker and require its reviewed notification policy, a live learner and that learner's learning permission. Revoked or expired grants cannot send. The grant's issuance and term dates select the confirmation even if delivery or a retry happens after the term starts; the worker does not send another activation email at that boundary.

The activation outbox's unique invoice key and `amp-funded-activation:<invoice>` provider key cover both messages. Concurrent workers lease one send; exact provisioning replays and repeated ClassWallet imports create no additional notification. Ambiguous delivery retries preserve the message and transport binding, stop before the provider's 24-hour idempotency window expires, and stop after eight attempts. A changed recipient or binding stops delivery for review. Provider acceptance is not proof of inbox delivery. Stopped historical notifications are not automatically reopened.

## Verification

The tests cover account isolation, server-bound prices, concurrent idempotency, consent/deletion checks, rollback, exact replay, policy mismatch, disabled/revised offers and financial-record retention. `tests/funded-invoice-term.test.ts` and `tests/funded-invoice-term.integration.ts` cover calendar boundaries, fixed dates, delayed claims, overlapping paid renewal, immutable retries, expired settlement and isolation from newer bridges. `tests/funding-offer-terms.integration.ts` covers issued-snapshot compatibility and capped temporary access. `tests/funded-monthly-adult.integration.ts` covers legacy scheduled confirmations, UTC grant dates, provisioning and ClassWallet replay, concurrent delivery, and retries across the term start. `tests/funding-purchase.integration.ts` covers activation copy and outbox safeguards. Run these with `AMP_TEST_DATABASE_URL` pointing to a dedicated disposable PostgreSQL database; fixtures migrate and remove their own schemas. `scripts/verify-funded-invoice-journey.ts` exercises the guest pitch, invoice, parent-entry setup and real practice against an isolated database. `npm run test:funding-ui` exercises three phone/landscape widths, consent gating, token renewal, submission without activation and PDF downloads. `tsx scripts/verify-funding-quote.ts` additionally requires Poppler tools (`pdftotext`, `pdfinfo`, `pdftoppm`) to inspect text, arithmetic, dimensions and rendered pages. Rendering checks do not establish acceptance by any funding program.

## Records and deletion

A student billing name belongs only to the immutable financial request and authenticated document; it is not copied into the learner profile, campaign aggregates or ad-platform exports. Exact retries must preserve that name; correcting it requires cancelling an unconverted request and preparing a new one.

Learning-data deletion removes unconverted requests, including drafts for a learner whose billing history requires a minimized retained record. Converted requests retain the financial snapshot and order link for reconciliation. Parent export includes an account-scoped funding-request dataset, including a supplied student billing name, with private approval, credit and document-policy evidence removed. The privacy-retention command removes expired unconverted requests; it does not purge linked financial transactions.

## Deployment and program review

Apply migration `0029-funding-invoices.sql` and deploy the compatible API and parent interface before enabling a named-invoice offer. Review the deployed parent notice for the student billing-name purpose and financial retention before collecting it. Existing opaque-reference offers and records keep their quote behavior. Do not change an issued offer snapshot or edit an applied migration. Use a new offer revision when document requirements change.

The Arkansas [vendor invoicing guide](https://efavendoronboardingchecklist.my.canva.site/efa-vendor-invoicing-and-payment-guide) requires a student name for services, vendor identification, itemized charges and a total matching the payment request. Rendering these fields does not establish approval for a specific SKU or successful ClassWallet submission. Verify the actual vendor/product route and payment/refund process before activation. Paid fulfillment uses the same learner permission as the claimed invoice. Temporary invoice access uses the restricted parent-entry learning permission; payment does not change that permission or the child's data handling.
