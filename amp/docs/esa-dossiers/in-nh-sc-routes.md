# Indiana, New Hampshire and South Carolina supplier preparation

Evidence checked October 17, 2026. These unsent drafts distinguish approved expense categories from acceptance of Corpora, its products, its listings and its payment arrangements. The [structured dossiers](../../src/funding/in-nh-sc-dossiers.ts) identify unresolved requirements. All operational offers are disabled.

## Indiana ESA: prepare monthly or book-only candidates

[IDOE administers the 2026–27 program](https://www.in.gov/doe/eoq/esa), serving eligible students with disabilities and their siblings. Its application window ended September 1. Access Indiana and ClassWallet handle applications and payments; the [February 2026 FAQ](https://www.in.gov/doe/files/2026-Education-Scholarship-Account-FAQ.pdf) distinguishes continued provider agreements from new-provider applications.

The [current IDOE-linked handbook](https://www.canva.com/design/DAHAkgmyfa0/ILZ0kO98sYWYQYQeEPDI-g/view), printed page 25, requires monthly educational subscriptions in its curricular-materials category and excludes annual and other multi-month subscriptions. It lists digital books and software, but requires curriculum purchases through ClassWallet Marketplace. The public viewer's “Go to page” control opens this as page 26 of 47. This evidence came from the current IDOE link, not an assumption that older Treasurer guidance remains operative.

**Application draft:** propose separately priced `[MONTHLY_SOFTWARE_SKU]` and `[BOOK_TITLE_EDITION_LICENSE]` candidates. Include actual coverage, accessible screenshots, sample pages, delivery method and license duration. Request publisher onboarding and exact item approval. Do not present the generic annual quote as an eligible Indiana product or disguise annual access as a book bundle.

**Transaction draft:** the handbook describes marketplace vendor review of website infrastructure, returns, legal and banking setup. Obtain those private requirements. Its service-invoice fields and payment estimates are not automatically a publisher marketplace contract. Request accepted digital delivery evidence, order/receipt identifiers and settlement rules before selecting activation timing.

IDOE's current webpage permits documented Direct Pay reimbursement; its linked handbook, printed page 17, says account holders cannot be reimbursed. Keep that conflict unresolved. The explicit curriculum marketplace requirement is a separate constraint. No parent reimbursement promise is justified by either an unpaid quote or a broad webpage summary.

## New Hampshire EFA: distinguish retailer and instructional service

[CSF's current eligibility guidance](https://nh.scholarshipfund.org/apply/nh-education-freedom-accounts/) requires verified eligibility and an award. Its [August 2026 capacity notice](https://nh.scholarshipfund.org/new-hampshire-education-freedom-account-program-reaches-12500-student-enrollment-cap/) says the 12,500-student cap was reached, while statutory priority groups can enroll and other eligible applicants can waitlist. Application availability is not a funded purchase authorization.

[PRO-05](https://nh.scholarshipfund.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/PRO-05-All-Provider-Must-Be-EFA-Approved-Policy.pdf) requires approval for direct service or instruction even when families seek reimbursement, while exempting nonstudent-facing retail vendors from that approval step. Obtain the classification for adaptive software; do not assume that either a tutor application or a retailer exemption applies.

The [2026–27 parent handbook](https://nh.scholarshipfund.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Parent-Handbook-CSF-8-2026.pdf) supports educational software, digital textbooks/workbooks and required curricular materials. It does not establish the annual AMP license, bundle or cross-year term. A bounded supplemental product description must match its actual coverage.

**Application draft:** use the [provider entry](https://nh.scholarshipfund.org/contact/) to identify the appropriate route. Attach `[EXACT_SOFTWARE_VERSION]`, named book editions, single-student terms, actual coverage and samples. Ask whether the experience is retail content or direct instruction, then obtain the required entity, product, ClassWallet and listing decisions separately.

**Document draft:** the [provider handbook, pp. 12–13](https://nh.scholarshipfund.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/Provider-Handbook-August-2025.pdf) separates retailer invoices from service-provider invoices. Retail documents identify vendor, date, item/description and amount; service-provider documents additionally identify student and service dates, with amounts per pupil. Paid receipts distinguish amount paid from amount due. Use the accepted classification rather than collecting student names by default. [Every transaction receives separate review](https://nh.scholarshipfund.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/STU-11-Pre-Approval-Qualifying-Educational-Expense-Policy.pdf).

[PRO-06](https://nh.scholarshipfund.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/PRO-06-Provider-Refund-Policy.pdf) requires refunds to the student wallet within 30 days, never directly to the family. Reconciliation needs purchase order, account-holder identifiers, items and refund amount. Obtain current private payment instructions through the approved operational channel; do not copy banking coordinates into the application or source repository. Digital proration and settlement timing need contractual confirmation.

## South Carolina ESTF: portal payments only

[SCDE reports the 2026–27 cap of 15,000 awards is filled](https://ed.sc.gov/newsroom/strategic-engagement/education-scholarship-trust-fund-program/). The [official-linked program site](https://sc-estf-program.com/en) gives eligibility restrictions, including exclusion of Option 1–3 homeschooling, and the provider intake. Families need an actual funded account. Provider intake continues independently of the closed student window.

The [current provider guide](https://sc-estf-program.com/hubfs/50248211/2026-2027-SC-ESTF-Service-Provider-Guide.pdf?hsLang=en) distinguishes math textbooks from online-course tuition. Neither category automatically establishes an annual app or PDF download license. SCDE approval precedes ClassWallet registration and bank linkage. Its staff background-check attestations require a publisher-specific applicability decision.

**Application draft:** supply `[LEGAL_ENTITY]`, authorized representative, actual software/book descriptions, proposed category, delivery method and private requested attestations. Ask for exact approval of each digital offering. Do not claim school or tutor status. [Section 59-8-140](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t59c008.php) requires annual provider reapplication; record that renewal obligation separately from a student's license renewal.

**Document and payment draft:** typed Pay Vendor invoices need provider/address, student and parent names, invoice/service dates, service type and total. Multiple students require separate lines and account submissions. The guide allows advance or after-service payment, but new-family services must begin on or after July 1, 2026. Exact annual digital terms are unresolved. Its payment statuses distinguish queued, initiated, processing and settled, with an estimated 2–10 business days for settlement; approval is not bank receipt.

[Sections 59-8-125(A) and 120(G)](https://www.scstatehouse.gov/code/t59c008.php) prohibit out-of-pocket parent reimbursement and direct parent/student refunds. Obtain the actual ClassWallet refund procedure, destination, deadline and digital cancellation rules. The generic AMP quote's opaque learner reference does not meet the named invoice requirement.

## Evidence required before an offer is enabled

For each route, retain approved legal entity, exact SKU/version, license term, category, listing, accepted document format, delivery event, settlement arrangement and refund policy. Keep business and student identity evidence in restricted records. A monthly candidate, a digital-book category, a filled student application or a marketplace listing does not independently establish a payable order.
