# ESA supplier dossiers

These unsent application drafts help Corpora prepare an educational math product for Arizona ESA, Florida PEP and FES-UA through Step Up, Texas TEFA, Alabama CHOOSE, North Carolina ESA+ West Virginia Hope, Iowa Students First, Georgia Promise and Louisiana GATOR. Evidence was checked October 17, 2026. No document establishes Corpora approval, a live listing, an accepted annual term, or a reimbursement guarantee. Private identity, banking and tax records belong in the restricted company record system, never this public repository.

The [combined structured requirements](../../src/funding/provider-dossiers.ts) distinguish ten dimensions for each program: vendor, software subscription, digital curriculum, printable download, bundle, term, listing, payment, invoice and refund. Each conclusion names its source and section; unresolved questions are explicit. The [audit](../../src/funding/dossier.ts) rejects missing dimensions, broken evidence references, invalid/future dates and evidence older than 90 days. This internal refresh interval is not an agency rule. The audit checks evidence organization, not factual truth, eligibility or legal compliance; recheck sources immediately before submission even when the audit passes.

## Recommended preparation order

1. Prepare Florida supplier interest materials and a single-student software/materials proposal. The category fit appears stronger than a claim to sell a complete grade curriculum. Obtain the issued onboarding packet before selecting an integration.
2. Prepare Arizona registration materials, but first resolve contract-year terms and a private named-student document workflow. The application software does not produce that route's required document.
3. Prepare Texas Educational Products evidence and confirm Texas registration. Resolve category/cap treatment and portal fulfillment terms before promising a funded annual purchase.

This order is a product/channel-fit hypothesis, not a revenue forecast or a promise of acceptance. Florida's linked intake describes selection and onboarding periods, so preparing the application is not immediate marketplace access.

Its public first page identifies **Mirakl**, private W-9/bank/business setup, a maintained catalog and test orders. The [supplier landing page](https://www.sufs.org/schools-and-providers/vendors/) also carries Ariba instructions. Record this as an unresolved platform conflict; do not implement either contract from marketing prose. Obtain the supplier invitation, catalog template, digital delivery, invoice, credit and settlement specifications.

[PEP category guidance](https://www.stepupforstudents.org/handbook-web/pep-purchasing-guide/) and [FES-UA's 2026–27 guide](https://fcsua.org/docs/fesua/2026-27-purchasing-english.pdf) support educational software and digital documents. Curriculum has a complete-course meaning; selective skills and two books do not establish a complete K–12 course. Price and describe each candidate accurately, obtain program-specific listing decisions, and keep bundle and cross-year annual terms unresolved until confirmed.

The [PEP handbook](https://www.stepupforstudents.org/handbook-web/pep-family-handbook/) and [FES-UA handbook](https://www.stepupforstudents.org/handbook-web/fes-ua-family-handbook/) distinguish purchasing from reimbursement. A marketplace item is not necessarily eligible for every scholarship. The draft receipt fields follow their materials sections; a quote alone is not payment proof. PEP prohibits returning scholarship funds directly to parents as a payment, refund or rebate. Supplier credit procedures must be obtained separately.

## Texas TEFA

Use the [Comptroller's application entry](https://educationfreedom.texas.gov/providers-vendors/) and [Odyssey Educational Products requirements](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/44035042254875-Who-Can-Become-a-Vendor-and-What-are-the-Requirements). A product publisher's evidence differs from tutor/therapy credentials. Corpora's Texas registration and tax standing have not been established by this repository.

[Offering categories](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/47640100236827-Texas-Marketplace-Categories-for-Offerings) distinguish software, educational applications, online programs, curriculum and workbooks. [October 5 purchase restrictions](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/52391445784347-What-Are-the-Purchase-Restrictions-for-TEFA) put software programs inside a collective technology limit of 10% of the student's annual award. Do not recategorize a software bundle as curriculum to avoid that limit; obtain the exact offering decision.

[General Odyssey payment guidance](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/22601445865883-How-Do-Vendors-Get-Paid) requires portal-based transaction documents and fulfillment evidence before the Net 30 period begins. This raises two blockers: the parent-download quote route is not established for TEFA, and fulfillment before settlement may require an explicit approved credit policy. The generic default activates only after settlement. Verify TEFA-specific digital delivery and contractual applicability before changing either behavior. [Refund guidance](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/33948374261275-How-To-Report-A-Refund) uses Odyssey approval; no digital-license-specific exception is assumed.
