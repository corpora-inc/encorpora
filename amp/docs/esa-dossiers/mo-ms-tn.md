# Missouri, Mississippi and Tennessee materials purchasing

These dossiers distinguish vendor eligibility from approval of a product, listing, order and payment. None establishes Corpora approval. Applications are unsent and offers are disabled. Sources were checked on October 17, 2026; inaccessible documents are identified below rather than treated as reviewed.

## Missouri MOScholars

Missouri is the strongest standalone-product lead in this group. The [Treasurer vendor page](https://treasurer.mo.gov/moscholars/Providers) accepts US educational-product entities with business registration or articles of incorporation. Prepare those private records separately from the public catalog. Tutor credentials belong to a different category. Related-party payments and returning scholarship funds to the parent or student are prohibited.

[Current marketplace categories](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/51891552203035-Missouri-Marketplace-Categories-for-Offerings) include educational applications, online math/learning software and curriculum. The September guide restricts Educational Applications to individual subscriptions, excluding family and lifetime plans. A second [October 7 guide](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/51282311836571-Missouri-Marketplace-Categories-for-Offerings) lists Educational Subscriptions and Educational Software under different categories. Obtain the operative category decision; do not assume the narrower restriction applies identically to every category or that its omission repeals it. This does not approve AMP, its annual term, its PDF licenses or a combined bundle.

The actual [2026–27 parent handbook](https://treasurer.mo.gov/pdfnew/Parenthandbook.pdf), revised January 5, 2026, limits service prepayment to the awarded July–June school year on printed page 4. Determine whether that rule applies to the software license before proposing twelve months from activation. A June 30 end date or different term may be necessary. Annual account renewal is not approval of a year-long product.

[Marketplace and school portal accounts are separate](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/52032964552091-Is-the-MOScholars-Marketplace-the-Same-as-the-School-Portal). The October category guide also lists tuition, creating an unresolved inconsistency with that portal article. Obtain the current non-tuition publisher workflow; neither school registration nor a category label proves an accepted seller route. Each [offering receives separate review](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/51427028992155-How-to-Upload-Offerings-to-the-Odyssey-Marketplace): prepare a unique SKU, exact category, bounded grade/age range, duration, included materials, total price, stock and image. Public product links do not replace marketplace checkout.

The [bulk-upload guide](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/51285284490651-How-to-Upload-Multiple-Offerings-at-Once-Bulk-Upload-Guide) requires its current dashboard template, exact category names and separate variant handling. Corpora's neutral supplier CSV is not a verified Odyssey upload. Public guides differ in SKU examples and character guidance; validate the actual template before implementing an export adapter.

Before integrating orders, obtain the Missouri vendor agreement and approved digital fulfillment model. Define order/student references, SKU version, amount/tax, licensed dates, delivery evidence, receipt identifier, settlement reference and refund-to-wallet reconciliation. Do not infer payment deadlines from another state's rules or use school tuition invoices as the software contract. Order submission is not settlement.

## Tennessee EFS

The [official FAQ](https://www.tn.gov/education/efs/efs-faqs.html) excludes standalone software. Its exception concerns software included without additional cost in an otherwise approved expense. Adding PDFs or calling an app curriculum does not establish that exception. Keep the standalone annual app offer disabled.

EFS first pays registered-school tuition and fees. Residual curriculum spending is tied to the enrolled school or approved summer/after-school academic programs. Establish an eligible materials route and exact PDF/license treatment. Purchases use the portal marketplace; funds are not paid directly to families. School tuition proration is not a digital-material refund contract.

The [school/provider page](https://www.tn.gov/education/efs/schools-and-service-providers.html) supplies school registration and invoicing guidance, not publisher approval. EFS, ESA and IEA have separate accounts, applications and rules.
