# Arkansas, Utah and Wyoming supplier preparation

Evidence checked October 17, 2026. These are unsent publisher application drafts and transaction requirements, not vendor approvals or accepted AMP offers. The [structured dossiers](../../src/funding/ar-ut-wy-dossiers.ts) retain unresolved classifications and ten evidence dimensions per program. Use the private record system for legal entity, tax, bank and student documents.

## Arkansas EFA

The [state program page](https://dese.ade.arkansas.gov/Offices/office-of-school-choice-and-parent-empowerment/education-freedom-accounts) describes 2026–27 funding and applications. A family's application is not a funded purchasing account. The [provider page](https://dese.ade.arkansas.gov/Offices/office-of-school-choice-and-parent-empowerment/education-freedom-accounts/information-for-service-providers) links an open vendor application while separately saying nonstudent-facing vendors need not apply as service providers. Resolve that distinction before choosing Corpora's intake; do not adopt tutoring credentials for a publisher.

The [rules, pp. 29–31](https://dese.ade.arkansas.gov/Files/Educational_Freedom_Account_Program_6CARpt.35_Legal.pdf) require reviewed invoices for direct payment and preapproval before incurring reimbursable curriculum expenses. Their refund provisions send unentitled funds back to the EFA or department, not the parent; a department-required return has a ten-business-day deadline after written notice. The annual software category, permitted term and digital delivery event need separate confirmation.

**Application draft:** Corpora supplies adaptive math software and separately identified authored books. Attach exact SKU descriptions, actual skill coverage, screenshots, sample pages and license terms. Request the nonstudent-facing publisher route and a decision for each software, PDF and bundle offering. Attach private `[LEGAL_ENTITY]`, `[EIN]`, `[AUTHORIZED_SIGNER]` and payment records only through the approved intake. Confirm whether service-provider financial-security obligations apply to this publisher classification.

**Document and adapter draft:** [School Choice Arkansas's state-linked ClassWallet guidance](https://schoolchoicear.org/classwallet) calls for vendor, student name, itemized prices/descriptions, total and service period. Prepare a private named-student invoice; an opaque learner reference is insufficient. Keep marketplace, Direct Pay and reimbursement distinct. The [reimbursement guidance](https://schoolchoicear.org/reimbursement) requires paid evidence and one-use preapproval. Bind that approval to the exact purchase rather than reusing it for renewals. Portal approval, fulfillment, bank settlement and refunds need separate records; settlement timing is unconfirmed.

## Utah Fits All

[USBE identifies Odyssey as administrator](https://schools.utah.gov/utahfitsallscholarship). The [provider instructions](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/37494425843611-Who-can-be-a-Utah-Fits-All-Scholarship-provider) require provider and offering review; prior ACE approval does not transfer. Prepare EIN, business/contact information and direct product descriptions, plus requested private records. [Eligibility](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/37838842666779-Eligibility-Requirements-for-the-Utah-Fits-All-Scholarship-Program) depends on K–12 age/residence/enrollment conditions and selection priorities. Sell only against an actual funded wallet, not an eligibility assumption.

The [expense guide](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/37894468672283-Eligible-Expense-Guide) lists educational software/apps and curriculum as goods. Obtain the exact annual license classification. The [spending guide](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/52216257447323-Utah-Fits-All-UFA-Spending-and-Reimbursement-Guide) distinguishes goods from services around the July 1–June 30 award year; some services may cross that boundary. That exception does not automatically authorize annual software. Student-specific spending also prevents treating one license as a pooled sibling purchase.

**Application draft:** submit `[SKU_VERSION]`, named book editions, single-student license, price components and proposed start/end rules for separate review. Keep whole-course claims bounded by the actual catalog. Obtain a written decision on digital delivery and cross-year access before selecting an activation policy.

**Document and adapter draft:** [receipt guidance](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/51870309021851-UFA-Acceptable-Documentation-for-Reimbursements) permits one uploaded file, so combine itemized vendor/date/price/total details with payment evidence. The August guidance permits some word-processor invoices with extra proof but excludes manually prepared cash/check documents. The September spending guide instead rejects invoices as purchase proof. Resolve that conflict; prepare a system-generated paid receipt with card last-four digits where applicable, not an invoice acceptance promise. An unpaid quote is insufficient. [Preliminary eligibility review is not final reimbursement approval](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/53057163439643-2026-27-UFA-Funding-is-Now-Available). The spending guide says completed reimbursement payments cannot be reversed; obtain a merchant refund/reconciliation procedure rather than promising automatic recovery from a parent account.

## Wyoming ESA / Steamboat Legacy

The [current WDE page](https://edu.wyoming.gov/parents/education-savings-accounts/) reports authorized payouts after the injunction was lifted, with underlying litigation continuing. The 2026–27 application period closed August 6. Reachable buyers are funded eligible families. Its four reimbursement categories include core K–12 curriculum, but not every marketplace software purchase. Do not move a software bundle into curriculum merely to obtain reimbursement.

The linked [provider handbook](https://edu.wyoming.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/ESA-Provider-Handbook.pdf) is labeled 2025–26. It permits out-of-state virtual/material providers and distinguishes tangible-goods-only vendors from providers requiring certification. That exemption does not establish treatment of digital downloads. It describes payment after service provision or product shipment, with limited tuition/enrollment exceptions, and refunds to the student ESA within 30 days of notification. Confirm the current agreement and digital delivery interpretation. Its financial-security threshold wording differs between the body and assurances; obtain the operative threshold before scaling.

**Application draft:** prepare the publisher's application and assurances, delivery modality, exact products and private financial records. Request current certification, security and category decisions for software and downloads. Listing acceptance is separate from entity acceptance.

**Document and adapter draft:** the linked [family handbook, pp. 10–11](https://edu.wyoming.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/ESA-Family-Handbook.pdf) requires an itemized vendor/date/total receipt and payment-method evidence, rejects handwritten receipts and states a 30-day submission window. Confirm current-year applicability: the separately indexed reimbursement-guidelines PDF returned 404 during review. The current webpage's narrower category list controls this preparation plan. No parent route is enabled on older handbook language alone.

## Shared operational decisions

[General Odyssey payment guidance](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/22601445865883-How-Do-Vendors-Get-Paid) describes portal documents, fulfillment and Net 30. It does not establish either Utah's or Wyoming's signed digital-product settlement contract. Obtain that contract before offering credit or changing settlement-first activation. Store accepted offer/version/term, student-specific authorization, delivery evidence, settlement reference and refund destination as distinct records.

The existing AMP unpaid PDF quote is a preparation artifact. None of these dossiers makes it a universal invoice, a paid receipt, a marketplace integration or an approval. Keep operational offers disabled until the exact application, SKU, term, document route and payment requirements have independent evidence.
