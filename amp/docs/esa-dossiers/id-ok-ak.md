# Idaho, Oklahoma and Alaska materials purchasing

These records cover two parent tax-credit routes and one public district purchasing route. They do not establish Corpora approval or product eligibility. Applications are unsent and runtime offers are disabled. Sources were checked on October 17, 2026.

## Idaho parental choice credit and advance

The [current statute](https://legislature.idaho.gov/statutesrules/idstat/Title63/T63CH30/SECT63-3029N/) permits curricula from multiple vendors. The [Tax Commission guidance](https://tax.idaho.gov/taxes/income-tax/individual-income/popular-credits-and-deductions/parental-choice-tax-credit-and-advance-payment/) applies this to 2026 onward and requires the combined academic instruction to cover English language arts, mathematics, science and social studies. AMP supplies mathematics materials; it is not a four-subject curriculum. The exact treatment of its software, PDF editions and bundles requires qualification.

This is a parent application and tax-credit/advance process, not a vendor marketplace. The state explicitly does not prequalify expenses, and an award is not approval of the purchases listed in an application. The [next published window](https://myschoolchoice.idaho.gov/how-to-apply/) is January 15–March 15, 2027 for the 2026 credit or 2027 advance. Older filing and residency examples on the same page describe the earlier cycle; do not reuse them as a complete 2027 checklist.

Paid evidence must identify the seller, purchased item, amount, payment status and payment date. An unpaid AMP quote does not satisfy this purpose. Keep licensed dates and any refund adjustment available for the family's records. Public-school enrollment restrictions and cross-year access make a rolling annual term an unresolved classification issue.

The [family FAQ](https://myschoolchoice.idaho.gov/faqs/) separates an advance from a recurring entitlement: each child can receive an advance only once, and a prior credit prevents a later advance. Its December 31, 2027 reconciliation deadline specifically concerns the advance awarded in 2026. A family award must never trigger merchant settlement or access activation by itself.

## Oklahoma homeschool credit

The [homeschool section](https://oklahoma.gov/tax/individuals/parental-choice-tax-credit/pctc-taxpayers.html) requires no application; the family claims eligible expenses on its tax return. The private-school application calendar, accreditation and Enrollment Verification Number process are separate. There is no publisher marketplace onboarding in this reviewed route.

The [official rules](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/tax/documents/resources/publications/individuals/pctc/710-Chapter-50-TEXT-PCTC.pdf), printed pages 8–9, section 710:50-15-173, support instructional materials and nonpublic learning programs. They explicitly illustrate a shared online subscription whose cost can be claimed only once. Expenses must be incurred and paid in the claimed tax year; excess costs cannot be carried into another year. Public/private enrolled semesters and their breaks are excluded. The per-student maximum and aggregate cap do not guarantee a family's credit.

The rules define curriculum as a complete course for a content area or grade. Use bounded descriptions of the actual AMP materials; do not label the available collection a complete K–12 program. Obtain an interpretation for the exact software/download bundle and any annual term spanning excluded periods.

The linked [Form 591-D](https://oklahoma.gov/content/dam/ok/en/tax/documents/forms/tax-credits/591-D.pdf) actually bears **2025**, despite the landing page's 2024 link label. It requires legible receipts, a separate form per student, and filing with Form 511 or 511-NR. Its expense table identifies vendor, description, purchase date and amount. A 2026 form was not verified. The merchant should provide truthful paid records and adjustments, not populate tax forms or promise a tax result.

## Alaska: Galena/IDEA purchase orders

Alaska distinguishes [district-supported correspondence schooling](https://education.alaska.gov/alaskan_schools/corres) from independent homeschooling. The [state directory](https://education.alaska.gov/alaskan_schools/corres/correspondence-directory) identifies Galena City School District's IDEA program. The following terms apply to that program only.

IDEA's [curriculum-vendor instructions](https://www.ideahomeschool.org/contact-locations/vendor-info/curriculum) describe an applicability review and W-9 collection for inclusion on its purchase-order list. The page expressly says that inclusion is not vendor approval or endorsement. Its public form link was identified but its embedded fields were not retrieved.

For direct orders, IDEA issues a PO, receives an invoice after delivery, and publishes Net 30 from invoice receipt. One PO can cover several families. The invoice needs seller contact details, PO number, family names, item titles/prices and shipping by family. Materials orders are accepted July 1–March 31; invoices must arrive within the July–June fiscal year. Orders taken at its spring fair for the following year wait for the post-July 1 PO before shipment.

The [allotment page](https://www.ideahomeschool.org/about/allotment) lists apps, online courses, textbooks and workbooks. These categories do not establish AMP's digital license, printable-download rights or annual duration. The vendor page's fulfillment description uses physical shipment. Obtain a digital delivery/acceptance agreement, access dates, cancellation/refund instructions and current district privacy requirements before adapting that process. Do not infer those terms from another Alaska district or treat a parent requisition as a paid order.

## Preparation and integration boundaries

| Route                       | Reviewable product evidence                                                  | Financial document                                     | Missing operational decision                                       |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Idaho parent credit/advance | Exact math scope, edition, software function and license dates               | Itemized paid receipt plus refund adjustments          | Eligible component classification and annual-period allocation     |
| Oklahoma homeschool credit  | Actual materials versus complete-course claims; individual/shared rights     | Per-purchase paid receipt without duplicate allocation | Exact SKU, overlapping-term treatment and applicable tax-year form |
| Galena/IDEA direct PO       | Secular learning-plan fit, named editions, price, access and delivery method | District PO-linked invoice with family line items      | Digital acceptance, year boundary, privacy and refund agreement    |

Tax-credit purchases follow ordinary merchant payment and fulfillment. The later tax claim is the family's process; it is not an ESA wallet settlement. District orders need separate PO, delivery, invoice and settlement states. The existing unpaid quote is useful preparation but is neither a paid tax receipt nor an accepted district invoice.

Keep business records and family identifiers private. Public catalog material can show synthetic examples; it should not contain tax returns, taxpayer IDs, real district orders or student records. The dossier audit checks evidence references, freshness and disabled status. It cannot determine tax eligibility or grant provider approval.
