# Alabama, North Carolina and West Virginia supplier packet

This unsent supplement covers three public purchasing routes checked October 17, 2026. It does not establish Corpora acceptance, product eligibility or an enabled offer. The [structured evidence](../../src/funding/additional-provider-dossiers.ts) separates software, digital curriculum, printable downloads and bundles. Category support is not approval of an AMP SKU. These routes are a subset of the national inventory, not exhaustive coverage of its second-wave programs.

Use the shared [product/application draft](product-and-application.md) and [document drafts](documents.md) with the route-specific requirements below. Keep legal names, tax identifiers, banking documents and student identities in restricted records. The public packet uses placeholders.

## Alabama CHOOSE

The [2026–27 provider guide](https://classwallet.com/alchoose/assets/CHOOSE_ACT_education_service_provider_guide_2026-2027.pdf), updated July 27, 2026, describes year-round provider applications followed by ALDOR approval, attestation and ClassWallet registration (pages 4–5). The family application deadline on the [ALDOR page](https://www.revenue.alabama.gov/tax-policy/the-choose-act/) is not the provider intake deadline. Use the [application linked by the guide](https://app.smartsheet.com/b/form/8fe31b08167e4b028e1082fdae9141f1); its complete fields require review before the draft can be considered complete.

Prepare this application attachment:

| Field                | Draft evidence or private placeholder                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Applicant            | [Exact registered legal entity], [authorized signer], [restricted entity/tax records]                                                                    |
| Provider description | Publisher of adaptive math practice and authored downloadable math materials; specify the actual skills and editions in the generated catalog attachment |
| Proposed category    | Educational software/apps for the software candidate; separate review of individual curriculum materials and downloadable books                          |
| Student scope        | One student per license; no tutor or therapy service represented                                                                                         |
| Service period       | [Explicit approved start and end dates within the funded academic year]                                                                                  |
| Fulfillment          | [Approved delivery route and evidence], [account activation policy]                                                                                      |
| Commercial terms     | [Accepted price], [fee/settlement agreement], [original-channel refund procedure]                                                                        |

Pages 9–12 support math software/apps and individual-student curriculum categories, but do not resolve a standalone PDF license or combined bundle. The online-learning-program category restricts educational subscriptions to the 2026–27 academic year; service invoices also concern that academic year. Confirm how the exact software offering is classified and whether the same term rule applies. A generic twelve-month term starting on activation can cross that boundary and must not be offered on this evidence alone.

**Typed invoice draft:** [provider legal name and address]; [parent name]; [student name]; [invoice number/date]; [service dates]; [specific service/product description]; [total]. Page 7 requires those named-party and service fields and prohibits references to **CHOOSE Act or ClassWallet** on the invoice. Keep program routing metadata outside the customer-facing document. Page 8 distinguishes service periods and individual student charges. The generic AMP quote names the program and uses an opaque learner reference; it does not satisfy this invoice design. A private named-document workflow, approved term and exact product payment/refund agreement are prerequisites.

## North Carolina ESA+

[SEAA's curriculum guidance](https://k12.ncseaa.edu/the-education-student-accounts/current-families/allowable-expenses/curriculum/) directs **new product/curriculum vendors to ClassWallet Marketplace**, rather than SEAA's credentialed service-provider registration. Online curriculum must provide academic instruction; mathematics is a supported core subject. Prepare the product catalog attachment and request the actual marketplace integration contract through [ClassWallet's ecommerce entry](https://classwallet.com/ecommerce-vendor-integration/). Published integration options and percentage-fee language are not Corpora's negotiated agreement.

The application attachment should identify [exact SKU/version], [instructional scope and sample lesson], [student license], [software versus downloadable component], [separate component prices], [proposed term], [digital delivery evidence], [support contact] and [refund procedure]. Do not describe selective skills as a complete grade curriculum. Obtain exact decisions for PDF licenses, bundles and cross-year terms.

The **existing Product Provider List** has a distinct Pay Vendor route. SEAA accepts an exact-item/final-price cart screenshot or invoice, quote or purchase order, reviews the request, and transfers approved funds before the vendor completes the sale. Do not configure that route without evidence that Corpora belongs to the relevant list. A sample for that route must identify [listed vendor], [exact item and quantity], [final price including applicable charges], [document reference] and [approved service term]; obtain the complete accepted document specification before enabling it.

The [2026–27 educational-app guidance](https://k12.ncseaa.edu/media/wi0miksa/educational-apps.pdf) provides a narrower prior-approval reimbursement route for mobile app-store purchases. Third-party vendor subscription receipts and desktop applications are excluded from that exception; in-app subscriptions must be purchased through the app store. Unlisted-app reconsideration is tied to annual June changes. A web Stripe receipt is not an eligible mobile-app receipt by inference, and no native AMP listing or approval is established. [Service-provider fee guidance](https://k12.ncseaa.edu/the-education-student-accounts/current-families/service-providers/) concerns a different provider scope; do not copy it into a product-vendor contract.

## West Virginia Hope

Start from the official [New Provider Request Form entry](https://hopescholarshipwv.gov/Home/Service-Providers). The [provider handbook updated August 10, 2026](https://hopescholarshipwv.gov/Portals/HopeScholarship/content/Documents/Forms/ESP/WV%20Hope%20Service%20Providers%20Handbook.pdf) describes coordinator review and provider agreements. Its categories include online programs, math curriculum/supplemental materials and required software; exact AMP subscription, download and bundle treatment needs review. The handbook defines curriculum as a complete course within its content area or grade, so a limited product should be described as supplemental where appropriate.

Prepare [legal entity and signer records], [offering descriptions and samples], [student-contact support inventory], [background-check certifications required for that contact], [price-parity review], [term and prorated-refund schedule] and [conditional control-employee disclosures]. The handbook's student-contact requirement includes virtual contact such as chat, email and telephone (page 10). A self-directed app does not by itself resolve the support team's obligations. Verify the complete request form and actual agreement before supplying private records.

Hope uses its own portal managed by Student First Technologies. The [portal guide](https://hopescholarshipwv.gov/Home/Service-Providers/Portal-User-Instructions.aspx), PDF pages 16–22, separates requested purchases, invoice review, approval, payment processing, paid status and completion. Generate the invoice from the requested purchase and check student/order details. A generic parent quote is not a portal invoice. The [bank guide](https://hopescholarshipwv.gov/Home/Service-Providers/Connect-Your-Bank-Account.aspx) is a separate setup prerequisite; no bank connection is implied by provider acceptance.

**Portal reconciliation draft:** [provider ID], [approved offering/SKU], [student purchase reference], [portal invoice ID], [amount and service term], [approval evidence], [payment reference], [digital delivery/completion evidence], [refund reference if applicable]. Preserve each stage independently. Confirm what constitutes digital delivery before marking completion; do not promise the guide's processing estimates as a cash receipt deadline.

Handbook pages 13–14 require returns for unprovided services, overcharges and other specified circumstances, generally within 30 days of the triggering event. Return funds to the student's Hope account, not directly to the parent. Record [trigger date], [unused-term calculation], [Board instructions], [original-channel return evidence] and [license revocation/adjustment]. Exact software proration and cross-year terms require agreement-specific confirmation.

## Readiness decisions

All three routes require a private business-record owner to reconcile the actual entity, tax and bank records; a product owner to bind version, license, term and price; and a finance owner to establish payment, delivery and refund evidence. Alabama additionally needs named invoices without program branding and academic-year term handling. North Carolina needs a verified new-vendor marketplace route or separately proven existing-provider status. West Virginia needs portal transaction reconciliation and student-contact obligations assessed against actual operations.

The dossier export is an evidence draft, not a submission file for any portal. No offer becomes enabled when an audit passes. Record actual vendor acceptance, exact product/term approval, listing and payment configuration before independently reviewing an operational offer.
