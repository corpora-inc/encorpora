# Corpora ESA readiness register

This register separates repository evidence from company and administrator evidence that must be supplied privately. Roles are proposed responsibilities, not claims that a person has accepted an assignment. Every first-wave offer is disabled; there are no submitted applications or verified approvals in this dossier.

## Release evidence packet

Create a restricted release folder with an index containing: source commit, generation date, deployed app version, exact book content/PDF hashes, catalog manifest, scope crosswalk, reviewed sample problems/keys, accessibility report, privacy/security notice and processor review references, product license/price schedule, support/refund terms, merchant identity record references and channel acceptance records. Store actual private documents separately; the index may point to their controlled record IDs.

Use `npx tsx scripts/provider-dossier.ts` to inventory the current source catalog and check research freshness. Source artifacts prove what is present in that revision; they do not establish deployment, comprehensive curriculum coverage, educational efficacy or administrator approval. A source date is when a researcher checked it, not the source's legal effective date. The source locator records current-year ambiguity and update dates when visible.

## Decisions required before enabling a route

The first-wave channels do not share a universal parent quote workflow. Select a permitted document route and approved activation policy for each SKU. If settlement follows fulfillment, finance must approve and evidence any credit exposure before selecting after-approval access. A parent saying “submitted” is neither agency approval nor payment. Reconcile accepted orders and existing grants before provisioning; overlapping unpaid purchases across channels still need operational reconciliation.

Keep public funding eligibility separate from parent consent. Do not assert that an ESA order, platform email or ordinary card charge is verifiable parental consent. Use the deployed reviewed consent method and avoid acquiring student details just to fill a sample application. Where an agency requires a real student name, design and review that narrowly scoped collection and document flow first.

Refresh source research before an application, after a rule/platform change, and at least every 90 days as an internal review policy. Reconcile historical Corpora vendor records before creating duplicate registrations. Applications, agency messages, merchant setup, real orders and live charges require their own authorized operational action; this packet performs none of them.
