# Iowa, Georgia and Louisiana marketplace readiness

These unsent supplier dossiers were researched October 17, 2026. A shared administrator does not give the three programs shared eligibility rules. The [structured evidence](../../src/funding/odyssey-state-dossiers.ts) records sources, category distinctions and unanswered questions. The combined dossier audit resolves its references to shared Odyssey payment evidence. No application, accepted SKU, live listing or enabled offer is established.

## Product decisions before application

**Iowa:** the [currently linked expense list](https://educate.iowa.gov/media/8595/download?inline=), dated May 25, 2023, requires additional verification for vendor-billed learning software and textbooks. It separately excludes educational subscriptions/memberships. Curriculum fees have a school-billed eligibility mark. An annual AMP software license therefore needs an exact classification decision, including any required syllabus or school verification. Neither renaming the subscription nor grouping it with a book resolves this issue. Confirm the older document's current applicability.

**Georgia:** the [offering categories](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/36309274113691-Georgia-Marketplace-Categories-for-Offerings) include learning software, applications, subscriptions, math curriculum and workbooks. The [linked expense list](https://mygeorgiapromise.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/5.8.25-Eligible-Expense-List.pdf) is titled 2025/26; confirm applicability before submitting a 2026–27 offering. [Offering instructions](https://mygeorgiapromise.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/06.25.25_Georgia-Promise-Scholarship_Offering-Portal-One-Pager.pdf) call for subject/grade metadata and accurate prices and permit applicable bundles. Individual approval is required. A publisher of materials must not claim the accreditation required for online-course tuition in the [provider handbook](https://mygeorgiapromise.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Service-Provider-Vendor-Handbook_Updated-June-25-2025.pdf).

**Louisiana:** [learning software and material subscriptions](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/36309591768091-Louisiana-Marketplace-Categories-for-Offerings) are categories, while the [approved textbook list](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/35905159982747-Approved-Curriculum-Books-for-the-LA-GATOR-Scholarship-Program) names particular editions. Obtain the review path for original supplemental books rather than assuming the broader materials language accepts them. [Provider policy](https://doe.louisiana.gov/topic-pages/louisiana-school-choice/la-gator/la-gator-policy) also requires annual attestations and an additional financial-soundness criterion when budgeted ESA receipts exceed $100,000. Private business evidence must establish the applicable criterion.

For every candidate, prepare [SKU/version], [actual covered skills/grades], [sample lesson and book edition hash], [one-student license], [software/download component prices], [term start/end rule], [personal-print rights], [support contact] and [refund procedure]. Exact term, bundle and PDF-license decisions are unresolved. Account rollover is not permission for an annual subscription crossing program years.

## Application and transaction drafts

Use the state-specific links in the [vendor lifecycle](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/47340139248923-Vendor-Application-Lifecycle), not a single cross-state approval claim. [Business verification guidance](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/24272969415835-How-Can-I-Apply-to-Be-A-Vendor) gives incorporation, W-9 and EIN documentation as examples; each state may require different records. Store those privately with [authorized signer], [legal entity], [approved public description] and [bank/Stripe setup evidence]. Georgia's current page says year-round provider intake despite older handbook windows; Louisiana describes [rolling applications](https://doe.louisiana.gov/topic-pages/louisiana-school-choice/la-gator/la-gator-service-providers). Obtain complete issued attestations before treating any packet as ready.

For Louisiana, [application instructions](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/33881578753691-How-to-Complete-the-LA-GATOR-Vendor-and-Service-Provider-Application) distinguish the private representative from the public organization/support profile. Prepare accurate grades, languages, operating details and the vendor-specific attestations; do not reuse student-facing school attestations without reviewing their scope.

**Internal order reconciliation record:** [state/program], [approved vendor ID], [approved offering ID/version], [student order reference], [exact amount/tax], [accepted term], [order authorization], [actual delivery confirmation], [settlement reference/date], [refund/adjustment reference]. This is an internal specification, not a parent submission attachment.

General [payment guidance](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/22601445865883-How-Do-Vendors-Get-Paid) puts transaction documents in the portal and starts Net 30 after delivery evidence. [Fulfillment instructions](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/21334947584923-How-to-Fulfill-an-Order) distinguish actual service receipts from physical tracking. Confirm the digital-license equivalent. Iowa's [older vendor FAQ](https://educate.iowa.gov/media/8672/download?inline=) gives a different 2–5-business-day estimate; obtain the governing current agreement rather than promise either schedule. An after-settlement activation default can conflict with delivery-before-payment: finance must approve a specific credit policy before activation on approval.

Georgia's provider handbook excludes product reimbursement. Iowa's expense list excludes off-marketplace purchases. The generic AMP parent quote is not an established purchase route for any of these three programs. Required adapter work is portal order ingestion/reconciliation, approved digital delivery evidence, state-specific terms and original-channel refund handling—not automatic acceptance of a parent's uploaded quote.

General [return guidance](https://support.withodyssey.com/hc/en-us/articles/21902731722651-Understanding-Refunds-Returns-and-Cancellations) restricts cancellation and returns and does not establish a software-specific proration contract. Louisiana policy requires refunds to the ESA, never the parent; its 30-day unused-reservation rule has a particular trigger and is not a universal refund deadline. Obtain each state's digital failure/cancellation policy and preserve approval, fulfillment, payment and revocation as separate events.
