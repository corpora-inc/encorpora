# AMP transaction and support drafts for Florida review

These are document fields and operating decisions for a proposed MyScholarShop product. They are not issued invoices, paid receipts, contractual promises or accepted platform instructions. Complete a restricted operational copy only after the exact product, term, price, tax treatment and supplier agreement have been reviewed.

## Product and invoice line

| Field | Value to supply |
| --- | --- |
| Document type | Quote, invoice, paid receipt or credit; never interchangeable |
| Merchant | Verified legal name, address and authorized contact |
| Document/reference | Unique immutable identifier, issue date and applicable expiration date |
| Program and order | FTC-PEP, administrator order/reference and approved candidate/SKU mapping |
| Purchaser/student | Only the identifiers required by the actual channel; no student name in public samples |
| Item | One learner's software access, or a separately identified PDF title and edition |
| Quantity | One student license or the actual authorized quantity |
| Term | Exact accepted start/end dates and activation rule; do not substitute a generic annual label |
| Amounts | Unit price, quantity, discount, taxable subtotal, tax, allowed fees and total in USD |
| Delivery | Actual activation/delivery reference and date; no shipping tracking for a digital download |
| Payment | Unpaid status on quote/invoice; actual settled amount, date and nonsecret payment reference on receipt |
| Credit | Original order/receipt reference, item, reason, amount, program-directed destination and completion reference |

Arithmetic: subtotal = sum(quantity × unit price) − discounts; total = subtotal + tax + permitted fees. Do not mark an invoice paid from a parent-reported submission or an approved-but-unsettled order. A quote is not proof of purchase. Do not expose full payment instruments or private banking details. Preserve separate component lines and actual price allocation for any accepted bundle.

## Parent support copy for review

“AMP support can help with account access, activation, technical difficulties and locating instructional materials. Include your order reference and a description of the issue. Do not send a child's sensitive records or payment details. The program administrator decides funding eligibility.”

Publish this copy only with verified support contact, hours, response target and escalation path. Those values are absent from this draft; no response-time promise is made. Establish accessible alternatives for parents who cannot use the normal support form.

## Fulfillment procedure to confirm

1. Reconcile the accepted order to the exact student license, quantity, amount, category and term. A parent-generated quote or reported submission is not an administrator order.
2. Reject unsupported, duplicate, mismatched or expired requests according to the actual marketplace contract. Record the reason without disclosing private family data.
3. Apply the agreed payment/credit activation policy. Record order approval, fulfillment, access activation and settlement as separate events.
4. Deliver activation instructions through the approved parent channel. Record the exact downloadable edition and delivery evidence if PDFs are purchased.
5. Reconcile order and settlement references; report delivery failure and follow the agreed rejection/cancellation process. Do not fabricate physical tracking numbers.

The software contains order/grant and quote mechanisms; a live MyScholarShop order adapter and its contract-specific delivery evidence are not established by this packet.

## Refund and withdrawal decisions

Before an offer is published, settle digital-product eligibility, cancellation windows, partial-use/proration, failed-delivery remedies, tax treatment, refund destination, processing deadlines and the effect on access/download rights. The administrator-directed credit process must be confirmed; do not promise a cash refund to a parent from scholarship funds. Preserve financial reconciliation records while applying the reviewed privacy/deletion policy to learning data. Consent withdrawal and a financial refund are distinct events.

## Test-order acceptance evidence

Use administrator-authorized sandbox or test orders only. Record exact request/response fixtures and expected outcomes for acceptance, rejection, duplicate replay, altered replay, amount/term mismatch, delivery failure, cancellation, partial/full credit and settlement reconciliation. Tests must prove that a parent submission alone never activates access. These drafts contain no real orders or payment instruments.
