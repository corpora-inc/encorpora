# How to make a lesson film

Design a lesson around what a learner must understand and be able to do. Automated checks establish technical properties; an independent review establishes whether the explanation teaches clearly.

## Read the standard and the problems

Read the relevant standards from their official source and inspect the app's generated variants with `npx tsx film/facts.ts <id>`. Identify prerequisites, the requested skill and any mismatch between the standard and the problems.

## Design the explanation

Choose the idea that makes the problem understandable, the picture a learner could sketch, and a realistic misconception to address. State what each example adds. For multiple-choice questions, include the choices and explain useful distinctions without repeating equivalent work.

## Build the foundations

Before solving, define the vocabulary, quantities and notation. Construct unfamiliar representations in front of the learner: build a line plot from measurements or cut a whole into tenths. Explain what the representation shows and what it is for. Use words appropriate to the learner's grade.

## Choose and build the picture

Choose the clearest visualization before selecting kit components. Extend the kit when the explanation needs a missing tool. Label quantities and units; preserve scale when size represents amount. Column arithmetic can be its own picture.

## Work each example fully

State the problem and its givens, work it through, and identify the answer. Add another example only when it introduces a distinct case or misconception. Different story structures need their own reasoning, even when grouped under one problem type.

## Pace the ideas

Give one new idea per beat. Let motions finish and leave text visible long enough to read. Dense material needs pauses and explicit connections. Keep every sentence useful without imposing a duration quota.

## Review the complete lesson

Inspect every storyboard frame and watch the voiced lesson, including phone playback. Review definitions, arithmetic, timing, label placement, choices and the match between speech and picture. Use a reviewer independent of the author. Keep review grades and personal notes outside the public repository.

Record reusable improvements in [KAIZEN.md](KAIZEN.md). Validate one representative lesson through the complete process before producing a family in bulk.

## Responsibilities

Family design establishes the explanation and required tools. Kit development implements and tests those tools. Screenplay authoring turns the design into beats. Independent review checks mathematical truth and learner understanding. Assign work by demonstrated quality on representative examples.
