# Improving lesson films

Use review findings to improve explanations, tools and the production process. This document records reusable teaching decisions. Individual grades, quotations and private review notes belong outside the repository.

## Explanation and scope

- Define the representation and vocabulary before solving. Explain what a chart measures, how it is built and what question it answers.
- Teach different story structures from their meaning. Do not force unrelated problems into one procedure.
- Work each example fully on its own screen. A second example should add a distinct case; answers appended to a finished problem do not teach that case.
- Include valid boundary cases, such as subtracting a number from itself or taking the square root of zero.
- Use realistic misconceptions. A warning must explain an error learners could plausibly make.
- Include answer choices when the problem asks the learner to choose, and explain the distinctions that matter.

## Pictures and tools

- Place-value pieces must have consistent scale and explicit units. A flat, rod and small square represent quantities relative to a named whole.
- Show column arithmetic with aligned place values, visible carries or borrows, and changes timed to the explanation.
- Build data displays from their observations. Spoken scales must match tick labels.
- Connect a sine wave to the height of a point moving around a circle before introducing amplitude and period rules.
- Connect conditional probability to a restricted row, column or group. A proportional mosaic can show independence as matching shares.
- Use directed segments for complex-number addition and rotation. Use a bar with aligned amount and percent scales for percent questions.
- Counters can stand for many objects; explain what is being counted.
- Keep labels inside their cards and clear of axes, numbers and other labels. Extend layout tools when a design exposes a recurring collision.

## Pacing

Use one idea per beat, plain language before formal notation, and a pause after a new connection. Let motion finish before the explanation advances. Warning cards need reading time. Viewer speed controls supplement a patient default.

For a type with several distinct skills, introduce each skill's reason immediately before its example. Consider separate lessons when combining those skills obscures the explanation. Estimate spoken length during authoring, then evaluate the actual pacing.

## Production improvement

Validate one lesson from concept through playback before expanding a family. A successful example does not establish that bulk production will meet the same standard. Keep the design, kit and independent review strong at every scale.

Treat family tool specifications as proposals until implemented and tested. Compare alternative authoring approaches on representative lessons with the same brief and independent review. Mathematical checks and layout checks support the review; they cannot establish teaching quality by themselves.
