# Balance and Change

An original illustrated algebra book and workbook for learners who can calculate with signed numbers, fractions and decimals. It develops selected grades 6–8 ideas and an introduction to linear equations and systems from Algebra I.

The route moves from expression structure and equality to paired quantities, functions, slope, linear models and intersections. Entry probes, worked reasoning, error analysis, graph construction, mixed retrieval and open design tasks give learners several ways to explain the same idea. Reasoned keys include acceptable alternatives for open tasks. A parent or teacher reviews written explanations and drawings; automatic practice does not grade them.

Physical balance pictures use nonnegative quantities. Signed equations use symbols rather than negative physical masses. Graphs distinguish unbounded lines from closed model domains, and whole-item contexts distinguish integer inputs from real-valued intersections. Tables preserve repeated inputs when teaching the function definition.

`book.ts` assembles the authored chapters. `alignments.ts` identifies selected standards, skills, pages and tasks with explicit limitations. `practice.ts` connects eight existing deterministic families to instruction and distinguishes numeric results from single-choice selections. A connection does not establish complete standard coverage or mastery. Reflection activities ask learners to select and repair their own work; they are not scored standards assessments.

The volume does not cover all Algebra I topics: inequalities, exponent laws, polynomial operations, quadratics, exponential functions, radicals and statistical modeling need additional instruction. Selected linear examples do not replace a complete treatment of each mapped standard.

Standards references follow the official Common Core requirements for [grade 6 expressions and equations](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/6/EE/), [grade 7 expressions and equations](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/7/EE/), [grade 8 expressions and equations](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/8/EE/), [grade 8 functions](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/8/F/) and [high-school reasoning with equations](https://www.thecorestandards.org/Math/Content/HSA/REI/). Crosswalk identifiers and source provenance are validated against the curriculum catalog; the prose, tasks and illustrations are original.

The app reader advertises `algebra-models-v1` before opening this volume. Older readers receive a refresh message. Downloaded PDFs do not require an app reader capability. The publisher validates exact diagram data and fails on page or label overflow rather than shrinking all text to fit.
