# The Fraction Field Guide

An original illustrated unit connecting fraction meaning, equivalent names, comparison, addition, subtraction, multiplication, and division. Its field-station investigations use measurement, sharing, gardens, and supply planning to connect words, pictures, and equations. Mixed workshops, delayed retrieval, open investigations, and detailed solutions support independent practice and discussion.

The intended audience is learners exploring fractions, typically grades 3–6, who can work with whole-number equal groups. The opening route and entry tasks help select a starting point. Reading demands and the later rate/constraint investigations may call for adult discussion. A learner may enter a familiar section directly and use earlier explanations when useful.

`book.ts` exports `fractionFieldGuide`. The source contains 41 instructional, workshop, reflection, and guide compositions plus solution compositions. All 85 tasks have a linked answer and explanation. The 21 exact semantic diagrams cover strips, number lines, area intersections, grouping, and proportional paired lines. The schema uses rational number pairs to preserve mathematical values across rendering formats. `alignments.ts` supplies 19 bounded editorial links to actual curriculum skills and related runtime templates, distinguishing taught, practiced, and reference content. Missing identifiers and mismatched template skills fail validation. These links do not award mastery or imply that a numeric template assesses written reasoning. Authoring details and the complete page plan are in [design.md](design.md).

This is a focused unit, not complete certification of fraction standards or a claim that completing its pages establishes mastery. The unit does not comprehensively teach decimal conversions, fraction probability, signed rational arithmetic, algebraic fractions, or all grade-specific standards and practices. The content coverage manifest and independent review evidence determine publication status; the source alone does not establish either approval or availability in a learner account.

Run the authoring checks with `npx tsx --test books/fraction-field-guide/book.test.ts` from the AMP directory. They verify task/solution linkage, identifier uniqueness, exact model bounds, proportional pairs, and critical grouping/sharing representations. They do not replace independent mathematical and instructional review or visual inspection of the exported pages.
