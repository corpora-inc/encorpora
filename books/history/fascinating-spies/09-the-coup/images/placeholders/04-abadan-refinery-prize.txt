Subject: The Abadan oil refinery complex in the early 1950s
Details: The world's largest oil refinery as it appeared before nationalization. Sprawling industrial infrastructure -- distillation columns, storage tanks, pipelines, loading docks along the Shatt al-Arab waterway. Ships at anchor. In the foreground, the contrast between the British residential quarter (manicured gardens, bungalows) and the Iranian workers' district. Smoke rises from refinery stacks. The scale of the complex should convey why it was worth fighting over.
