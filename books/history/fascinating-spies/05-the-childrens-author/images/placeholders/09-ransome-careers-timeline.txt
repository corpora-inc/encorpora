Style: Crosshatch pen illustration, diagrammatic, sepia/earth tones
Subject: A visual timeline of Arthur Ransome's three careers -- journalist, intelligence asset, children's author
Details: A horizontal timeline spanning 1884-1967. Three overlapping bands represent the three careers: journalism (1913-1924), intelligence connections (1917-c.1924, with question marks extending later), and literary career (1930-1947 for the Swallows series). Key events are marked: arrival in Russia (1913), revolution (1917), diamond courier run (1919), marriage to Evgenia (1924), Swallows and Amazons published (1930), Carnegie Medal (1936), MI5 files released (2005, marked posthumously). The overlap zone of all three careers (1917-1924) is emphasized with denser cross-hatching.
