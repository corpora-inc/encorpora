Detailed map illustration of the Eastern Mediterranean circa 1250 BCE showing the major Bronze Age powers: Egypt (New Kingdom), Hittite Empire in Anatolia, Mycenaean Greece, Cyprus (Alashiya), and the Levantine city-states including Ugarit, Byblos, Sidon, and Tyre. Major trade routes shown connecting tin from Afghanistan, copper from Cyprus, gold from Egypt. Warm parchment tones, clean cartographic style, scholarly illustration.
