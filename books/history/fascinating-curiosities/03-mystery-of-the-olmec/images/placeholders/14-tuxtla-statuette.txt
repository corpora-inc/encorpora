The Tuxtla Statuette — a small jade figurine about 16 cm
tall, depicting a figure wearing a duck-billed or beak-shaped
mask and elaborate costume. A column of carved glyphs runs
down the front and sides of the figure. The glyphs are small
and intricate, arranged vertically. The jade has a polished,
greenish surface. The figure stands upright with arms at its
sides.
Crosshatch pen-and-ink illustration style. No text or captions.
