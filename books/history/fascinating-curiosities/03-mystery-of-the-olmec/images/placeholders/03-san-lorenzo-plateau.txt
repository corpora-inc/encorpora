The San Lorenzo plateau rising above flat, green lowlands.
A modified natural ridge about fifty meters above the
surrounding floodplain. On top: terraced platforms and plazas
with stone monuments visible. Tropical forest surrounds the
base. A river (the Coatzacoalcos) visible in the background.
The plateau appears partly natural, partly shaped by human
hands — a massive earth-moving project.
