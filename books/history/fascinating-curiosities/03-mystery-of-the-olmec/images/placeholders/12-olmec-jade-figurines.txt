A group of Olmec jade figurines in the characteristic
"baby-face" style — figures with rounded, infantile faces,
elongated heads, and polished green jade surfaces. Several
figurines of varying sizes, some standing, some seated. The
largest perhaps 20 cm tall. The jade ranges from pale green
to deep emerald. Displayed together as a collection, showing
the consistency of the Olmec jade-carving tradition.
Crosshatch pen-and-ink illustration style. No text or captions.
