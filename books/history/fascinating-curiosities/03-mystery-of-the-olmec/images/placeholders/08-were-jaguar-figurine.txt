An Olmec were-jaguar figurine carved from jade. The figure
has a human infant's body and posture but a jaguar's face —
a snarling downturned mouth with prominent fangs, a cleft
forehead, and almond-shaped eyes. The surface is polished and
greenish. The figure sits in a characteristic Olmec pose. The
scale is small — perhaps 15 cm tall. Displayed against a
neutral background.
