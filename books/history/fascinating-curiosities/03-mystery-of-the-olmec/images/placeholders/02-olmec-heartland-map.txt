A map of the Gulf Coast lowlands of Mexico showing the Olmec
heartland. The states of Veracruz and Tabasco visible. Three
major sites marked: San Lorenzo (inland, near the Coatzacoalcos
River), La Venta (in coastal swamps of Tabasco), and Tres
Zapotes (at the edge of the Tuxtla Mountains). The Tuxtla
Mountains shown as a volcanic range to the northwest. Rivers
winding through flat lowlands to the Gulf of Mexico. Scale bar
and compass rose included.
Crosshatch pen-and-ink illustration style. No text or captions.
