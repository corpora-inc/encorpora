A scene of Olmec stone transport. A massive basalt boulder
sits on a log raft being floated down a wide, slow river
through tropical lowlands. Dozens of workers on the banks pull
ropes to guide the raft. Dense vegetation on both sides. In
the distance, low mountains (the Tuxtlas) are visible. The
scale of the boulder dwarfs the human figures. Swampy terrain
alongside the river.
Crosshatch pen-and-ink illustration style. No text or captions.
