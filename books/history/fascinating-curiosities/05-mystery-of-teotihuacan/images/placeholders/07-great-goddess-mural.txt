Recreation of the Great Goddess mural from Tepantitla. A
frontal female figure with an enormous headdress from which
streams of water flow downward, carrying seeds, jade beads,
and flowers. Spider imagery woven into the headdress. The
figure's mouth is partially obscured by a nose ornament. Rich,
flowing lines in the water streams. Symmetrical composition.
Crosshatch pen-and-ink illustration style. No text or captions.
