Cutaway reconstruction of a Teotihuacan apartment compound.
The exterior wall is blank and windowless. Inside, rooms open
onto interior courtyards. A small shrine occupies one courtyard.
People engaged in daily activities: cooking, crafting, carrying
water. Drainage channels visible. The walls are plastered and
painted with simple murals. Multiple family units share the space.
Crosshatch pen-and-ink illustration style. No text or captions.
