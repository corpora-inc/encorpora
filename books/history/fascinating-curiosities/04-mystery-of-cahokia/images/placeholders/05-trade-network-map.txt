Map of North America showing Cahokia's trade network. Cahokia
marked at the center, near the confluence of the Mississippi
and Missouri rivers. Lines radiate outward to: Lake Superior
(copper), the Appalachian Mountains (mica), the Gulf of Mexico
(marine shells), and the Rocky Mountains (obsidian). Each source
region labeled with its material. The network spans the entire
continent east of the Rockies.
Crosshatch pen-and-ink illustration style. No text or captions.
