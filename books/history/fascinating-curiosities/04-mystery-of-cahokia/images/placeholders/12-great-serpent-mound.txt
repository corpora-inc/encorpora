The Great Serpent Mound in Ohio, seen from above. A quarter-
mile-long earthen effigy in the shape of a serpent with a
coiled tail, its mouth open around an oval shape (the "egg").
The mound follows the curve of a natural ridge. Trees and
grass surround the effigy. The snake's sinuous curves are
clearly visible from the aerial perspective.
Crosshatch pen-and-ink illustration style. No text or captions.
