Aerial reconstruction view of Cahokia at its peak (~1100 CE).
Monks Mound dominates the center, with a large timber building
on its summit. The Grand Plaza stretches south of the mound,
a flat open space. Dozens of subsidiary mounds visible, arranged
around plazas. Residential areas with thatched-roof houses in
organized rows. A wooden palisade encircles the central
precinct. The Mississippi River visible in the background.
Crosshatch pen-and-ink illustration style. No text or captions.
