# The Grassland Today

The temperate grassland is one of the most important biomes for people.

The deep, rich soil makes it perfect for growing food.

Much of the bread, pasta, and corn that the world eats comes from these lands.

But the cost has been high.

Most of the original grasslands of the world have been plowed up.

The great herds of bison and wild horses are mostly gone.

The little burrowing animals have fewer places to live.

The grass that once stretched to the horizon now grows only in small patches.

In some places, when the grass is gone and the soil is left bare, the wind blows the dark earth away.

Many groups around the world are working to take care of what is left.

They set aside parks where the grass can grow tall again.

They help bison and other animals come back to lands they once filled.

They study the deep soil so we can use it without losing it.

A healthy grassland is one of the most alive places on Earth.

It is worth our care.
