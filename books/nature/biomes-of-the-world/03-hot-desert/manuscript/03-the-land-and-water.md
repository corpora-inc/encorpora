# The Land and the Water

Most of the hot desert is not made of sand.

Most of it is rock, gravel, and hard dry ground.

There are also places with great seas of dunes.

The wind moves the dunes slowly across the land.

A dune can be smaller than a house, or taller than a tall building.

In some places, mountains rise out of the desert.

Wind and sand carve the rocks into strange shapes over many years.

Water is the rarest and most precious thing in the desert.

Most rivers run only after a storm, and then they dry up.

But deep below the sand, water can rest in the rocks.

In some places, this hidden water rises to the surface and makes a small pool of green.

This is called an oasis.

At an oasis, trees grow, birds sing, and people stop to drink.

An oasis can be a small ring of palm trees in a vast sea of sand.

Without these spots of water, no one could cross the great deserts at all.
