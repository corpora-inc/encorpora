# The People

People have lived in the hot desert for thousands of years.

They have found their own ways to live in a land of sun and sand.

In many deserts, families travel from one place to another with their herds.

In the Sahara and the deserts of Arabia, people keep herds of camels, goats, and sheep.

The animals can walk for a long time between drinks of water.

The people follow the animals, and the animals lead them to food.

Their tents are made of cloth woven from the hair of their animals.

A tent can be put up in an hour and taken down in an hour.

It keeps the burning sun out, and lets the cool night air pass through.

The people of the desert wear loose, long clothes.

Long sleeves and a covered head keep the body cool and protect it from the sun.

A bright pattern of cloth helps a friend see you from far away.

Some desert people live in towns built around an oasis.

They grow date palms, fruits, and grains in the green ring around the water.

They have learned to dig deep wells to find the hidden water below the sand.

Travelers from far away come to the oasis to rest and to fill their water bags.

The people of the desert know every star in the night sky.

They know the names of every wind that blows.

They can read the tracks of every animal in the sand.

This knowledge is passed from parents to children, year after year.

In every great desert, this kind of life has its own languages, songs, and stories.
