# The Desert Today

The hot desert is a beautiful and demanding biome.

It covers a large part of the land on Earth.

The strong desert sun is also one of our biggest sources of clean energy.

Great fields of sun-catching panels are being built in many deserts.

They turn the desert sun into electricity for far-away cities.

But the desert is in trouble in some places.

In some areas, the desert is growing, because people have used up the water and the grass at its edges.

The grass dies, the trees go, and the sand moves in.

In other places, the towns at the edge of the desert are using up too much of the hidden water below the ground.

When the deep water is gone, the oases dry up too.

Many groups around the world are working to take care of the deserts.

They plant rows of trees and grasses to hold back the moving sand.

They help the people who live in the desert to use water wisely.

They study the deserts so we can understand them better.

A healthy desert is full of life that you would not expect.

It is worth our care.
