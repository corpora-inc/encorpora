# Where Deserts Are

Welcome to the hot desert.

This is one of the great biomes of the world.

A biome is a part of the Earth with its own kind of weather, plants, and animals.

The hot desert is a dry place.

Very little rain falls here.

Most days are very hot, and most nights are cool or cold.

The biggest hot desert in the world is the Sahara, in northern Africa.

It stretches from the Atlantic Ocean almost all the way to the Red Sea.

There are great deserts in the middle of Arabia, like the Empty Quarter.

There are hot deserts in North America, in places like the southwest of the United States and northern Mexico.

There are smaller deserts in South America, in southern Africa, in India, and across much of Australia.

Each of these deserts grew up on its own, far from the others.

So the plants and animals are different in each part of the world.

A cactus you see in one desert is not the same as one you see in another.

But they are all part of the same biome.

When you stand in a hot desert, the air feels dry.

The sun is strong on your face.

The ground is rocky in many places, and sandy in others.

You can see for a very long way.

The sky is huge.

You may not see another person or another animal for a whole day.

That is the hot desert.

A wide, dry land of sand, rock, and burning sun.
