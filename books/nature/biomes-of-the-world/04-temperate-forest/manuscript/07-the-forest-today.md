# The Forest Today

The temperate forest is one of the most important biomes for people.

Most of the great cities of the world stand in places that were once temperate forest.

The forests give us wood for building, paper for writing, and clean water for drinking.

The trees take in the gas we breathe out and use it to grow.

They give back the gas we breathe in.

In autumn, the forest is one of the most beautiful sights on Earth.

People travel long distances just to see the colored leaves.

But the temperate forest has lost a lot of its land.

Over thousands of years, much of the original forest was cut down.

Where there were once endless trees, there are now fields, towns, roads, and cities.

The forests that remain are smaller and broken up.

Many groups around the world are working to take care of what is left.

They plant new trees where old ones were lost.

They protect old forests from being cut down.

They study the forest so we can understand it better.

A young forest can grow back in a hundred years.

An old forest can hold living trees that are older than any building near it.

It is worth our care.
