# The Weather

The weather in the tropical rainforest is simple.

It is warm, and it rains a lot.

The temperature stays about the same all year.

It does not get cold in winter.

It does not get very hot in summer.

Most days are warm and steady.

It rains almost every day.

Some places get more than two meters of rain in a year.

That is more rain than most parts of the world ever see.

The rain often comes in the afternoon.

Big clouds gather above the trees.

A heavy shower falls for an hour or two.

Then the sun comes back out.

The leaves shine, and steam rises from the ground.

There are no four seasons here.

There is only a wetter time and a drier time.

Even in the drier time, it still rains often.

For the plants and animals of the rainforest, every day feels much the same.
