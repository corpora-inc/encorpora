# The Rainforest Today

The tropical rainforest is one of the most important biomes on Earth.

The trees pull water up from the ground and breathe it into the sky.

The clouds that form above the forest carry rain to lands far away.

The trees breathe too, but the other way around from us.

They take in the gas we breathe out, and they use it to grow.

And they give back the gas we breathe in.

But the rainforest is in trouble.

In many places, people have cut down the trees to plant crops or to raise animals.

When the trees are gone, the soil washes away, and the rain stops falling the way it used to.

The animals and people who live there lose their home.

Many groups around the world are working to protect what is left.

They plant new trees.

They help the people who live in the forest to look after it.

They study the forest so we understand it better.

A rainforest takes hundreds of years to grow.

But a healthy rainforest can keep growing for thousands of years.

It is worth our care.
