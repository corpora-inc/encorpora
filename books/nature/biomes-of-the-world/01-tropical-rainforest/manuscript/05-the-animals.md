# The Animals

More kinds of animals live in the tropical rainforest than anywhere else on land.

Most of them live up in the trees, not on the ground.

There is more food and more light up high.

Monkeys live in many rainforests.

They jump from branch to branch and call to each other across the trees.

In the rainforests of South America, some monkeys have long tails they use like an extra hand.

Parrots and other bright birds fly between the high branches.

They eat fruit and seeds.

Their feathers are red, green, blue, and yellow.

In many rainforests, big cats hunt on the ground at night.

In the Americas, this cat is the jaguar.

In Asia, it is the tiger.

In Africa, it is the leopard.

All of them are strong and quiet, with spots or stripes on their fur.

Snakes slide through the leaves and the trees.

Some snakes are very small, and some are very long.

Most of them are shy and stay away from people.

Frogs sing every night, especially after the rain.

Some frogs are tiny and bright.

Bright colors warn other animals not to eat them.

Insects fill every corner of the forest.

There are more insects in the rainforest than any other kind of animal.

Ants march in long lines across the forest floor.

Beetles climb the trunks.

Butterflies float through the air on wide, painted wings.

Fish live in the rivers, in many shapes and sizes.

Some fish are tiny and silver, and some are bigger than a child.

High above it all, eagles glide over the green roof of the forest.

They watch for movement below.

Bats fly out at sunset to eat fruit and insects.

In one rainforest tree, you might find more kinds of animals than in a whole town somewhere else.

That is the wonder of this biome.
