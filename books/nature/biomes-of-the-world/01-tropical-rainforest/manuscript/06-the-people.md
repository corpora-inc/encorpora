# The People

People have lived in the tropical rainforest for a very long time.

They have learned how to live well with the trees, the rain, and the animals.

Many people of the rainforest live in small villages.

The houses are often built on tall wooden posts to keep them above the wet ground.

The roofs are made of leaves laid in thick rows.

A good leaf roof keeps the heavy rain out.

The walls are often open to let the warm air pass through.

The people fish in the rivers and gather food from the forest.

They hunt small animals with bows and spears.

They grow food in small gardens cleared from the trees.

What they grow depends on where they live.

In the rainforests of South America, people have grown cassava and sweet potatoes for thousands of years.

In the rainforests of Africa, people have grown yams and palm fruit.

In the rainforests of Southeast Asia and the Pacific, people have grown taro, rice, and breadfruit.

After a few years they let the garden grow back into forest, and they make a new one somewhere else.

This is how the forest stays healthy.

The people of the rainforest know thousands of plants.

They know which ones give food, which ones give medicine, and which ones are not safe to eat.

They learn the songs of the birds.

They learn the tracks of the animals.

This knowledge is shared from parents to children, year after year.

In every rainforest, this kind of life has its own languages, its own stories, and its own music.
