# Where This Biome Grows

Welcome to the tundra.

This is one of the great biomes of the world.

A biome is a part of the Earth with its own kind of weather, plants, and animals.

The tundra is a cold, open land where it is too cold for trees to grow.

The word tundra comes from an old word that means a treeless plain.

The tundra is the coldest biome on land.

Most of the tundra lies in a wide ring around the top of the world.

It covers the northern edges of Canada, Alaska, and Greenland, in North America.

It covers the far north of Norway, Sweden, and Finland, in northern Europe.

It covers the long northern coast of Russia, across the top of Asia.

This cold ring of land sits north of the boreal forest and around the frozen sea.

There is another kind of tundra as well.

High on tall mountains, above the last trees, the same cold and open land appears.

This mountain tundra is found on high peaks all over the world, even near the warm middle of the Earth.

Wherever it grows, the tundra is a land of low plants, cold wind, and wide open sky.

There are no forests here.

The ground is frozen hard for most of the year.

But even in this cold place, life finds a way.
