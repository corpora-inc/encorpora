# Closing

That is the tundra.

A cold, open, treeless land of frozen ground, low plants, and wide bright sky at the top of the world.

If you ever stand in one, be still for a minute.

Feel the cold wind moving across the open land.

Look down at the tiny flowers and soft moss under your feet.

Look up at the huge and empty sky.

You will feel the quiet, patient heart of the biome beating around you.
