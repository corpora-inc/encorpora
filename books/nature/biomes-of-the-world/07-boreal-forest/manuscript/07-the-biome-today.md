# The Biome Today

The boreal forest is huge, but it is not as untouched as it looks.

For more than a hundred years, people have cut large amounts of wood from this forest.

The tall straight trees make good lumber and good paper.

In many parts of the boreal forest, whole sections of trees have been cut down and slowly grown back.

The boreal forest also holds an enormous amount of carbon, in the trees and in the soil and bogs below.

Healthy boreal soil keeps that carbon locked safely away from the air.

When the trees and soil are kept whole, the forest helps keep the air of the whole Earth in balance.

In recent years, the boreal forest has grown warmer.

Long warm summers have brought larger forest fires than the people of the north remember.

Some of these fires now last for weeks and burn through millions of trees.

In the far north, the warmer summers have begun to soften the permafrost.

Many people and groups today are working to take care of the boreal forest.

They set aside large parks where the forest can grow as it always has.

They help the people who have lived there for many generations care for their own lands.

They study the forest to understand how it lives and how to keep it whole.

A healthy boreal forest is a great green blanket that helps keep the world cool.

It is worth our care.
