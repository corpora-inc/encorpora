# Closing

That is the boreal forest.

A wide green belt of evergreen trees, lakes, and quiet snow that runs almost all the way around the top of the world.

If you ever stand in one, be still for a minute.

Listen to the wind in the high needles.

Watch the sun coming through the trees onto the moss.

Smell the cold pine air.

You will feel the slow, patient heart of the biome beating around you.
