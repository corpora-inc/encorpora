# Where This Biome Grows

Welcome to the boreal forest.

This is one of the great biomes of the world.

A biome is a part of the Earth with its own kind of weather, plants, and animals.

The boreal forest is a wide green belt of evergreen trees that runs across the top of the world.

It is also called the taiga.

The boreal forest is the largest land biome on Earth.

If you flew high above the world, you would see this dark green belt stretching almost all the way around.

It grows across most of Canada and Alaska, in North America.

It grows across Norway, Sweden, and Finland, in northern Europe.

It grows across Russia, all the way from Europe to the Pacific Ocean.

In each of these places, the forest looks very much the same.

Tall pointed trees stand close together, with soft moss and small bushes on the ground.

There are many lakes, rivers, and bogs between the trees.

The boreal forest sits just south of the tundra and just north of the temperate forests.

It is not a warm place.

Long, very cold winters are the rule here, with deep snow on the ground for many months.

The summers are short and cool, with long days and bright nights.

Few people live in the boreal forest, but it is full of life.

It is home to bears, wolves, moose, owls, and many other animals.

And it grows quietly across more land than any other forest on Earth.
