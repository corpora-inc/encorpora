# The Biome Today

The Mediterranean biome is small but very important.

Although it covers only a small part of the Earth, it holds a huge number of plants and animals found nowhere else.

For people, this biome has been the cradle of food and farming for thousands of years.

But the biome is in trouble in many places.

Cities and towns have grown along the coasts where the weather is mild and the sea is near.

Roads, houses, hotels, and farms have replaced many of the wild bushes and trees.

Long dry summers, hotter than before, are making the fires bigger and more dangerous.

Some animals, like the lynx and the Mediterranean tortoise, have become very rare.

Many groups around the world are working to take care of what is left.

They set aside parks where the wild plants and animals can still live.

They plant new olive trees and protect old ones.

They help farmers grow food in ways that are kind to the land.

They study the biome so we can understand it better.

A healthy Mediterranean biome is a quiet, fragrant, beautiful place.

It is worth our care.
