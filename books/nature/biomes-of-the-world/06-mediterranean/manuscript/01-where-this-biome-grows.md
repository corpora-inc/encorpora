# Where This Biome Grows

Welcome to the Mediterranean biome.

This is one of the great biomes of the world.

A biome is a part of the Earth with its own kind of weather, plants, and animals.

The Mediterranean biome is named for the lands around the Mediterranean Sea.

But it does not only grow there.

The Mediterranean biome appears in five small parts of the world.

It grows in the lands around the Mediterranean Sea, in southern Europe and along the coast of North Africa.

It grows in California, in the western part of North America.

It grows along the middle of the coast of Chile, in South America.

It grows near the southern tip of Africa, around the Cape.

It grows in the southwest and southern parts of Australia.

Each of these places is far from the others.

But they all share the same special weather.

The summers are hot and dry, and the winters are mild and wet.

That is rare on Earth.

Most biomes have their rainy time in the warm months.

Here, the rain falls in the cool months instead.

Each of these five places has its own plants and animals.

But the shapes are the same.

Short tough shrubs, scattered trees, fragrant herbs, and bare rocky hills.

When you stand in a Mediterranean land in summer, the air smells of dry herbs and warm stone.

The light is strong and golden.

The hills are dotted with low silver-green bushes.

That is the Mediterranean biome.

A bright, dry, fragrant world that grows in five small corners of the Earth.
