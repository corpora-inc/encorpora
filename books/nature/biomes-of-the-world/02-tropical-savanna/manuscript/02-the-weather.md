# The Weather

The weather in the tropical savanna is simple.

It is warm all year.

But unlike the rainforest, the savanna has two very different seasons.

There is a long dry time and a shorter wet time.

In the dry time, the sky is clear for many months.

The grass turns golden and brittle.

The rivers shrink, and many small ponds dry up completely.

The trees lose their leaves to save water.

The wet time begins with great clouds piling up on the horizon.

The first rains fall in heavy storms with thunder and lightning.

When the rain comes, the savanna changes overnight.

New grass shoots up from the dry earth.

Trees grow fresh green leaves.

Flowers bloom in waves of color.

The wet time is short, but it is when most of the life happens.

The dry time is long, but the savanna is built for it.

Every plant and every animal here knows how to wait for the rain.
