# Where Savannas Grow

Welcome to the tropical savanna.

This is one of the great biomes of the world.

A biome is a part of the Earth with its own kind of weather, plants, and animals.

The savanna is a wide grassland with scattered trees.

It is warm all year.

It has one long dry time and one shorter wet time.

The biggest savannas in the world are in Africa.

They stretch across much of the middle of the continent.

There are large savannas in South America too, called the cerrado.

There are tropical savannas across the north of Australia.

There are smaller savannas in India and in Southeast Asia.

Each of these grew up on its own, far from the others.

So the plants and animals are different in each part of the world.

A grazing animal in Africa is not the same as one in South America or Australia.

But they are all part of the same biome.

When you stand in a savanna, you can see for a long way.

The grass reaches your waist or higher.

A few flat-topped trees rise above the grass.

The sky is huge.

The light is golden.

You hear the wind moving through the grass.

You hear birds calling in the distance.

That is the savanna.

A great open land of grass, scattered trees, and big skies.
