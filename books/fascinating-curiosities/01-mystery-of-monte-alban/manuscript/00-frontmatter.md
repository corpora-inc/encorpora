# A Note to the Reader

This book is about a real mystery. Not the kind where someone hides the answer at the back. The kind where nobody has the answer yet.

About 2,500 years ago, people in southern Mexico flattened the top of a mountain and built a city on it. They carved hundreds of stone figures. They invented one of the oldest writing systems in the Americas. Their city thrived for over a thousand years. Then they walked away from it, and nobody knows exactly why. Their writing system died — not because invaders destroyed it, but centuries before any invader arrived. The language survived. The script did not.

We are going to look at what we know, what the best scholars in the world think they know, and where the boundary is. When something is well-established, we will say so. When experts disagree, we will show you both sides and let you decide. When we genuinely do not know — and there is a lot we genuinely do not know — we will say that too.

The mystery is not a problem to be solved and forgotten. It is an invitation to pay attention.
