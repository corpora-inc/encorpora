Reconstructed Woodhenge at Cahokia. Tall red cedar posts
arranged in a large circle, each post about six meters tall.
The sun rises on the horizon between two of the posts, aligned
to a solstice position. An observation post stands at the
center. Early morning light. Monks Mound visible in the
background to the east. A figure stands at the center post,
observing the sunrise.
Crosshatch pen-and-ink illustration style. No text or captions.
