Detail of the carved facade of the Temple of the Feathered
Serpent. Alternating projecting stone heads: feathered serpents
with goggle-eyed rain deity faces between them. The serpent
heads are surrounded by carved feathers and shells. The
talud-tablero architectural profile is visible. The carving is
deep and dramatic, with strong shadows.
Crosshatch pen-and-ink illustration style. No text or captions.
