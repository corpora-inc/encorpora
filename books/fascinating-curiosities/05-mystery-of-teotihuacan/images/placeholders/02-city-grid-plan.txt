Bird's-eye plan view of Teotihuacan showing the city layout.
The Avenue of the Dead runs north-south through the center.
The Pyramid of the Sun on the east side, Pyramid of the Moon
at the north end, the Ciudadela compound at the south. A grid
of apartment compounds extends in all directions. The San Juan
River crosses the city. Scale markers suggest the vast extent.
