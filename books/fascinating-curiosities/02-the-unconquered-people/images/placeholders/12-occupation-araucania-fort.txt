A Chilean military fort of the 1870s-1880s being constructed
on a hilltop in Araucanía. Soldiers in 19th-century uniforms
(kepis, dark jackets) working on earthworks and timber walls.
Telegraph wires stretching to the horizon on wooden poles.
Dense forested Mapuche territory stretching below and beyond
the fort in every direction. The fort small against the
immensity of the land it claims to control.
