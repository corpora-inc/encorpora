La Venta's Great Pyramid — an earthen mound roughly thirty
meters tall, with a conical shape and possible fluted ridges
running down its sides. The pyramid rises from a narrow ridge
of land surrounded by swampy lowlands. A long ceremonial
corridor stretches northward from the pyramid through aligned
plazas and platforms. Tropical vegetation encroaches on the
edges.
