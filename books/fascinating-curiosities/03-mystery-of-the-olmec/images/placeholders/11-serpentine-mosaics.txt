One of the buried serpentine mosaic pavements from La Venta.
Hundreds of rectangular blocks of greenish serpentine laid
in a rectangular pit, forming an abstract pattern resembling
a stylized face or mask — a broad, rectangular design with
geometric features. Layers of colored clay visible around the
edges. The mosaic is being uncovered by archaeologists, showing
the transition from buried state to revealed pattern.
