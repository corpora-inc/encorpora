A side-by-side comparison showing the transformation from
Olmec throne to colossal head. Left: a massive basalt throne
(sometimes called an altar) showing a seated figure emerging
from a niche in the front. Right: the same block recarved into
a colossal head — the throne's mass reshaped into a helmeted
portrait face. Dotted lines suggest the original throne shape
within the head's contours.
