# Your Mind

The strongest muscle a striker has is the mind. Your feet score the goals. Your mind creates the goals. The way you think during a game shapes the way you play. A striker with a strong mind plays with confidence, patience, and hunger.

## Missing Happens

Every striker misses shots. Every striker in the history of soccer has sent a shot wide, hit the crossbar, or had the goalkeeper make a great save. Missing is a normal part of being a striker. Missing is not failure. Missing is part of the process.

The best strikers forget the miss and look for the next chance. A missed shot is already in the past. You cannot change the past. You can control the next moment. The next ball might fall to your feet. The next run might put you through on goal. Stay ready for what comes next.

A striker who dwells on a miss plays scared. A striker who forgets a miss plays free. Let the miss go. The next chance is coming.

## Stay Hungry

Your goal can come at any moment. The sixty-seventh minute is just as important as the seventh minute. Stay sharp for the whole game. Stay alert for the whole game. The striker who loses focus in the second half misses the chance that wins the game.

Keep making runs even when the runs do not produce chances. Keep finding space even when the ball does not come your way. Keep pressing even when your legs feel heavy.

A hungry striker never stops looking for the goal. Every restart, every goal kick, every throw-in is a new opportunity. The next chance is yours.

## Confidence

Confidence means believing you will score before the chance arrives. Confidence means running at a defender without hesitation. Confidence means shooting even when the shooting angle is tight. A tight shooting angle means you are off to one side of the goal, near the corner or the sideline. From that position, you can only see a thin sliver of the net. A wider shooting angle, from the center of the field, shows you the full goal. Confident strikers score from tight angles because confident strikers trust their ability to find that sliver.

Confidence comes from practice. The more you practice your finishing, the more confident you feel in a game. The more confident you feel, the calmer you are when the chance comes. Calm strikers make better decisions.

You deserve to feel confident. You have practiced your shooting. You have worked on your first touch. You have earned your place in the attacking line. Trust the work you have put in.

## Patience

Not every game will bring you a goal. Some games, the other team defends well and chances are scarce. Some games, the ball does not bounce your way. That is okay. A patient striker waits for the right moment instead of forcing a chance that is not there.

Patience means trusting the process. Keep doing your job. Keep finding space. Keep making runs. Keep pressing the defense. The chances will come. The goals will come. A patient striker scores goals over a whole season, not just in one game.

## The Team Goal

A goal belongs to the whole team. The goalkeeper made a save to start the counter-attack. The sweeper sent the perfect ball forward. The defender won a tackle in midfield. You scored, but every teammate played a part.

Celebrate with your teammates every time. Run to the player who made the assist. A striker who shares credit earns the love and trust of every teammate.

When a teammate scores, celebrate that goal like you scored the goal yourself. The team's success is your success.

## The Joy of Striking

Playing striker is a joy. You get to do the thing every player dreams about. You get to score goals. You get to feel the ball hit the net. You get to hear your teammates cheer.

Carry that joy onto the field every game. Play with a smile. Play with energy. Play with the love of scoring goals that drew you to the striker position in the first place.

You are the striker. Stay hungry. Stay confident. Stay patient. The next goal is coming. Go get the next goal.
