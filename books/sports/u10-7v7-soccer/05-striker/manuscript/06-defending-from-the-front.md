# Defending from the Front

Every player on the team defends. The striker is no exception. The best strikers press, chase, and fight to win the ball back. Defense is not just the defenders' job. Defense is everyone's job.

## Press When They Have the Ball

When the other team has the ball in their own half, you are the first line of defense. Run toward the player on the ball. Close the space. Force the player to rush a pass or make a mistake.

You do not have to win the ball every time you press. Pressing is about creating pressure. A goalkeeper who feels a striker closing in will rush a kick. A defender who sees a striker sprinting toward the ball will play a bad pass. Your pressure forces mistakes. Mistakes create scoring chances for your team.

## Close Down Passing Lanes

Smart pressing means closing down the easiest passing lane. Your pressing angle is the direction you run at the ball carrier. If you run straight at the ball carrier from the front, every passing lane stays open. But if you curve your run to one side, your body blocks the pass in that direction. For example, if the ball carrier has a teammate to the right, run at the ball carrier from the right side. Your body now stands between the ball carrier and that teammate. The only options left are backward or to the other side. One smart pressing angle can cut off the most dangerous pass on the field.

Pick your moments. Press when the ball carrier has a heavy touch. Press when the ball carrier is facing the wrong direction. Press when the ball carrier has limited options. Save your energy for the moments when pressing can win the ball.

## Chase Back

When your team loses the ball, chase back. Sprint toward your own goal. Get between the ball and your own goal. Your defenders need help. Your sweeper needs help. The striker who chases back shows the whole team that every player fights together.

Chasing back is hard. You want to stay high up the field and wait for the next attacking chance. But a striker who chases back earns the respect of every teammate. Your coach sees your effort. The whole team plays harder when every player commits to defense.

## Win the Ball High

The best thing a striker can do on defense is win the ball high up the field. If you steal the ball near the other team's goal, you are already in a scoring position. A tackle or an interception in the attacking third is the fastest way to create a goal.

Watch the defender on the ball. Wait for a loose touch. Pounce on the mistake. Steal the ball. Now you are one on one with the goalkeeper while the other team scrambles to recover.

Winning the ball high creates counter-attacks that are almost impossible to defend. Suddenly the ball is at your feet and you are running at the goal. The defenders are out of position. These moments produce some of the easiest goals in soccer.

## Track Back to Help

Sometimes the other team builds a long attack. Your defenders are under pressure. Your sweeper is dealing with multiple opponents. In those moments, track back all the way into your own half and help the defense.

You might not make a tackle. Your presence alone changes the numbers. Instead of three attackers against three defenders, the other team now faces three attackers against four defenders. The extra body makes a difference.

When the ball is won back, you are in a perfect position to receive a pass and launch the counter-attack. The field opens up in front of you as you carry the ball forward.

## Energy and Effort

Defending from the front costs energy. You sprint to press. You sprint to chase back. You sprint again when the team wins the ball and attacks. A striker covers a lot of ground during a game.

Manage your energy wisely. Press hard in bursts. Recover during the moments when the ball is far from you. Save your biggest effort for the moments when pressing can make the biggest difference.

The hardest-working striker earns the most chances. Your teammates reward your defensive effort by finding you with the ball in dangerous positions. A striker who defends and attacks is the complete striker.
