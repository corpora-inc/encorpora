# Under 10 Soccer 7v7 — Style Guide

## Voice

Direct, practical, personal. First-person where it helps. Written from real experience coaching real kids, not from a textbook. Plain language the players can read. No motivational cliches. No preaching.

## Sentence Quality

Every sentence must be concise, complete, and able to stand alone as a language-learning utterance. This book will become a Corpan narration pack. Aim for 10-25 words per sentence. Use active voice. Avoid dangling pronouns — no "it" or "this" without a clear antecedent in the same sentence.

## Structure

- Short paragraphs, 3-5 lines. Faster pace than a textbook.
- Lists when they genuinely clarify.
- **Bold** sparingly — the book is short enough to read straight through.
- Blockquotes rarely. One or two for emphasis across the whole book.
- No emoji. No exclamation marks unless quoting speech.

## Formatting

- Markdown with pandoc extensions.
- Chapters start with `#` (H1). Sections use `##`. Subsections use `###`.
- No formation codes. The system is "1-2-3" and nothing else.
- No image placeholders unless a diagram adds clear value.

## Audience

- First-time U10 7v7 coaches.
- Parents reading along to understand what the coach is doing.
- Players age 9-10 who can read the whole thing themselves.
- Everyone reads all of it — the book is short enough.

## Technical Notes

- 7v7 means 6 field players plus a goalkeeper.
- The formation is 1-2-3: one sweeper, two defenders, three attackers.
- No left/right mid, no point, no shape-shifting codes.
- Personality archetypes are anonymized. No real names.
