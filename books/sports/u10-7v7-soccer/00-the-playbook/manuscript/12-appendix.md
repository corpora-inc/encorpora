# Appendix

## Joint Mobility Routine

This routine takes five minutes and should be done before the pairs warm-up on game days. It is optional at practice since the pairs passing warm-up serves a similar purpose, but it is good for players who arrive early and want to get loose.

**Neck circles.** Slow circles in each direction, five each way. Not fast head-snapping — smooth, controlled rotations.

**Arm circles.** Small circles forward for ten, then backward for ten. Then large circles forward for ten, backward for ten. Loosens the shoulders, which matters for throw-ins.

**Hip circles.** Hands on hips, feet shoulder-width apart. Rotate the hips in a large circle, five each direction. This opens the hip flexors, which are the most important joints for kicking and sprinting.

**Leg swings — forward and back.** Hold a teammate's shoulder for balance. Swing one leg forward and back like a pendulum, ten times. Switch legs. Keep the swinging leg straight. This opens the hamstrings and hip flexors dynamically.

**Leg swings — side to side.** Same setup. Swing one leg across the body and out to the side, ten times. Switch legs. This opens the groin and inner thigh.

**Ankle circles.** Lift one foot off the ground. Rotate the ankle in circles, ten each direction. Switch feet. Ankle mobility prevents the most common U10 injury: the rolled ankle.

**Bodyweight squats.** Ten slow squats. Feet shoulder-width apart, toes slightly out, squat until the thighs are parallel to the ground, stand back up. This activates the quads, glutes, and core all at once.

The routine is: neck, arms, hips, leg swings forward, leg swings side, ankles, squats. In that order. Every time. Consistency makes it automatic so the players can do it without instruction.

## Formation Quick Reference

**Defensive Shape: 6-0 — Code: "Sixty"**

|  | Left | Center-Left | Center-Right | Right |
|--|------|-------------|--------------|-------|
| Defensive block | LW | Sweeper | Point | RW |
| Defensive line | LB | | | RB |
| Goal | | | GK | |

Defense comes first. Everyone defends. All six field players in the defensive third. Backs pinch into center with Sweeper — deny the middle, force them wide. Point adds a fourth body in the center. Wing strikers track back. Coiled for counter. On turnover: send it OUT to the sidelines.

**Neutral Shape: 2-1-1-2 — Code: "Twenty-one twelve"**

|  | Left | Center | Right |
|--|------|--------|-------|
| Attacking third | LW | | RW |
| Upper middle | | Point | |
| Lower middle | | Sweeper | |
| Defensive third | LB | | RB |
| Goal | | GK | |

Default shape. Two interlocking triangles (back: LB-RB-Sweeper; front: Sweeper-LW-RW with Point). Used at kickoffs, after goals, and during general open play.

**Attacking Shape: 2-1-3 — Code: "Two-thirteen"**

|  | Left | Center | Right |
|--|------|--------|-------|
| Attacking line | LW | Point | RW |
| Holding mid | | Sweeper | |
| Defensive third | LB | | RB |
| Goal | | GK | |

Used when your team has sustained possession in the opponent's half. Wing strikers push high — completely opportunistic, going wherever the probability of scoring is highest. Point central and high. Sweeper holds as safety valve.

**Transition triggers:**

- Opponent has ball in your half: everyone gets back into the 6-0
- Your team has sustained possession in opponent's half: shift to 2-1-3
- Turnover (we win the ball): wing strikers and point explode forward for counter
- Turnover: nearest player presses, everyone else goalside

## Set-Piece Cheat Sheet

### Offensive Kickoff
- Point taps to Sweeper
- Sweeper distributes wide or builds centrally
- Team in neutral 2-1-1-2 shape

### Defensive Kickoff
- Point and Sweeper press at center circle
- Wing strikers, backs, GK hold neutral shape
- Do not overcommit — only the two forwards press

### Offensive Corner
- Kicker: wing on the side of the corner
- Near post: Point
- Back post / penalty spot: Sweeper
- Top of box: opposite Wing
- Stay back: LB and RB at halfway line

### Defensive Corner
- GK centered in goal, yard off line
- Point and Sweeper mark two most dangerous opponents
- Wing strikers on posts (one near, one far)
- Backs at edge of six-yard box
- Clear it OUT — always to the sideline

### Goal Kick (with build-out line)
- LB and RB position near sidelines in defensive third
- Sweeper drops to center as secondary option
- GK plays short to a back near the sideline — NOT through the center

### Goal Kick (without build-out line)
- Backs check toward GK near the sidelines
- If pressed, GK kicks long down the sideline — NOT to the center
- Wing strikers chase the long ball with the touchline protecting one side

### Throw-Ins (attacking half)
- One player checks short, one runs up the line
- Short throw to feet preferred
- Always throw forward to maintain territory

### Throw-Ins (defensive half)
- Priority: keep possession safely
- Short throw to nearest teammate facing the field
- If short is covered, throw down the line

### Free Kicks (attacking, near goal)
- Best dead-ball shooter takes it
- Aim low, to the corner
- Point near goal for rebound, Sweeper at edge of box

### Free Kicks (defending)
- Two-player wall covering near post
- GK covers far post
- Rest of team marks up in box

### Penalty Kick (taking)
- Pick a side before approaching
- Inside of the foot, firm, to the corner
- Do not change your mind

### Penalty Kick (defending)
- GK stays centered on line
- React to the ball, do not guess
- Dive after the ball is struck
