## Chapter 3: The Formation (~12 pages)
- Why formation matters at U10
- Six positions, not zones (LB, RB, Sweeper, Point, LW, RW — no left/right mid)
- The three shapes: defensive first, then neutral, then attacking
- Defensive shape (6-0): everyone defends, coiled for counter — presented first
- Neutral shape (2-1-1-2): two interlocking triangles, the default
- Attacking shape (2-1-3): wing strikers and point high, sweeper holds
- Transitions between shapes (6-0 to counter-attack is the signature play)
- Context-driven shifting: reading the game
- The build-out line (with and without)
- Diagrams: neutral, defensive, attacking shapes
