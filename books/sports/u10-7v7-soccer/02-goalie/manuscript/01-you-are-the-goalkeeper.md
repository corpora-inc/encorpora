# You Are the Goalkeeper

You are the goalkeeper. You are the last player between the ball and the net. Every teammate on the field trusts you to protect the goal. That trust is something you earn every single game.

You are the one player on the field who can use your hands. Your hands are your greatest tool. You catch the ball. You punch the ball. You throw the ball. You push the ball around the post. No other player on your team can do what you do.

You wear different gloves. You wear a different colored jersey. You play by different rules. You are a different kind of player. That difference is something to be proud of.

The goal is your home. The area in front of the goal is your territory. You own that space. You decide what happens inside your box. When a ball comes into your area, you take charge.

Being a goalkeeper takes courage. The ball comes at you fast. Attackers run straight at you. You have to stand your ground. You have to make yourself big. You have to be brave in moments when other players might turn away.

You see the whole field from your position. You see things that no other player can see. You see where the danger is building. You see which teammate is open. You see the shape of the game from the back. Your view of the field is the best view on the team.

Your voice matters. You talk to your defenders. You tell them where to move. You warn them when a player is behind them. You call for the ball when you want the ball. A goalkeeper who talks makes the whole defense stronger.

You are the leader of the back line. Your defenders look to you for direction. When you are calm, your defenders feel calm. When you are organized, your defenders stay organized. Your confidence spreads to every player in front of you.

Every great team has a great goalkeeper. The best teams in the world build everything from the back. The goalkeeper is the foundation. You are the foundation of your team.

You will make incredible saves. You will dive full stretch and tip the ball over the bar. You will smother the ball at an attacker's feet. You will catch a shot that everyone thought was going in. Those moments belong to you.

You will also have quiet stretches in a game. The ball will be far away. Your team will be attacking. During those moments, you stay ready. You stay focused. You stay on your toes. The next ball could come at any second.

Some kids want to play forward. Some kids want to play in the midfield. You chose the goal. You chose the position that requires the most bravery. You chose the position that carries the most responsibility. That choice says something about who you are.

You are not just standing in front of the net. You are reading the game. You are organizing your team. You are making decisions every single second. You are always involved in the game, even when the ball is far away.

The best goalkeepers love their position. The best goalkeepers take pride in a clean sheet. The best goalkeepers celebrate a big save the same way a striker celebrates a goal. A save can change a game just like a goal can.

You are the goalkeeper. You are brave. You are smart. You are the backbone of your team. Every time you step between the posts, you are doing something special.
