# Distribution

You made the save. You have the ball in your hands. The danger is over. Now the fun begins. Now you start the attack for your team.

Distribution is the word for how you deliver the ball to your teammates. Good distribution turns defense into attack in one quick moment. The way you give the ball to your team matters just as much as the way you stop the ball.

Look up immediately after you secure the ball. Scan the field. Find your teammates. Find the open player. The faster you look up, the faster you find the best option. Speed matters because the other team is still recovering from their missed chance.

Roll the ball to a nearby defender. The roll is the simplest and most accurate distribution. Bend down and roll the ball along the ground with your hand. Aim for your defender's feet. A clean roll to a defender keeps the ball safe and lets your team start moving forward.

Use the roll when a defender is close and has time on the ball. The roll is low risk and high accuracy. Your defender receives the ball with no bounce and no surprise. The roll is your most common distribution.

Throw the ball overhand to a teammate farther up the field. Step forward with the opposite foot from your throwing hand. Snap your arm forward and release the ball at the right moment. An overhand throw can reach a wing player quickly. The throw is faster than a kick and more accurate at medium range.

Use the throw when you spot a wing player in space. A quick throw to the wing can start a fast break. The other team might not be set up defensively yet. Your throw puts your teammate in a great position to attack.

Punt the ball when you need distance. Hold the ball out in front of you. Drop the ball onto your kicking foot. Strike the ball with your laces. Follow through with your leg. A punt travels the farthest of any distribution method. Use the punt when your team is under heavy pressure and needs the ball as far from the goal as possible.

Take goal kicks with confidence. Place the ball on the edge of the six-yard box. Pick your target before you approach the ball. Strike the ball cleanly with your laces. Follow through toward your target. A good goal kick reaches your sweeper or your defenders in the midfield area.

Aim your goal kicks away from the center of the field. Kick toward the sides where there is more space. A goal kick that goes straight down the middle often lands in a crowded area. A goal kick toward the wing gives your teammate more room to receive the ball.

Find your sweeper whenever possible. Your sweeper is the player who starts the attack from the back. A ball to the sweeper gives your team a calm and controlled way to build forward. The sweeper can then pick the right pass to an attacker.

Quick distribution catches the other team by surprise. When you see a teammate open right after making a save, do not wait. Throw the ball immediately. A fast throw while the other team is still out of position can create a scoring chance from your own save.

Vary your distribution throughout the game. If you always roll to the same defender, the other team will figure out the pattern. Mix in throws to the wings. Mix in punts down the field. Keep the other team guessing about what you will do with the ball.

You are the first attacker on your team. Every attack can start with you. The ball in your hands is an opportunity. Choose wisely, deliver quickly, and watch your team push forward.
