# You Are the Sweeper

You are the sweeper. You are the most important field player on the team. You play behind the two defenders, in front of the goalkeeper. You are the deepest field player. You are the last line of defense before the goal.

Your coach chose you for the sweeper position because you have the best touch on the team. Your coach chose you because you think faster than anyone else on the field. The sweeper position demands the smartest player. That player is you.

## The Field General

You are the field general. A general sees the whole battlefield and tells the army where to go. You see the whole field and guide your team from the back. You read the game before anyone else reads the game. You make the decisions that shape the match.

From your position behind the defenders, you can see every player on the field. You can see your teammates. You can see every opponent. You can see the spaces between players. You can see where the danger is building. You can see where the opportunity is opening.

No other player on the field has the view that you have. The attackers face away from you. The defenders face the ball. You face everything. The whole game unfolds in front of your eyes.

## Your Two Jobs

Your first job is defense. You stop the other team from scoring. You intercept passes through the center. You step up and win the ball when an attacker breaks through. You clear danger before the danger reaches your goalkeeper. Defense always comes first.

Your second job is attack. You start the counter-attack with one brilliant pass. You find the open attacker with a long ball. You turn defense into offense in a single moment. The best goals your team scores will start with your passes.

These two jobs make the sweeper the most complete player on the field. You defend. You attack. You control the game from the back.

## The Shape Around You

Picture the formation. Your goalkeeper stands behind you in the goal. You stand in the center of the field, deeper than anyone else. Two defenders play in front of you, one on the left and one on the right. Three attackers spread across the front line.

You are the back point of a triangle. The two defenders form the middle. The three attackers form the top. The whole team builds from your position outward.

## Trust

Your teammates trust you. Your goalkeeper trusts you to clean up any ball that gets through the defense. Your defenders trust you to cover the space behind them. Your attackers trust you to find them with the right pass at the right time.

Your coach trusts you with the hardest job on the field. The sweeper position carries the most responsibility. You read the game. You organize the team. You defend the goal. You launch the attack. You hold the whole system together.

That trust is earned every time you make a calm decision under pressure. That trust is earned every time you win the ball and pick the right pass. That trust is earned every time you talk to your teammates and help them find their positions.

## This Is Your Position

You are not just a defender. You are not just a passer. You are the player who controls the game. You are the heartbeat of the team. Every great team needs a great sweeper. Your team has you.

Own the position. Love the position. The sweeper sees more, thinks more, and touches the ball more than any other player on the field. The game flows through you. You are the sweeper.
