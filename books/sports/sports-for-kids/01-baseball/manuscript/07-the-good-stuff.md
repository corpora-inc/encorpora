# The Good Stuff

Now let me tell you about the really fun moments.

The big stuff.

The stuff that makes you remember a game forever.

First — the home run.

A home run is when you hit the ball so far that you can run all the way around the bases.

You touch first, second, third, and home, all in one play.

You don't even have to hurry.

You can jog and smile.

Your whole team is waiting for you at home plate.

They give you high-fives.

I have hit one home run in my life.

Just one.

I still think about it.

Then there's the double play.

That's when the fielders get two outs in one play.

It happens fast.

The ball gets hit on the ground.

The shortstop scoops it up.

He throws it to second base.

The second baseman tags the runner.

Then he throws it to first base.

Two outs.

Just like that.

It feels like magic when it works.

Then there's the stolen base.

That's what Marcus does.

The pitcher throws to home plate.

While the ball is in the air, Marcus runs.

He sprints from first base to second base.

If he gets there before they tag him, the base is his.

He stole it.

That's why we call it a steal.

Strikeouts are exciting too, if you're the pitcher.

When Jerome strikes a batter out, the dugout goes wild.

The dugout is where we sit when we're not playing.

It's like a little bench under a roof.

We bang on the fence.

We yell, "Yeah, Jerome!"

He just smiles a little and gets the ball back.

He acts like it's no big deal.

But you can tell he loves it.

Every kid loves the big moments.

But I love the little ones too.

A good catch.

A clean throw.

Sliding into second base just in time.

Those count just as much to me.
