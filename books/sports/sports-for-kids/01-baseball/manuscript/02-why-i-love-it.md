# Why I Love It

Okay, so why do I love baseball?

It's hard to pick just one reason.

I love how it feels to hit the ball.

When the bat connects, you hear a loud crack.

The sound goes right through you.

It makes my whole arm tingle.

I love running, too.

I love sprinting from one base to the next.

My legs feel like they are flying.

My heart pounds in my chest.

I love being outside.

The sky is huge above the field.

The grass smells fresh, especially after it rains.

I love being on a team.

When I do something good, my friends cheer.

When they do something good, I cheer too.

Nobody plays baseball alone.

You always have your team behind you.

I love the quiet moments, too.

Sometimes the game gets really still.

The pitcher is about to throw.

Everybody is watching.

Nobody is talking.

Then, in one second, everything explodes.

The ball flies.

The runners take off.

People shout from the stands.

I love that part.

I love that anything can happen.

You never know how a game will end.

You can be losing the whole time.

Then in the last inning, your team scores.

You win, and your friends jump on you.

That feeling is the best feeling.

Even when we lose, I still love it.

Because tomorrow there's another practice.

And next Saturday there's another game.

Baseball just keeps going.

That's why I love it.
