# Baseball with Ryan

Ryan is ten, plays Little League, and would talk about baseball until the cows
come home. This book is him introducing the sport to another kid — maybe one
who has never picked up a bat, maybe one in another country learning English.
Every sentence stands alone as a language-learning utterance: one thought, one
sentence, mostly short and concrete.
