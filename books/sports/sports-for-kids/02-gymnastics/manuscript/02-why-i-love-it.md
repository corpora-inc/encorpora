# Why I Love It

People ask me why I love gymnastics.

I always have to think for a second.

Because there are so many reasons.

But the biggest one is the flying.

When you flip in the air, you are flying.

It only lasts for a moment.

Maybe half a second.

Maybe less.

But in that moment, nothing else exists.

You are not on the ground.

You are not thinking about school.

You are just spinning through the air.

It is the best feeling in the world.

I also love the moment after the flip.

When you land on your feet and stop perfectly still.

We call that sticking the landing.

When you stick a landing, your whole body knows.

You feel it from your toes up to your head.

I love the slow stuff too.

Like a handstand that stays still for a long time.

It feels like balancing on a tiny mountain.

I love the music on floor.

I love the chalk on my hands.

I love how my heart beats really fast before my turn.

I love how my coach smiles when I do something new.

But mostly, I love that I can do things I could not do last year.

A year ago I could not do a back handspring.

Now I can.

Next year there will be something new.

There is always something new.

That is why I keep coming back.
