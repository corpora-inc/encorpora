# What My Body Can Do

Gymnastics teaches your body to do strange and wonderful things.

When I started, I could barely touch my toes.

Now I can put my whole chest on my legs.

I can do the splits, both sides.

I can do a backbend and walk around in it.

Your body changes when you practice.

It gets stronger and bendier slowly, over months.

You do not really notice it day by day.

But one day you try something you could not do before.

And there it is.

Your body just does it.

I will tell you the basic skills.

The most basic one is the forward roll.

You tuck your chin and roll like a ball.

Anyone can learn that.

It is the first thing every gymnast learns.

Then comes the cartwheel.

You go sideways, hand, hand, foot, foot.

It feels like a little spin around the world.

Then comes the handstand.

You put your hands on the floor and kick your feet up.

You hold your whole body straight, upside down.

It is harder than it looks.

The first time you balance for even two seconds, you scream a little.

Then comes the round-off.

That is a cartwheel that ends with both feet together.

It is how we start most of our tumbling.

Then comes the back handspring.

That is a backwards flip, but with your hands touching the ground.

A flip without your hands is called a salto.

A salto is what you see in the Olympics, where the gymnast spins in the air.

Some skills you can do on the floor.

Some skills you only do into the foam pit at first.

That keeps you safe while you learn.

My coach says every gymnast has skills they are still working on.

Even Olympic gymnasts have skills they are still working on.

That is part of the sport.

You are never done learning.
