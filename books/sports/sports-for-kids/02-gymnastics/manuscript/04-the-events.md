# The Four Events

In artistic gymnastics, there are four events for girls.

Four things we compete in.

I will tell you about each one.

The first one is vault.

Vault is short and fast.

You sprint down a long runway.

Then you jump onto a springboard.

Then you push off a soft block with your hands.

Then you fly through the air and twist.

Then you land.

The whole thing takes maybe five seconds.

It is over so fast.

But your heart pounds for a long time after.

The second event is the uneven bars.

Two bars, one high and one low.

You swing and spin and let go.

You jump from one bar to the other.

Your hands get tired and a little sore.

That is why we use chalk.

When you are on the bars, you feel like a bird.

The third event is the balance beam.

The beam is four inches wide.

Four inches is about as wide as my palm.

You walk on it, jump on it, do flips on it.

Yes, real flips, on a beam that skinny.

It sounds scary.

It kind of is.

You fall off a lot when you are learning.

But when you stay on, it feels amazing.

The fourth event is floor exercise.

We just call it floor.

It is the big springy square.

You do a routine with music.

You do flips and leaps and dance moves.

You go from corner to corner across the floor.

Floor is my favorite.

I love picking the music.

I love feeling the bounce under my feet.

There is also another kind of gymnastics called rhythmic.

In rhythmic, gymnasts use ribbons and balls and hoops.

It is beautiful.

But I do artistic, so we will mostly talk about that.
