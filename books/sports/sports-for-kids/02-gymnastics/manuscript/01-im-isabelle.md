# Hi, I'm Isabelle

Hi.

My name is Isabelle.

I am nine years old.

I do gymnastics.

I have done gymnastics since I was six.

That is three whole years.

When people ask me what I do, I always say it first.

I do gymnastics.

I say it before I say anything else.

Some kids play soccer.

Some kids play piano.

I tumble.

I balance.

I fly.

That is what gymnastics is, mostly.

You learn to use your whole body.

You learn to flip and spin and land on your feet.

It is hard.

I will tell you that right away.

But it is the best kind of hard.

It is the kind that makes you feel proud.

I go to a gym near my house.

I go three days a week.

Sometimes four.

My coach is named Coach Maya.

She is patient and kind.

She is also very, very strict about safety.

I love her.

I love the gym.

I love the smell of the mats.

I love the soft thump when you land just right.

I want to tell you all about it.
