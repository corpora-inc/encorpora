# Inside the Gym

Let me show you the gym.

When you first walk in, it is huge.

The ceiling is really high.

It has to be, because we throw ourselves into the air.

The floor is mostly soft.

It is padded, like a giant mattress.

Some parts are extra springy.

Those parts are made for flipping.

There is a smell that I love.

It smells a little like rubber and a little like sweat.

It also smells like chalk.

The chalk lives in a big bucket near the bars.

We rub it on our hands so we do not slip.

It makes everything a little bit white.

My leotard, my arms, even my nose sometimes.

Around the gym, there is equipment everywhere.

There is the floor area, which is a big square.

There is the balance beam, which is long and skinny.

There are the uneven bars, with one bar high and one bar low.

There is the vault, which is a soft block at the end of a runway.

There is also a giant foam pit.

It is full of soft foam blocks.

That is where we learn new flips safely.

When you mess up, you just sink into the foam.

It is the most fun thing in the gym.

Trust me.

There are also pull-up bars and small trampolines.

There is a wall with mirrors, so we can see what we are doing.

And there are other kids, always.

Little kids learning forward rolls.

Big kids spinning on bars.

The gym is a busy, happy, bouncy place.
