# Practice and My Coach

Practice is most of what gymnastics is.

You do not just show up and do flips.

You warm up first.

Always.

We run laps around the gym.

We stretch every part of our bodies.

We do shoulder circles and leg swings.

We do little jumps to wake our feet up.

Warming up takes maybe twenty minutes.

Then we do conditioning.

That is the strength part.

Push-ups.

Sit-ups.

Holding a handstand against the wall.

Climbing a rope.

It is hard, but you get stronger every week.

Then comes the fun part, the events.

We move from event to event.

We spend maybe thirty minutes on each one.

We do the same skill again and again.

Sometimes I do a cartwheel on the beam fifty times.

Fifty.

I am not joking.

That is how you get really good.

You do something so many times that it stops feeling scary.

It just feels normal.

That is the secret.

My coach watches everything.

She sees things I do not even know I am doing.

She will say, your left arm bent a little.

And I will say, no it did not.

And then we watch the video, and yep, it bent.

She does not get mad.

She just shows me what to fix.

She is patient like that.

She also catches us sometimes.

That is called spotting.

When you are learning a flip, the coach stands right there.

She has her hands ready to help if you fall.

A good coach makes you feel safe enough to try scary things.

That is the most important part.

A good coach makes you feel safe.

So you try.

And you get better.

That is the deal.
