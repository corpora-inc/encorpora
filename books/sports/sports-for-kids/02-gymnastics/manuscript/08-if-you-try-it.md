# If You Ever Try It

I have told you almost everything.

There are a few last things.

We wear leotards.

A leotard is like a one-piece bathing suit, but for gymnastics.

Mine has sparkles on the shoulders.

Some kids have plain ones.

Some kids have wild rainbow ones.

You can pick whatever you like.

We compete with other gyms sometimes.

We do our routines and judges give us a score.

Scores are not the most important thing.

But it is fun to wear your team leotard and cheer with your friends.

The other gymnasts are not really enemies.

They are kids who love the same thing you love.

You usually end up making friends.

I want to tell you one more thing.

You do not have to be born flexible to do gymnastics.

You do not have to be tiny.

You do not have to start when you are three.

I know kids who started at eight, nine, even ten.

They got really good.

You just have to like trying.

You have to like falling and getting up.

If that sounds like you, you should try a class.

Just one class.

See how it feels.

If you do not have a gym near you, that is okay.

You can still try a forward roll on a soft bed or a thick rug.

Tuck your chin so the back of your head touches first.

Roll like a ball.

That is the first skill every gymnast learns.

You can also try a handstand against a wall.

Just kick your feet up and hold on.

Even five seconds counts.

Maybe you will love it.

Maybe you will not.

Either way is fine.

I just wanted to share the thing I love most.

So thanks for listening.

Thanks for letting me tell you about my sport.

Now I have to go.

Practice is in twenty minutes.

Bye.
