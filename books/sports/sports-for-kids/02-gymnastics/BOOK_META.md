# Gymnastics with Isabelle

Isabelle is nine. She trains a few times a week and loves the feeling of
flying. This book is her introducing gymnastics to another kid — maybe a kid
who has never tried any sport, maybe a kid in another country learning
English. Every sentence stands alone as a language-learning utterance: one
thought, one sentence, mostly short and concrete.
