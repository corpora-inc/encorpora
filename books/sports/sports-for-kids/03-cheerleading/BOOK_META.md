# Cheerleading with Avery

Avery is a ten-year-old girl on a coed competition cheer team that also
cheers on the school's basketball games. The book covers both sides of the
sport honestly: sideline cheer (supporting a team) and competitive cheer
(routines, stunts, tumbling, music).

Every sentence stands alone as a language-learning utterance: one thought,
one sentence, mostly short and concrete. Cheer-specific vocabulary (flyer,
base, spotter, stunt, pyramid, round-off, herkie) is introduced and explained
in the same breath, kid-style.
