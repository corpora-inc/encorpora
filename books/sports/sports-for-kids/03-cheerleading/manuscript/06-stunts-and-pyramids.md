# Stunts and Pyramids

Okay, stunts.

This is the part people lose their minds over.

A stunt is when a flyer is lifted into the air.

The simplest stunt is called a thigh stand.

The flyer stands on the bases' thighs.

That's it, pretty low and pretty safe.

We all start there.

The next step up is a prep.

A prep is when the flyer stands on the bases' hands.

The hands are about chin height.

It looks higher than it is.

Then there is an extension.

In an extension, the bases push the flyer all the way up.

Arms locked out.

The flyer is high above our heads.

A flyer up in an extension can see the whole gym.

Now, I have to be honest with you.

Stunts can be dangerous.

That's not a secret.

That's why we have rules, so many rules.

We always have a back spot.

We always have mats when we are learning.

We never try something new without our coach.

We progress slowly.

That word matters.

Progress.

You don't just decide one day to do a basket toss.

A basket toss is when the bases launch the flyer up.

She flips and comes back down.

You don't do that on day one.

You do it after months of practice, maybe years.

You earn it.

Pyramids are stunts connected together.

Multiple flyers in the air at once.

Holding hands, linked at the wrists.

It looks like a moving sculpture.

When the music is right, it's the most beautiful thing in the sport.

The whole team is one shape.

That moment is worth all the work.
