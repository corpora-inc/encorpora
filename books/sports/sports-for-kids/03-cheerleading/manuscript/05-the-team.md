# The Team

Okay, this is important.

A cheer team has roles.

Different bodies do different jobs.

The first role is the flyer.

A flyer is the cheerleader who goes up in the air.

They get lifted, they get tossed.

They stand on people's hands.

They smile the whole time.

Flyers are usually smaller, but not always.

It's about being strong and steady.

You have to hold your body super tight.

A floppy flyer is a falling flyer.

The next role is the base.

A base is one of the cheerleaders who lifts the flyer.

There are usually two bases per flyer.

Bases need strong legs and strong shoulders.

I am a base on my team.

I am pretty strong.

Then there is the back spot.

The back spot stands behind the stunt.

Their job is to keep the flyer safe.

They hold the flyer's ankles and guide them up.

They catch them coming down.

They are like the team's safety net.

So that's a stunt group.

A flyer, two bases, a back spot.

Four people, all for one stunt.

Trust is everything in cheer.

I trust my flyer to stay tight.

My flyer trusts me to hold her.

I trust the back spot to catch.

We have all caught each other before.

Trust isn't a word you just say.

It's something you build at practice, slowly.

Then one day you look up and you have it.
