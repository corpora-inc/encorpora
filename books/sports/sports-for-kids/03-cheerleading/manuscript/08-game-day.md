# Game Day

Game days are a whole vibe.

That's how my coach says it.

We have a varsity basketball team at our school, and we cheer for them.

You feel it before your feet touch the floor.

We pack our bag the night before.

Uniform, shoes, bow, water bottle, snack.

The uniform is a big deal.

We all match, that's the whole point.

Some of the boys wear a top with shorts.

It still matches.

The point is that you look like one team.

Hair has to be tight and out of your face.

In a high pony with a bow.

The bow is part of the uniform, too.

It's bigger than your head.

Pom-poms.

People think cheer is just pom-poms, but it's not.

We do use them though.

A pom-pom is two layers of plastic strips on a handle.

When you shake it, it sounds like rain.

A whole team shaking them sounds amazing.

Game day starts before the game.

We warm up in a side hallway.

We stretch and run through chants.

We hype each other up.

Then the players run out of the locker room.

We line the tunnel and slap their hands.

The gym is already loud.

The pep band is playing.

Then the game starts.

If our team scores, we go.

If they steal the ball, we go.

If they need a boost, we go louder.

Timeouts are our time.

When the buzzer goes for a timeout, we run out onto the court.

We do a quick cheer, then we run off before the players come back.

Halftime is our biggest moment.

We do a full performance at halftime.

Music, stunts, the whole thing.

The whole gym is watching.

I love halftime.

After the game we line up again.

We slap hands with our players, win or lose.

That part matters.
