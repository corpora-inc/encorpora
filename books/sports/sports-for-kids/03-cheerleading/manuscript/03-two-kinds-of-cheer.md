# Two Kinds of Cheer

A lot of people don't know this.

There are basically two kinds of cheer.

The first one is sideline cheer.

That's the one you've probably seen on TV.

The cheerleaders stand on the side of the basketball court, or the football field.

They cheer for the team that is playing.

They lead the crowd and do chants.

They do little stunts during timeouts.

Their job is to bring the energy.

We do that for our school.

We cheer at basketball games all winter.

The second kind is competitive cheer.

This is its own sport.

The cheer team is not cheering for anyone else.

The team is the team being judged.

You perform a routine, other teams perform their routines.

Judges score everything.

The team with the highest score wins.

A competition routine is about two and a half minutes.

That doesn't sound long, but it's forever.

You do tumbling, stunts, a pyramid, a dance.

All to music, all without stopping.

So just remember.

Sideline cheer supports another team.

Competitive cheer is the team.

Most cheerleaders I know do both.

I do both.

Both are great.

Both are cheer.
