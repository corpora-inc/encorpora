# Why I Love It

So why do I love cheer.

A lot of reasons.

The first one is the team.

We see each other almost every day.

We sweat together and mess up together.

We get it right together.

When you nail a routine, everyone screams.

Not just the crowd, you scream too.

You can't help it.

The second reason is the crowd.

Have you ever been in a loud gym.

It's amazing.

A whole room of people clapping.

You start a chant and the crowd picks it up.

Suddenly hundreds of people are saying it with you.

That is the coolest feeling.

The third reason is harder to explain.

It's the feeling of doing something brave.

Cheer is a brave sport.

You jump and you flip.

You lift other people, and you trust other people to lift you.

When you do something you couldn't do last month, that's huge.

You feel taller after.

The last reason is the music.

When a competition song hits, your body just goes.

You don't even think.

You've practiced it so many times.

Your arms know what to do.

It's like flying.

That's why I love it.

That's not even all of it, but it's a start.
