# A Note for the Listener

This is a book about the oud, a wooden musical instrument with strings. People have been playing it for more than a thousand years.

You can hear the oud in cities by the sea and in villages in the mountains. You can hear it in old palaces and on small wooden stages.

In this book, we will learn what the oud looks like, how it is built, and the names of the people who shaped its story. By the end, you will know why the oud is sometimes called the king of all the strings.
