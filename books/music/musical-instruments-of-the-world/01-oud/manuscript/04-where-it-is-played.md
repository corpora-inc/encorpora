## Chapter 4 — Where the Oud Is Played

The oud is played all across the Arab world. You will hear it in Egypt, Syria, Iraq, Lebanon, the Gulf, and North Africa.

It is also played beyond the Arab world. The Turks have their own oud. The Greeks have their own oud. The Armenians and the Iranians play cousins of it. In Spain, it was played for hundreds of years.

There are three main types of oud today. The Arabian oud is large and deep, with a full and slow sound. It is the oud of Egypt and the Levant.

The Turkish oud is smaller and brighter, tuned a little higher than the Arabian oud.

The Persian cousin is called the barbat. It is small and sharp, and it uses a different set of notes.

The oud is the heart of classical Arab music and of Turkish makam music. It plays at weddings and at funerals, in concert halls and in living rooms with friends.

Wherever people gather and want music with a soul, someone may bring out an oud.
