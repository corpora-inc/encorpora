## About This Book

This is the first volume in Musical Instruments of the World. Each book in this series tells the story of a single instrument. Each book is short, and each book is true.

The next book is about the sitar, the great long-necked lute of India, with its many strings and its deep, ringing voice. After the sitar, we will travel to West Africa for the kora, and then to Japan for the shakuhachi.

Thank you for listening. Go find an oud, online or in your city, and let it play for you.
