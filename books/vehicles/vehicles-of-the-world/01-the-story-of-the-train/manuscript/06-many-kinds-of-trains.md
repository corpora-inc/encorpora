## Chapter 6 — Many Kinds of Trains

Not all trains are the same. There are many kinds.

A passenger train carries people. It has seats, windows, and sometimes a dining car or a sleeping car.

A freight train carries goods. The cars are called wagons. Some carry grain. Some carry oil. Some carry whole shipping containers stacked two high.

A subway is an underground train in a city. The first subway opened in London in 1863. Today, big cities have huge subway networks under their streets.

A tram or light rail is a small train that runs on city streets. It often shares the road with cars and buses.

A mountain train climbs up steep hills. Some have a special toothed rail down the middle, called a cog. The locomotive grips the cog to go up and to come down safely.

Some trains are also tourist trains. They run through forests and mountains, just for the view.
