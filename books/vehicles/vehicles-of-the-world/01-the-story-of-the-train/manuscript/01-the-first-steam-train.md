## Chapter 1 — The First Steam Train

A train is a line of cars that runs on iron rails. The first car is the locomotive. It pulls all the others.

The first trains were powered by steam. A steam engine burns coal to boil water. The hot steam pushes pistons. The pistons turn the wheels. The wheels run on the rails.

The story begins in England. In the year 1825, the first public railway opened. It was called the Stockton and Darlington Railway. It carried coal from a mine to the river.

A man named George Stephenson built the locomotives. People called him the Father of the Railways.

In 1829, his son Robert Stephenson built a famous locomotive called the Rocket. It won a race for the fastest train. The Rocket could go almost 30 miles per hour. That was faster than any horse.

The age of the train had begun.
