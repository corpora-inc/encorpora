## Chapter 5 — Bullet Trains and the Race for Speed

In 1964, Japan opened a brand new railway. It ran from Tokyo to Osaka. The trains looked like long white bullets. People called them bullet trains. The Japanese name is Shinkansen.

The first bullet trains went 130 miles per hour. That was very fast for a train in 1964. The line opened just in time for the Tokyo Olympic Games.

Other countries followed. France opened its high speed line in 1981. The trains are called the TGV. Germany followed with the ICE. Italy, Spain, and Korea built their own high speed lines.

China was last to start, but it built fast. Today, China has the largest high speed network in the world. There are tens of thousands of kilometers of track.

Modern bullet trains can cruise at more than 200 miles per hour. They are smooth, quiet, and almost always on time. For many trips, they are faster than flying.
