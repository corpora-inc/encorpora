## Chapter 7 — The Future on the Rails

The story of the train is not over. New kinds of trains are being built right now.

The most amazing is the maglev. The word maglev is short for magnetic levitation. A maglev train does not touch its track. It floats on a cushion of magnets. There are no wheels turning. There is no friction.

In 2004, a maglev line opened in Shanghai, China. The train can reach more than 250 miles per hour. In Japan, a test maglev train has gone faster than 370 miles per hour.

Other engineers are working on the hydrogen train. A hydrogen train makes electricity from a small fuel cell. The only thing that comes out of the engine is water vapor. No smoke. No coal. The first hydrogen trains began service in Germany in 2018.

Trains are very old. They are also very new. Two hundred years after the Rocket, the rails are still the future.
