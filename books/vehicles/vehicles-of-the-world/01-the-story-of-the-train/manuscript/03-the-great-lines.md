## Chapter 3 — The Great Lines

Some railways are short. Some are very long. The longest lines crossed whole continents.

In the United States, two companies built a railway across the country. One started in California and worked east. The other started in Nebraska and worked west. They met at a place called Promontory Summit, in Utah.

This was in the year 1869. The last spike was made of gold. People called it the Golden Spike. The line was called the Transcontinental Railroad. Now you could ride a train from one ocean to the other in about a week.

Russia built an even longer line. It is called the Trans Siberian Railway. It runs from Moscow in the west to Vladivostok in the east. The line was finished in 1916. It is more than nine thousand kilometers long. A full trip takes about seven days. It is still the longest railway in the world.
