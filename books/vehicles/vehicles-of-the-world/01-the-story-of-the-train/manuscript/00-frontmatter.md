# A Note for the Reader

For two hundred years, the train has been the iron road of the world. Trains move people. Trains move goods. A long train can carry a whole town's worth of grain. A fast train can outrun a car on the highway.

The story of the train begins in England, almost two hundred years ago, with a man, a fire, and a kettle of boiling water.
