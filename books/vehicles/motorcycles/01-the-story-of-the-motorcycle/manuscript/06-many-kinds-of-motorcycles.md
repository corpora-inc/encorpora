## Chapter 6 — Many Kinds of Motorcycles

Today, motorcycles come in many shapes. Each kind is built for a different job.

A sport bike is built to go very fast. It is light and low. The rider leans forward. Sport bikes are made for smooth roads and racetracks.

A dirt bike is built for mud and dirt. It has tall wheels and big tires. Dirt bikes can jump over hills. Many young riders learn on dirt bikes first.

A cruiser is long and low. The rider sits up tall and rides slowly. Harley-Davidson is famous for cruisers. So is the Indian.

An adventure bike is built for long trips. It can ride on roads and dirt. The BMW GS is a famous adventure bike. So is the KTM.

There are also chopper bikes, scooter bikes, racing bikes, and electric bikes. There is a motorcycle for almost every kind of road.

Today, you can find a motorcycle in every country in the world.
