## Chapter 7 — A New Road

Today, the motorcycle has a new kind of engine. It is the electric engine. An electric motorcycle is silent. It does not need gasoline. It just needs to be plugged in like a phone.

A company called Zero builds electric motorcycles in California. Harley-Davidson now builds an electric bike called the LiveWire. An Italian company called Energica builds electric racing bikes. They are very fast.

The first motorcycle, the Reitwagen, could go 7 miles per hour. Today's electric motorcycles can go more than 200 miles per hour. That is the story of the motorcycle so far.

This was the big story. The next eleven books will tell the small stories. Each one is about one part of the motorcycle world.

Volume two is about Harley-Davidson. Volume three is about Honda. Volume four is about Ducati from Italy. Volume five is about BMW from Germany. Volume six is about Triumph from England.

Volume seven is about dirt bikes. Volume eight is about sport bikes. Volume nine is about cruisers and choppers. Volume ten is about adventure bikes. Volume eleven is about racing.

Volume twelve is about electric bikes and the future. Pick the one that looks like the most fun. Then come along for the ride.
