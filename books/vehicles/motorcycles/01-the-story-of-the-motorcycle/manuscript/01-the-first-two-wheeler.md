## Chapter 1 — The First Two-Wheeler That Went Vroom

A motorcycle is a bike with an engine. The engine pushes the wheels. The rider does not pedal.

Long ago, there were no motorcycles. People rode horses. People rode bicycles. A bicycle has two wheels and no engine. You have to push the pedals with your feet.

In the year 1885, a man in Germany had a big idea. His name was Gottlieb Daimler. He took a small gasoline engine and put it on a wooden bicycle.

He called his machine the Reitwagen. The word means "riding wagon" in German. It was the first motorcycle in the world.

The Reitwagen was loud. It was bumpy. It had only one speed. It could go about 7 miles per hour. That is slower than a person on a bike today. But it could move without a horse, and it could move without pedals.

The motorcycle was born.
