## Chapter 2 — The First Companies

After Daimler, other people wanted to build motorcycles too. Soon, small workshops opened all over the world.

In England, a man named Siegfried Bettmann started a company called Triumph. The first Triumph motorcycle was built in the year 1902.

In America, two friends named William Harley and Arthur Davidson built their first bike in a small wooden shed. The shed was in the city of Milwaukee. The year was 1903. They called the company Harley-Davidson.

Another American company started a few years earlier. Two men in Springfield, Massachusetts, built their first machine in 1901. They called it the Indian.

In India, a company called Royal Enfield began making motorcycles in 1901 as well. Today, Royal Enfield is the oldest motorcycle company in the world that is still building bikes.

In Germany, the BMW company started making motorcycles a little later, in 1923. BMW means "Bavarian Motor Works" in German.

By the year 1910, motorcycles were everywhere. They were on city streets and country roads. The age of the motorcycle had really begun.
