## Chapter 3 — Motorcycles Go to War

Motorcycles are fast. They can fit on small paths. They can carry messages between camps. So when wars came, armies wanted motorcycles.

The First World War started in 1914. Soldiers used Triumph motorcycles to carry orders. The Triumph Model H was strong and quiet. It could go where horses could not.

The Second World War started in 1939. By then, motorcycles were faster and stronger. American soldiers rode the Harley-Davidson WLA. German soldiers rode the BMW R75. The R75 had a sidecar with a third wheel and a seat for another soldier.

After the war, the soldiers came home. They brought their motorcycles with them. Many of them wanted to keep riding. Some of them started their own motorcycle clubs.

The wars made the motorcycle famous. Millions of people had seen one for the first time. Many wanted one of their own.
