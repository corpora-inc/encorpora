# A Note for the Reader

This is the story of the motorcycle. It is a true story.

Long ago, no one had a motorcycle. Now, there are millions of them. They are loud and fast and shiny.

We are going to start at the very beginning. We will meet the first builder, the first riders, and the first races. Then we will look at the world today. By the end, you will know how this little machine changed the road forever.
