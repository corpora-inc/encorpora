# Cells Working Together

A single cell can do many things. But in large organisms, cells work together. Groups of similar cells form tissues. A tissue is a collection of cells that share the same structure and function.

Your body has four main types of tissue. The first type is epithelial tissue. Epithelial tissue covers surfaces. Your skin is epithelial tissue. The lining of your mouth is epithelial tissue. The lining of your stomach is epithelial tissue. Epithelial cells fit tightly together. They form barriers. They protect what lies beneath.

The second type is muscle tissue. Muscle tissue can contract. To contract means to shorten. When muscle cells contract, they pull on bones and create movement. Your heart is made of a special kind of muscle tissue. It contracts rhythmically without stopping.

The third type is connective tissue. Connective tissue supports and connects other tissues. Bone is connective tissue. Cartilage is connective tissue. Cartilage is the firm, flexible material in your ears and nose. Blood is also a connective tissue. It connects every part of your body by carrying nutrients and oxygen.

The fourth type is nervous tissue. Nervous tissue sends electrical signals. Your brain is made of nervous tissue. Your spinal cord is made of nervous tissue. Nerves throughout your body are made of nervous tissue. Nervous tissue allows you to think, feel, and react.

Different tissues join together to form organs. An organ is a structure made of two or more tissues that work together to perform a specific function. Your heart is an organ. It contains muscle tissue, connective tissue, epithelial tissue, and nervous tissue. All four types work together to pump blood.

Organs work together in organ systems. An organ system is a group of organs that perform a related set of functions. The digestive system breaks down food. It includes the mouth, stomach, and intestines. The respiratory system handles breathing. It includes the lungs and airways.

The human body has about eleven major organ systems. Each system depends on the others. The circulatory system delivers oxygen that the respiratory system collects. The digestive system provides nutrients that the circulatory system distributes.

From cell to tissue to organ to organ system to organism. This is the hierarchy of life. Every level depends on the one below it. It all begins with the cell.
