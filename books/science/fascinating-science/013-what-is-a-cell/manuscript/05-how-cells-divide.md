# How Cells Divide

Cells do not live forever. They wear out. They get damaged. The body needs new cells constantly. New cells come from existing cells. A cell makes a new cell by dividing into two.

The most common type of cell division is called mitosis. Mitosis produces two identical daughter cells. Each daughter cell has the same DNA as the parent cell. Mitosis is how your body grows. It is how your body repairs wounds.

Before a cell can divide, it must copy its DNA. DNA is stored in structures called chromosomes. A chromosome is a long, tightly coiled strand of DNA. Human cells have 46 chromosomes. These 46 chromosomes come in 23 pairs.

The process of copying DNA is called replication. Replication means making a copy. The DNA molecule unzips down the middle. Each half serves as a template. New matching pieces attach to each half. The result is two complete copies of every chromosome.

Mitosis happens in stages. The first stage is called prophase. During prophase, the chromosomes condense. They become thick and visible under a microscope. The nuclear envelope begins to break down.

The second stage is called metaphase. The chromosomes line up in the middle of the cell. They form a neat row. Tiny fibers attach to each chromosome. These fibers are called spindle fibers.

The third stage is called anaphase. The spindle fibers pull the chromosomes apart. One copy goes to each side of the cell. The chromosomes move quickly.

The fourth stage is called telophase. A new nuclear envelope forms around each set of chromosomes. The chromosomes uncoil. Two new nuclei now exist inside one cell.

Finally, the cytoplasm divides. This step is called cytokinesis. Cytokinesis means "cell movement." The cell membrane pinches inward. It squeezes the cell in half. Two new cells result. Each cell has a full set of chromosomes.

There is another type of cell division called meiosis. Meiosis makes sex cells. Sex cells are eggs and sperm. Meiosis produces cells with only 23 chromosomes, half the normal number. When an egg and a sperm join, the full 46 chromosomes are restored.

Cell division is essential for life. Every second, millions of cells divide in your body.
