# The Pump That Never Stops

Your heart is beating right now. It beats while you sleep. It beats while you eat. It beats while you read this sentence. Your heart never takes a break. It is the most reliable pump in the world.

The heart is a muscular organ in your chest. An organ is a body part with a specific job. The heart's job is to pump blood. It sits slightly to the left of the center of your chest. It is roughly the size of your fist.

The heart is made of a special type of muscle called cardiac muscle. Cardiac means "related to the heart." Cardiac muscle is different from the muscles in your arms and legs. You control your arm muscles with your thoughts. You cannot control your cardiac muscle. It contracts on its own. It contracts automatically, every second of your life.

Each contraction pushes blood through your body. Blood carries oxygen to your cells. Oxygen is a gas that cells need to produce energy. Without oxygen, cells die within minutes. The heart makes sure oxygen reaches every part of your body.

Your heart pumps about five liters of blood per minute. A liter is roughly one quart. That means your entire blood supply circulates through your heart about once every minute. In one day, your heart pumps roughly 7,500 liters of blood. In one year, it pumps nearly three million liters.

The human heart begins beating early. It starts about three to four weeks after conception. Conception is the moment a new life begins. The heart is one of the first organs to form. It beats before the brain fully develops.

Ancient peoples knew the heart was important. The Egyptians believed the heart was the center of intelligence. The Greeks debated whether the heart or the brain controlled the body. Aristotle thought the heart was the seat of the soul. We now know the brain controls thought. But the heart keeps the brain alive.

The circulatory system is the network that moves blood through your body. The heart is at the center of this system. Circulatory comes from the word "circulate," which means to move in a circle. Blood leaves the heart, travels through the body, and returns to the heart. This loop never stops.

Your heart has been beating since before you were born. It will beat billions of times in your lifetime.
