# Elements

An element is a substance made of only one type of atom. Pure gold is an element. Every atom in a bar of pure gold has seventy-nine protons. Pure oxygen is an element. Every atom in a tank of pure oxygen has eight protons.

There are about ninety-two elements that occur naturally on Earth. Scientists have created additional elements in laboratories. The total number of known elements is over one hundred and eighteen. Each element has a unique number of protons.

Every element has a name and a symbol. Hydrogen is H. Oxygen is O. Carbon is C. Iron is Fe. Gold is Au. The symbols come from Latin, Greek, or English names. Fe comes from the Latin word ferrum. Au comes from the Latin word aurum.

Hydrogen is the simplest element. A hydrogen atom has one proton and one electron. Hydrogen is the most common element in the universe. Stars are made mostly of hydrogen.

Carbon is the element of life. Carbon has six protons. Carbon atoms can form bonds with many other atoms at the same time. This ability makes carbon the backbone of all living things. Every protein, every strand of DNA, and every sugar molecule in your body contains carbon.

Oxygen has eight protons. Oxygen is the element you breathe. Your lungs pull oxygen out of the air and send it into your blood. Your cells use oxygen to release energy from food.

Iron has twenty-six protons. Iron is the element that makes your blood red. Iron is the main ingredient in steel. The core of the Earth is mostly iron.

The lightest elements are the most common in the universe. Hydrogen and helium together make up about ninety-eight percent of all the atoms in the universe. The heavier elements are rare by comparison. Every atom of every heavy element was forged inside a star.
