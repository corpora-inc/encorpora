# Atoms Are Everything

You now know the basics. Everything is made of atoms. Atoms contain protons, neutrons, and electrons. The number of protons determines the element. Atoms bond to form molecules. Atoms are always in motion. Stars created the atoms that make up our world.

This knowledge is the foundation of chemistry, biology, physics, and medicine. Every field of science connects back to atoms.

When a doctor prescribes a medicine, that medicine is a specific arrangement of atoms designed to interact with the molecules in your body. When an engineer builds a bridge, the strength of that bridge depends on how the iron atoms in the steel are arranged. When a farmer grows a crop, the crop grows because carbon atoms from the air combine with water and sunlight inside the leaves.

Cooking is chemistry. When you bake bread, heat causes the atoms in the dough to rearrange. Proteins unfold. Sugars break apart and recombine. New molecules form that create the color, texture, and flavor of the crust. Every recipe is a set of instructions for rearranging atoms.

Breathing is chemistry. You inhale oxygen molecules. Your blood carries those molecules to your cells. Inside your cells, oxygen atoms combine with carbon atoms from food. The reaction releases energy. You exhale the carbon and oxygen as carbon dioxide. Every breath is a chemical reaction.

Rust is chemistry. Iron atoms on the surface of a nail react with oxygen atoms in the air. The iron and oxygen combine to form a new substance called iron oxide. Iron oxide is the reddish-brown material we call rust. The iron atoms are still there. They have just bonded with oxygen.

Atoms are not abstract. Atoms are the physical reality of everything you touch, taste, see, and smell. The more you understand atoms, the more you understand the world. This is the beginning. There is much more to explore.
