# Everything Is Made of Atoms

Look around you. Everything you can see is made of atoms. The air you breathe is made of atoms. The water you drink is made of atoms. Your body is made of atoms. The ground beneath your feet is made of atoms.

An atom is the smallest piece of matter that still has a chemical identity. You cannot see a single atom with your eyes. You cannot see a single atom with a regular microscope. Atoms are that small.

A single grain of sand contains about fifty quintillion atoms. That number is fifty followed by eighteen zeros. There are more atoms in one grain of sand than there are grains of sand on a beach.

Atoms are the building blocks of everything in the physical world. A wall is made of bricks. A sentence is made of words. The universe is made of atoms.

The word atom comes from ancient Greek. The Greek word atomos means uncuttable. Ancient Greek philosophers thought atoms were the smallest possible pieces of matter. They were close to right. Atoms are not truly the smallest particles, but they are the smallest units that keep their chemical properties.

Atoms are incredibly durable. The atoms in your body are billions of years old. Some of the hydrogen atoms in the water you drank today were created in the first minutes after the Big Bang. You are made of ancient materials.

Atoms are never created or destroyed in ordinary life. They rearrange. When wood burns, the atoms do not disappear. They rearrange into smoke, ash, and gas. When ice melts, the atoms stay the same. Only their arrangement changes.

Understanding atoms is the foundation of chemistry. Chemistry is the science of how atoms combine, rearrange, and interact. Everything that happens in the physical world comes down to atoms and what they do.
