# Our Star

The Sun is a star. A star is a giant ball of hot gas that makes its own light. The Sun looks different from other stars because it is close to Earth. Other stars are so far away that they look like tiny dots in the night sky.

The Sun is the center of our solar system. A solar system is a star and everything that moves around it. Eight planets move around the Sun. Earth is one of those planets. Moons, asteroids, and comets also move around the Sun. An asteroid is a small rocky object in space. A comet is a ball of ice and dust that travels through space.

The Sun is about 93 million miles from Earth. Scientists call this distance one astronomical unit. An astronomical unit is a way to measure distances in space. Light from the Sun takes about eight minutes to reach Earth. Light is the fastest thing in the universe. It travels at 186,000 miles per second.

The Sun is not solid like Earth. It is made of gas and plasma. Plasma is a state of matter. There are four states of matter: solid, liquid, gas, and plasma. Plasma forms when gas gets so hot that its atoms break apart. Most of the Sun is plasma.

The Sun has been shining for about 4.6 billion years. A billion is one thousand million. Scientists know this because they study rocks and meteorites. A meteorite is a rock from space that lands on Earth. These rocks contain clues about how old the solar system is.

Every culture in human history has noticed the Sun. Ancient people watched the Sun rise and set every day. They used the Sun to mark time. They built calendars based on the Sun's movement. Some cultures worshipped the Sun as a god.

The Sun makes life on Earth possible. Without the Sun, Earth would be dark and cold. Plants need sunlight to grow. Animals need plants for food. The Sun drives the weather. It heats the air and the oceans. It powers the water cycle. The water cycle is the movement of water between the ground, the air, and back again.

The Sun is ordinary in one way. It is just one of billions of stars in our galaxy. A galaxy is a huge collection of stars. Our galaxy is called the Milky Way. But the Sun is extraordinary in another way. It is our star. It gives us light, warmth, and life.
