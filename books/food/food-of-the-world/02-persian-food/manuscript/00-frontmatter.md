# A Note for the Reader

Hi. I'm Kym. I love food. I love stories about food.

This is a small book about a big food. The food is Persian food. It is the food of Iran. It is also the food of a much older country called Persia.

Persia is one of the oldest places on earth where people grew food on purpose. Wheat, barley, the peach, the apricot, the pomegranate, spinach, saffron — many things on your plate were born there.

We will start with the land. Then we will look at the things that grow there. Then we will look at the rice, the bread, the meat, and the stews. By the end, you will know what is on a Persian table, and you will know where it came from.

Sit down. Pour some tea. Let's begin.
