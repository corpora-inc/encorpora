## [0.1.0] — 2026-05-09

- Initial release. 7 chapters + frontmatter + backmatter, ~1,800 words,
  ~220 segments. Volume 2 of the Food of the World series, narrated by
  Kym (kym-40.wav).
- Coverage: 23 Chatterbox languages (English plus 22).
- First book in the series with the new ASR + LLM-judge tier in the
  validate phase (ttsctl validator shipped 2026-05-08).
- First book in the series with first-person Kym in frontmatter and
  backmatter; body chapters are narrator-agnostic.
