## Chapter 6 — The Classic Plate

A Sunday dinner plate is full.

In the middle is fried chicken. Next to the chicken is a scoop of mashed potatoes with brown gravy.

On one side is a pile of collard greens, slow-cooked with smoked pork. The greens are dark and soft.

On the other side is macaroni and cheese, baked until the top is golden and crisp. Many cooks add a layer of cheese on top.

There is cornbread on the table. Cornbread is made from yellow cornmeal and baked in a hot iron skillet. It is heavy, a little sweet, and perfect with butter.

To drink, there is sweet tea. Sweet tea is black tea, brewed strong and mixed with sugar while it is hot. It is served cold over ice.

After dinner comes pie. Sweet potato pie is the most Southern pie. It is brown, smooth, and warm with cinnamon and nutmeg.

This is the classic plate.
