## Chapter 7 — The Table

A Southern dinner happens at the table, with everyone together.

The food is brought to the table in big bowls and on big plates. This is called "family-style." Each person passes the bowl to the next. Each person takes what they want.

The forks and knives are simple. There is no special order. You eat the chicken with a fork, or you pick it up with your hands. Both are fine.

Cornbread is broken with the hand, never cut with a knife.

Before the meal, someone often says a short prayer. They thank God for the food and for the cooks.

If you are a guest, you will be told to take more, and more, and more. You should say yes at least twice. To leave hungry is not allowed.

At the end of the meal, no one is in a hurry to stand up. The talking is half of dinner.
