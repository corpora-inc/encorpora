## Chapter 3 — The Meats and the Fish

The most important meat in soul food is pork.

A small farm could keep a pig. The pig ate scraps. In the fall, the family killed the pig, and they used every part. They smoked the ham. They cured the bacon. They cooked the feet in vinegar. They saved the fat for biscuits.

The next big meat is chicken. Chickens lived in the yard. They laid eggs in the morning, and they made Sunday dinner.

Fried chicken is the most famous Southern dish. The chicken is rolled in flour, salt, and pepper. Then it is dropped in hot oil. The skin gets crisp and golden.

Catfish lives in the rivers and the lakes. Southern cooks roll the catfish in cornmeal and fry it. They eat it with hush puppies, which are small balls of fried cornmeal. The name is funny but the food is serious.
