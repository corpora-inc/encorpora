# A Note for the Reader

This is a small book about a big food. The food is soul food, from the Southern United States.

Soul food is real food. People cook it at home. People eat it on Sundays. People eat it on a Tuesday too.

We will start with the name. Then we will look at the plants and the animals. Then we will look at the pots and the pans. By the end, you will know what is on the plate, and you will know where it came from.

My name is Kym and I'm going to tell you the story.
