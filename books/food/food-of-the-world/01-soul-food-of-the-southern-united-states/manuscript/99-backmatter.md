# About This Book

This is the first book in the Food of the World series. It is read aloud by me, your friend Kym. Every sentence is short and clear so you can read along in any language.

If you want to hear how a sentence sounds in another language, listen to the same book in that language. The words on the page will match what you hear. That is how you learn.

Now go find some good food, and a friend to share it with.
