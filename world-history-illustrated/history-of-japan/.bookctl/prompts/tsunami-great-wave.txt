A towering tsunami wave with churning foam crest approaches a rocky Japanese coastline at dawn, inspired by Hokusai's compositional style. The wave's face shows intricate linear details rendered in bold pen strokes, with layered ink wash creating depth and movement. Foreground rocks jut dramatically from darker waters rendered in deep indigo and black. The distant shore shows silhouetted buildings and trees in muted grays and greens. Sky above transitions from pale cream to soft blue-gray, suggesting stormy conditions. Fine pen lines define the wave's musculature and spray, while watercolor washes add atmospheric perspective and suggest the wave's tremendous power and speed. The composition emphasizes the wave's scale compared to human structures barely visible on shore, conveying the awesome force of nature described in the text. Traditional Japanese artistic sensibility combined with educational clarity for young readers.
