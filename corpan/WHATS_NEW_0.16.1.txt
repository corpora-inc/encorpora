On-device language tutors

Meet Tutomaton: a patient tutor for every language you're learning, running entirely on your device. Ask anything, practice freely, work through a sentence, or just chat. Type or speak — it can talk back — and it even draws on the phrases you've already been learning in Corpán. Your conversations stay private and work offline. A one-time model download gets you set up (it's large, so Wi-Fi is best).

A new home

Home is now one calm place for everything. A "For you" suggestion offers something worth doing next — tap to start, or ask for another. Tell it what you like, skip what you don't, and your recommendations adjust over time. Below that: what you've used recently, a short row to browse, your phrase packs, and your full library with one-tap updates. Settings is simpler too — a single pane, with Corpán Plus and your downloads in clear reach.

Voices that fit your language

Voice setup now leads with the right voice for your language and dialect — a Portugal voice for European Portuguese, a Taiwan voice for Traditional Chinese, a UK voice for British English — with a button to hear it instantly. A clear guide shows exactly how to switch on your device's best built-in voices, and voices you install now appear on their own.

A gentler start

Onboarding is shorter and steadier: choose your languages and topics, set up voices, tell us what you're here for, then take a quick guided tour of what's inside before you dive in.

Throughout

- A quick-settings panel on Phrase Flip to adjust speed, languages, levels, and active packs on the fly
- The app's interface speaks around 50 languages
- An in-app notice when a new version is available
- Corpán Plus: subscribe, manage, or restore in one place
- More reliable downloads for large content, including the tutor models
- Many stability and reliability fixes across iPhone and iPad

Pure learning. No ads, ever. Your data stays on your device.
