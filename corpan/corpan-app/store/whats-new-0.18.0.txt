You never forget a song.

That's the whole idea behind beatlounge — the new studio inside Corpan, and the most fun you'll ever have learning a language. Or fifty.

beatlounge is a real instrument. The ribbon, the turntable, the effects rack — professional, expressive, and yours to play any way you like. Not sure where to start? Tell it what you want in plain words and the loop reworks itself. You don't need a shred of music theory to make something that sounds incredible — and if you want to learn it, beatlounge teaches you, with a harmony engine fluent in modes, maqam, and ragas. Whether it's your first beat or your hundredth, it meets you where you are.

Then comes the part that still gives us chills: your samples are the language you're learning. Build a groove, pull in real phrases — tens of thousands of them, across 50+ languages — and scratch, chop, and play them on the deck. Sliced into words and fragments, that's more than ten million utterances at your fingertips. Every one of them on your device. No connection. No catch.

You won't feel like you're studying. You'll feel like you're making something — and the words stay with you, because you put them on the beat.

This release also runs smoother and steadier, with cleaner transitions between experiences and tougher handling under the hood.
