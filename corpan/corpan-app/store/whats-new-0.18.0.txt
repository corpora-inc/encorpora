beatlounge
A dark, premium beat studio. Build a loop on the sequencer, set a key and mode, and play melodies on a wide ribbon that always stays in tune. Tap plus or minus to grow or thin a groove, sample words from the phrase corpus straight into your tracks, and reshape the whole loop with a single word. It is made to be played with — and every sound can carry a phrase, so you learn in rhythm.
