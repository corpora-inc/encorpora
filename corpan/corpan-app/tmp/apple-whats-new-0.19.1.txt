Corpán is getting more alive from the very first tap.

This update adds a new first-run experience that lets you choose how you want to begin: read, study, make music, play language games, or let Corpán surprise you. Then the app brings your choice to life with a smoother animated launch, real content, and a faster path into the good stuff.

Reading now starts with less setup. New users can land directly in a narrated book preview, with language support ready in the background so you can start listening, reading, and exploring right away.

Phrase Flip is now a first-class part of the app, with its own artwork and a proper home alongside your installed experiences. It is still quick, simple phrase practice, but now it feels like it belongs.

Home is cleaner and more useful too. Pack updates are shown where you actually need them, with one-tap updating instead of hiding behind settings.

Corpán Plus is clearer and friendlier, with better free-trial messaging, better code redemption, and fewer confusing subscription moments.

We also fixed catalog loading, offline subscriber access, first-launch routing, duplicate pack cards, and several rough edges in the onboarding and paywall flow.

Corpán is still the same idea: languages through books, games, music, phrase practice, and curiosity. No ads. No login. Built to make learning feel like something you want to come back to.
