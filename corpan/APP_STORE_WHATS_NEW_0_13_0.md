What's new in Corpán 0.13.0

• Corpán Plus — one membership unlocks every book in every language, with no ads and nothing to track. The first part of every book stays free, forever.
• A warmer welcome — the new setup learns whether you're here to learn languages, enjoy content in your own language, or set things up for a kid, and tailors Corpán to you. It all stays on your device.
• Continue where you left off — your reading progress is remembered, on-device only.
• Faster, calmer reading and listening across the library.

Note for returning readers: per-book purchases have been retired in favor of one simple Corpán Plus membership. If you bought a book before, it stays yours.
