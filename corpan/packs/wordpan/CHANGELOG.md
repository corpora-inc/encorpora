## [0.1.0] - 2026-06-24

### Added
- First release. Data-only pack shipping `word_explanation` rows for the
  ~11,757 unique English corpus words, with English + Spanish explanation
  paragraphs (23,514 rows total, ~9.2 MB).
- Consumed by the built-in Phrase Flip experience: long-press an English word
  to read its meaning in Spanish (native-first, English fallback).
- Registered on the `preview` channel.
