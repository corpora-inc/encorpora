# Agent Notes for Third-Grade Homeschool Book

- Headers: rendered bold via Pandoc/LaTeX; do not wrap headers in `**`.
- Headers: avoid parentheses and colons; keep them short and punchy.
- Tone: plain, calm, optimistic. Avoid hype, pop-philosophy, and "real job" framing.
- Tone: avoid “get it over with” language; prefer savoring, returning, and steady joy.
- Joy is a core theme: emphasize low cost (time, energy, friction) and high return (capability, confidence, character).
- Define jargon on first use: Bonsai, Kaizen, Kanban, JIT, Chronos, Kairos. Keep definitions brief and useful.
- Distinguish compound interest (skills building over time) from compound movement (one activity trains multiple skills).
- Prefer meaty paragraphs over strings of short, disjointed paragraphs; use lists sparingly and only when they help.
- Blockquotes: never place two blockquote blocks back-to-back. Either merge into one blockquote (use a `>` blank line between entries) or insert a normal paragraph between them.
- Images: always on their own line with a blank line before and after; never place an image inside a list item or at the end of a paragraph line.
- Lists: always include a blank line before any list block (bulleted or numbered).
