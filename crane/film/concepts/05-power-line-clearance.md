# Power lines: the work zone at full radius

Skills: `powerlines.work-zone`, `powerlines.twenty-foot-check`, `powerlines.options`, `powerlines.table-a`, `powerlines.over-350kv`, `loadchart.max-radius`. Exam domain: Site (about 20%). Rules: 29 CFR 1926.1408(a)(1)–(2), Table A, 1926.1409. Numbers come from `crane/content/tools/powerline/clearance.json`.

## The idea

The check is about where the crane could go, not where the lift is planned. Unless the work zone is limited with marked boundaries the operator may not pass, it is "the area 360 degrees around the equipment, up to the equipment's maximum working radius" [§1926.1408(a)(1)(ii)]. Then: could "any part of the equipment, load line or load (including rigging and lifting accessories), if operated up to the equipment's maximum working radius in the work zone, ... get closer than 20 feet to a power line" [§1926.1408(a)(2)]? If yes, the employer must use Option (1) deenergize and ground, Option (2) 20 ft clearance, or Option (3) the Table A distance for the line's voltage.

For lines over 350 kV up to 1,000 kV, every "20 feet" becomes "50 feet" [§1926.1409(a)].

## Cases (three)

1. **Clear.** Max radius 90 ft, line 115 ft away: 25 ft gap at full radius. No option is required by this check.
2. **Inside 20 ft at full radius.** Line 100 ft away: the circle reaches within 10 ft. Options (1), (2) or (3) apply, even if today's lifts are on the other side, unless the zone is demarcated and the operator kept out of it.
3. **Table A, by voltage.** Option (3) uses the line's voltage: up to 50 kV, 10 ft; over 50 to 200 kV, 15 ft; over 200 to 350 kV, 20 ft, up to 45 ft for over 750 to 1,000 kV. The voltage must be known (from the utility) before Table A can be used.
