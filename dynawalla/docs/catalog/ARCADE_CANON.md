## Strongest ten

- SKY LEDGER (Missile Command) — the most specific juice spec in 234 designs: per-link hitstop, bloom, chroma and timescale with named caps and a hard snap-back, because the release is what makes spectacle feel earned. The sky IS the coordinate plane, the astrolabe dial makes the child PRODUCE the ordered pair rather than point at it, and there is no win state — the run ends and the observatory writes it down. A ten-year-old will replay a nine-link chain for an hour.
- THE LATTICE (Geometry Wars 2) — the pack that proves the whole feel kernel. A GPU mass-spring grid that genuinely tears and re-knits is the single most convincing 'this is not a school app' signal available, and prime factorisation as a growing tile bar means a child sees 2·2·3 become 12 hundreds of times a session with sound and colour attached. Honest caveat: the passive layer is absorption, not reasoning; the target-product resonance is where the thinking is and it must ship on.
- THE SUNDERED FIELD (Qix) — the best mathematical loop in the document. Estimate-then-verify: declare the fraction you believe a region encloses before you close it, get double if you are right, get ground truth either way. That is calibrated estimation with immediate feedback, hundreds of times an hour, wrapped in molten glass and a fresco you are revealing. Nothing else here trains a skill this valuable this often.
- THE GRAPPLE FOUNDRY (Pro Wrestling) — four seconds, two pedals worth 3 and 5, a target of 26, three referee slaps and a crowd. It is a coin problem with a heart rate. Overshoot is fatal so mashing loses instantly, and the escape explosion is the biggest reward in the family. This is the pack to show a sceptic: it is unambiguously a great wrestling game and unambiguously mental arithmetic, at the same instant.
- METRONOME SIEGE (Space Invaders Extreme) — fractions ARE beat subdivision; that is not a metaphor, it is the definition, and no children's product has ever built on it. Thirds against quarters resolving as twelfths is a common-denominator problem the child solves with their hands and hears succeed. It also forces the SDK's audio clock to be right, which every rhythm pack downstream needs.
- THE COIL OF NINETY-SIX (Centipede) — cutting at segment k produces a partition of N, and the game sonifies both halves as note-runs so the arithmetic is heard as well as carved into the wall. Structurally exact, impossible to fake, and the terrain punishes thoughtless play by choking your own board rather than buzzing at you. The IK coil plus true analog input is also the strongest argument for the high-rate pointer path.
- VITRAIL (Puzzle Bobble) — the highest payoff-per-input in the catalogue: one bead, forty falling, a 1.6-second glissando and moving caustics across a stone floor. And its difficulty dial is the best-designed in the document — three preview tiers at 1x / 1.5x / 2.5x score, so the child chooses to remove their own training wheels and is paid for it. That is the healthiest progression structure here.
- THE WELL OF SIXTEEN LANES (Tempest) — the only interactive, consequential, self-verifying encounter with modular arithmetic I have seen anywhere. Two dials, a live preview of which lanes 'congruent to 2 mod 3' means, and a rim detent whose pitch encodes the residue class so the modulus is audible while you skate. Prime wells feel qualitatively different and the child notices without being told.
- LINEAGE ENGINE (Infinity Gene) — the medium visibly improves as the child improves, from ink on parchment to full HDR, mid-fight, with no cut. It is the retention engine for the entire catalogue and the hardest engineering in it, which is exactly why it belongs in wave one as a forcing function for the render pipeline. Be honest internally: its maths is the thinnest per minute in the document and it is here on spectacle and structure, not pedagogy.
- THE TRUE DRAW (Wild Gunman) — the cheapest pack on the list and one of the two most rigorous. A GO/NO-GO inhibition task with a 50% mashing ceiling designed to read as failure, a statement bank that spans the whole syllabus by data alone, and the best use of restraint in the catalogue: correct holds are rewarded with a bow and a slate that corrects itself, wrong draws are punished by being ignored. Ships in weeks and proves the feel kernel.

## Missed classics (the 2026 gap)

- IKARUGA (Treasure, 2001) — the single most glaring omission. Polarity absorb/repel IS signed arithmetic and parity, and the chain system is 'clear in groups of three of one sign'. Design: light and dark bolts; absorbing a same-sign shot charges you, and your discharge damage equals the signed SUM of what you absorbed. Chains pay for three-of-a-kind. This is a better integer pack than anything currently in the catalogue.
- XEVIOUS (Namco, 1982) — the whole air/ground dual-plane shooter is missing. Two firing planes = two number lines. Air targets take direct fire; ground targets need a bombsight lead (rate x time). Buried Sol citadels are hidden factors revealed only by bombing the right coordinate.
- GRADIUS (Konami, 1985) — the power meter is the best resource-allocation ladder ever built and there is nothing like it here. Design: the meter's cells are priced as a partition of a budget; spending early is cheap and weak, hoarding to the last cell is a multiplication. Children discover greedy vs optimal partitioning.
- SPACE HARRIER / AFTER BURNER (Sega, 1985/87) — the entire into-the-screen rail lane is absent from a 234-pack catalogue. That is a structural hole. Design: targets addressed in three-space; lead the shot in depth as well as in plane, so coordinates become (x, y, t).
- VAMPIRE SURVIVORS (2022) — cited once in passing and never built. The auto-attacking horde-survivor is the dominant arcade form a ten-year-old actually plays in 2026, and its level-up draft is a pure comparison-of-expected-value problem every ninety seconds. Its absence is the biggest 2026-relevance gap in the document.
- FRUIT NINJA (2010) — the most-played touchscreen arcade form ever made, on the exact device this product ships on, and it is not here. Slicing a whole into parts is fractions made literal; the slice angle is geometry; combos are sets. Design: slice a numbered fruit along a line that partitions it into two named fractions.
- ANGRY BIRDS / trajectory launcher (2009) — the canonical touch physics game. Trebuchet Troupe is close but is Circus Atari. Design: launch angle and power as an ordered pair; structures are labelled with the load they bear so the child computes where the tower fails.
- TEMPLE RUN / behind-the-back endless runner (2011) — Crossy Road is covered from above; the third-person 3D runner is the form children actually play and is missing. Design: three lanes as three residue classes; swipe to the lane congruent to the called value.
- GUITAR HERO / fretboard rhythm (2005) — the best-selling rhythm form ever and there is no fretboard pack. Design: frets are set members, a chord is a subset, strum is the commit. Teaches sets and simultaneity in a way the lane-tap packs cannot.
- PAC-MAN CHAMPIONSHIP EDITION DX (2010) — the shooters family explicitly cites modern descendants, but the maze family stops at 1986. CE DX's ghost trains and dynamic maze regeneration are the modern reinvention and should be a pack: the train length IS the running total.
- BANK PANIC (Sanritsu, 1984) — twelve doors on a rotary, customer versus bandit, and you can be shot from a door you are not watching. Perfect modular-position discrimination and a genuinely better fit for arcs of twelve than any current gallery pack.
- SMASH TV (Williams, 1990) — twin-stick arena with a DOOR CHOICE between rooms. That choice is expected value with visible odds and is exactly the probability seam the catalogue currently only has once (Caravan Gambit).
- WRECKING CREW (Nintendo, 1985) — demolition ORDER matters, because a wall you knock early traps you later. Genuine sequencing and dependency-graph reasoning, and nothing in the platformer family covers it.
- SUPER MONKEY BALL (Amusement Vision, 2001) — tilt-the-world, gyro-native on a tablet, and absent. Design: the tilt vector is a signed pair the child reads off a gauge; goals are gated on holding a stated slope.
- SUPER MARIO BROS. (Nintendo, 1985) — there is no side-scrolling run-and-jump platformer with momentum in a 22-pack platformer family. That is indefensible. Design: the world is a number line; jump distance equals the value of the expression you are carrying, and pipes are divisors.
- AGAR.IO / SNAKE.IO growth-arena (2015) — massively played by this exact age band. Design: your mass is a number; you may only absorb strictly smaller numbers, and splitting divides your mass into named parts. Comparison and division at social scale, offline against automata.
- AIR HOCKEY — Windjammers covers disc angle but not the two-player momentum duel with a mallet, which is the physical form every child knows. Design: puck value accumulates per wall bounce, so a long rally is worth more and both players know it.
- TETRIS EFFECT / TETRIS 99 (2018/19) — the modern reinventions are cited nowhere. At minimum, Ashlar should have a partner pack where the well is scored to an adaptive soundtrack and clears are banked against a stated target rather than counted.
