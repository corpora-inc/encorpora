Homeschool Offline is a 100% offline calendar app designed specifically for homeschooling families. Track your homeschool days, add notes, attach photos, and keep everything organized - all without an internet connection.

✨ FEATURES

📅 Calendar View
• Mark days as homeschool days
• View monthly calendar at a glance
• Navigate easily between dates
• Track total homeschool days

📝 Daily Notes
• Add notes for each day
• Auto-saves as you type
• Rich text support
• Perfect for lesson plans and observations

📸 Photo Attachments
• Attach multiple photos per day
• Capture student work and activities
• Photos stored locally on your device
• Easy photo gallery view

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Multiple Students
• Support for multiple children
• Switch between students easily
• Separate tracking for each student
• Perfect for families with multiple grades

💾 Backup & Restore
• Export all data to ZIP file
• Import from backup
• Transfer between devices
• You own your data
