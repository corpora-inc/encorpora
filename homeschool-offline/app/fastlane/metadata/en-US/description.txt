Homeschool Offline is a simple, privacy-focused calendar and tracker designed specifically for homeschooling families.

KEY FEATURES:
• 100% Offline - All data stays on your device
• No cloud, no accounts, no tracking
• Multi-student support - track multiple children in one app
• Daily calendar with notes and photos
• Mark homeschool days to keep accurate records
• Attach photos, videos, and files to any day
• Export your records for compliance and portfolios

PERFECT FOR:
• Homeschool parents who need to track attendance
• Families who value data privacy
• Anyone who wants a simple, distraction-free homeschool log

YOUR DATA IS YOURS:
Unlike other apps, Homeschool Offline never uploads your data to the cloud. Everything stays on your device, giving you complete privacy and control. No accounts, no subscriptions, no tracking.

SIMPLE & FOCUSED:
We built this app to do one thing well: help you track your homeschool days without getting in your way. No complicated features, no social networks, just a simple daily log for your homeschool journey.
